## Code-level conclusions

- Volume Z is used first as a setup hard gate and then receives full participation base credit. A candidate that reaches multifactor therefore already owns 6/6 volume participation points.
- Trend evidence is expressed through 1h regime eligibility, setup geometry, 4h macro alignment, and an unconditional 30/30 `trend_structure` group after macro alignment. This is the clearest triple-or-more expression of related evidence.
- RSI is a hard admissibility gate inside `assess_factors`; it is not assigned a separate numeric score. Nevertheless, passing RSI is required before the momentum group can contribute its base 8 points.
- ROC enters the momentum group. It also controls whether rising/falling open interest is interpreted as confirming in the derivatives group, so enabling derivatives would make ROC participate in two groups.
- CVD and candle Volume Z are different measurements, but both represent participation/flow. Treating them as independent proof deserves a future controlled experiment; this audit does not remove either.

These labels identify correlated or repeated expressions, not proof that a feature is invalid. Any deletion or reweighting requires a separately pre-registered v0.3.2 experiment.
