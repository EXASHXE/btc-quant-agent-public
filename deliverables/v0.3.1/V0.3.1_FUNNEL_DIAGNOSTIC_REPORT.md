## 7. TRANSITION Attribution

Reasons can co-occur. Among 98,368 TRANSITION decisions, structure mismatch appears 92,832 times (94.37%), multiple failures 57,976 (58.94%), slope mismatch 49,484 (50.30%), EMA-order mismatch 43,892 (44.62%), and ADX too low 30,184 (30.68%). The classifier demands simultaneous EMA order, both slope signs, ADX ≥20, and exact `HH_HL`/`LH_LL`; failing this conjunction while ADX remains above the range cutoff produces TRANSITION.

2023 and 2025 are similar: TRANSITION is 20,516/35,040 (58.55%) and 20,540/35,040 (58.62%). Structure mismatch dominates both (19,480 and 19,208 occurrences).

## 11. Evidence Redundancy

The separate redundancy matrix identifies Volume Z at setup plus participation (`POTENTIAL_DOUBLE_COUNT`); trend/structure through regime, setup, 4h macro, and fixed 30/30 score (`POTENTIAL_TRIPLE_COUNT`); RSI as a hard gate that also unlocks momentum; and ROC in momentum plus future OI interpretation. ATR participates in regime exclusion, setup tolerances, target/stop/RR, but those roles are not identical.
