## Stage survival

2023 and 2025 were not devoid of trend regimes or patterns:

- 2023 had 5,088 trend decisions, 148 pattern candidates, 56 candidates that cleared multifactor, and zero that cleared the RR/risk plan.
- 2025 had 6,004 trend decisions, 203 pattern candidates, 60 candidates that cleared multifactor, and zero that cleared the RR/risk plan.
- Across every year, Trend Pullback produced 404 candidates; 139 cleared multifactor and **0** cleared the RR/risk plan.
- Breakout produced 546 candidates; 185 cleared multifactor and only 14 cleared risk. Those 14 occurred in 2021 (6), 2022 (6), and 2024 (2 raw / 1 filled).

For Trend Pullback, the largest early geometric reductions are EMA25-zone touch (72.14% LONG, 74.00% SHORT) and previous-extreme break (65.65% LONG, 66.67% SHORT). Even after those filters, the terminal cause is decisive: risk-plan survival is 0/139.

For Breakout, the initial breakout-close condition removes 79.61% LONG and 79.79% SHORT of trend-eligible decisions. Among the 185 candidates that pass positive groups, RR/risk removes 171 (92.43%).

## Conclusion for 2023 and 2025

The primary cause is **not absence of trend regimes** and not target availability. Both years produce many candidates. The direct cause is that none of their post-factor candidates satisfy the frozen net-RR/risk plan. High TRANSITION share reduces the opportunity pool—58.55% in 2023 and 58.62% in 2025—but does not by itself explain zero trades because 5,088 and 6,004 trend decisions remain. Factor rejection is a secondary selector, especially in 2025 (143/203 candidates), while RR is the final zero-producing gate.

This is a mechanism diagnosis, not permission to relax RR or regime rules.
