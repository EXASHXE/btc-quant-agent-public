## H29 interpretation

The sample gate passed comfortably. Both primary signed medians were positive,
and weekly-bootstrap p05 was +0.10257 ATR at 4h and -0.01459 ATR at 8h,
above the frozen -0.05 floor. The study nevertheless failed support because the
4h center-first rate was below 0.50 and Late 8h stability was -0.05277 ATR,
slightly below the required floor. It also avoided falsification: both signed
medians were not non-positive and both center-first rates were not ≤0.50.

## H30 incremental test

Matching was exact on every frozen stratum, K never exceeded five, all pairs
were separated by at least 24h, and no outcome field entered matching. The
positive return deltas did not translate into better target-before-adverse
ordering: center-first deltas were negative at both primary horizons. H30 is
therefore falsified under its explicit rule. Its 8h signed-return bootstrap p05
was also weak at -0.12676 ATR. Equal-weight day sensitivity changed the 8h
median delta from +0.00313 to -0.00742 ATR.

## H31 and serial dependence

Within 8h, the median future range was 2.94562 ATR, median center crossings were
four, and 44.99% reached both frozen center ±0.5 ATR. These describe two-sided
motion only. Episode-member diagnostics were weaker than onset estimates for
center-first and center-hit rates, confirming why repeated extremes cannot be
treated as independent observations.
