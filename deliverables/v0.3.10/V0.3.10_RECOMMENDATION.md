# v0.3.10 Recommendation

## Decision

```text
INCONCLUSIVE_RANGE_MECHANISM
```

H29 was neither supported nor falsified. Its 4h and 8h signed-return medians
were positive and its clustered-bootstrap lower bounds passed, but the 4h
center-first rate was only 44.50% and the Late 8h signed median was
`-0.05277 ATR`, just below the frozen `-0.05 ATR` stability floor.

H30 was falsified. The RANGE candidates had positive matched signed-return
deltas, but their center-first deltas versus exact-signal non-RANGE controls
were negative at both 4h and 8h. The existing frozen RANGE classifier therefore
did not demonstrate the preregistered incremental ordering benefit.

H31 found two-sided motion in 44.99% of complete 8h RANGE episodes, but this is
only a motion diagnostic. It cannot validate a grid, establish PnL, or upgrade
H29/H30.

## Authorized scope

Do not build or deploy a bounded-grid strategy from this result. Any continued
work requires a separately preregistered study; v0.3.10 does not authorize
parameter scans, Martingale, averaging down, paper/testnet/live execution, or a
candidate freeze.
