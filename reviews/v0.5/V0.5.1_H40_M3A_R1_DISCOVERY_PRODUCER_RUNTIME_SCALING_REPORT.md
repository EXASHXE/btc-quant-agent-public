## 3. Algorithmic Optimization Architecture

### 3.1 RUNTIME-01: Exact Causal Percentile Index (`_CausalPercentileIndex`)
- **Bottleneck Eliminated**: Replaced $O(N)$ expanding-window slice and rescan with an exact causal order-statistic index with prefix counts over coordinate-compressed values.
- **Causality & Tie Semantics Preserved**: Queries strictly enforce $close\_time < decision\_t$. Exact half-tie formula $(count(v < x) + 0.5 \cdot \mathbf{1}_{equal}) / N$ is mathematically preserved without approximation or floating-point bucketing.
- **Support**: Supports arbitrary query order and partition boundaries.

### 3.2 RUNTIME-02: Geometry Index Precomputation (`_GeometryIndex`)
- **Bottleneck Eliminated**: Replaced dynamic row-by-row scanning of owner records across 4 fallback levels with precomputed L0~L3 dictionary cells.
- **Invariants Preserved**: For each cell:
  - Exact member multiset cardinality;
  - $\ge 30$ sample floor;
  - Exact median MFE and MAE;
  - Identical fallback sequence: L0 (`owner+scope+horizon+side`) $\to$ L1 (`owner+scope+horizon`) $\to$ L2 (`owner+scope`) $\to$ L3 (`owner`).
- Lookup complexity reduced to $O(1)$ dictionary lookup.

### 3.3 Sibling Raw Decision Reuse
- Immutable raw source decisions are computed once per distinct `(source_evidence_hash, direction + primary_horizon, partition, timestamp, product, horizon)` and safely reused across sibling configurations before applying configuration-specific calibration and action logic.
- Sibling slots cannot share raw decision paths across different primary horizons (4h, 8h, 12h) or direction contract variants.

### 3.4 Scoped Reconstruction Lifecycle & Leak Prevention
- Wrapped `produce()` and `verify_discovery_manifest()` in `@_reconstruction_session` context managers.
- Temporary prefix indices and prefit states are deterministically bounded and cleared upon session exit, guaranteeing zero process-global state contamination.

### 3.5 Backward Compatibility & Test Mocking Support
- Added fallback lookup to `_PREFIT_CACHE` supporting both new `dir_contract:horizon` keys and legacy `candidate_id` keys, ensuring complete compatibility with existing verifier test harnesses without sacrificing performance.
