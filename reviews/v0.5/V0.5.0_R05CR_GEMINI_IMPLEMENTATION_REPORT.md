This report records the completion of the bounded R05CR acceptance repairs discovered after R05C independent review on repository `EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent` branch `v0.5-refactor`:

1. **R05C-F1 (MEDIUM)**: A qualification could omit the run-bound funding evidence ID from `required_evidence_ids`, permitting a P5 decision to omit the explicit immutable funding root from its evidence chain.
   - **Resolution**: Enforced that `candidate_run.identity.funding_evidence_id` must be present in `required_evidence_ids` at the evaluator boundary (`evaluate_formal_economic_qualification`), the object invariant boundary (`EconomicQualificationResult.__post_init__`), and the independent cold persisted validator (`validate_persisted_qualification_semantics`). Any omission fails closed with `ValueError("qualification required evidence omits bound funding evidence")`.
2. **R05C-F2 (LOW)**: `FundingIntervalIdentity.coverage_start_ms` and `coverage_end_ms` were not required to equal the comparison `data_interval` even though the evidence document itself was checked against that interval.
   - **Resolution**: Required exact equality between `funding_interval` coverage bounds and `data_interval` bounds during `ComparisonContract.__post_init__()` (`ValueError("funding_interval coverage must equal data_interval")`), and verified that `expected_identity` coverage bounds equal `expected_start_ms/end_ms` at the entry of `load_and_validate_funding_evidence()` (`ValueError("funding interval identity coverage differs from the expected interval")`).

The `R05CR_ESCALATION_REQUIRED_REGISTRY_AUTHORITY_CHANGE` guard was **not** triggered: registry production code was untouched, because existing registry logic already enforces that all `attestation.required_evidence_ids` must be registered in submitted `evidence_references`.
