## Independent conclusion

AG-02 is closed for the supported formal candidate producer rules.

The repaired authority is no longer caller-selected. Both bundle construction and cold validation reconstruct the required material-input window from:

1. the full frozen candle dataset;
2. the signal decision timestamp;
3. the protocol-bound immutable `SignalProducerContract`;
4. deterministic selector semantics.

Caller `input_references`, `observation_open_times_ms`, and `material_input_open_times_ms` are assertions only. They must exactly equal the reconstructed references.

The cold verifier independently invokes the selector from the full dataset before replay. Therefore a candidate cannot recover authority by recomputing `input_set_sha256`, preimage hashes, or `bundle_sha256`.

## Selector semantics verified

- `RETURN_SIGN`: exactly latest 1 eligible candle.
- `MOMENTUM_THRESHOLD`: exactly latest `lookback_bars` eligible contiguous candles; `min_lookback_bars` alone fails closed as ambiguous.
- `PRICE_BREAKOUT`: exactly latest 1 eligible candle.
- synthetic `FIXED_DIRECTION`: exactly latest 1 eligible candle; synthetic-only boundary remains separate.
- `RANDOM_MATCHED_OPPORTUNITY`: not redesigned.

Eligibility is closed + explicit availability + availability by decision time + close by decision time. The selected N>1 window must be contiguous and single-symbol/single-interval.

Formal runtime candle validation already enforces the full formal dataset as a single-product, single-interval, chronological contiguous sequence. Thus selector checks are defense in depth rather than a new multi-product authority.

## Astra reproduction review

The permanent R05B suite reproduces the economic consequence and the cold-rehash attack:

```text
latest [2,3] -> LONG -> accepted
[0,3]        -> SHORT under the old producer formula -> rejected
[0,1,3]      -> rejected
```

The full-rehash test replaces persisted references with genuine rows, recomputes `input_set_sha256` and `bundle_sha256`, and calls the cold validator directly. It still fails because the persisted references differ from the independently reconstructed window.

Additional regressions cover stale-but-contiguous windows, extra rows, substitutions, duplicates/order, future/unavailable rows, cadence gaps, mixed symbol/interval windows, ambiguous lookback parameters, exact-one rules, auto-construction, and forged generation-contract selector fields.
