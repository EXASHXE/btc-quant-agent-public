## 6. Regime system and model-class choice

Propose at most three orthogonal completed-bar labels: (i) volatility `LOW/NORMAL/HIGH` from realized range or ATR; (ii) trend strength `WEAK/STRONG` from absolute completed-bar slope; (iii) participation `NORMAL/ELEVATED` from canonical kline volume. Each label's formula, missing-data behavior, and fixed TRAIN-only quantiles must be frozen before D0. Do not cross every label with every candidate. Derivatives crowded/neutral is **not** an active V1 regime until a separate paired PIT source authority exists. No regime boundary is fitted to Discovery returns or H41 near-positive candidates.

Three forms were compared:

| Form | Strength | Main burden | V1 decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Deterministic conditioned rules | Auditability, direct PIT proof, small formal K, playbook-readable | Threshold and regime overfit still require TRAIN-only freeze | **Primary** |
| Small regularized model | Can combine weak signals | Supervised label reuse, feature selection and tuning multiply implicit tests; forward sample and validation burden rise | Defer to separate campaign |
| Deterministic opportunities plus compact scoring model | Separates event discovery from ranking | Two-layer selection and calibration create more authority surfaces and post-selection risk | Defer until deterministic testability is established |

## 7. Endpoint, friction, and playbook interpretation

Propose one **delayed, event-conditioned primary endpoint** to avoid treating same-boundary kline Open as a realizable fill. A decision at UTC boundary `t` uses only bars completed through `[t-1h,t)`. After a verified forward local receipt of that completed bar, the reference entry is native economic `Open(t+1h)`; exit is native `Open(t+1h+h)` for the frozen h. Open and preceding Close are never equated. The primary directional quantity is `side * (P_exit/P_entry - 1) - C_primary`, equal-entry-notional and before sizing/leverage. This is still an economic reference, not proof of executable fill.

`C_primary` is one fixed, prospectively justified round-trip cost budget: documented entry/exit taker fees plus a conservative spread/slippage allowance and a fixed funding reserve for any settlement crossing. Its numeric components are **unresolved and must be independently evidenced and frozen before Discovery**; no cost-model switch after outcomes. Stress diagnostics use a separately frozen wider slippage/adverse-funding budget and never replace the primary endpoint. Actual funding cashflows may enter a later primary endpoint only after a separate complete PIT funding source authority; the generic funding parser does not suffice. A playbook must later pass forward paper-fill/latency, spread, funding, capacity, and operational checks without changing the scientific lock.

## 8. Inference and multiplicity

All formal tests concern one-sided after-cost mean event expectancy >0. Preserve candidate and asset dependence with a shared UTC clock and paired BTC/ETH source bundle; use non-circular moving blocks, studentization, and a joint maximum. Unsupported coordinates have no formal p/LCB claim and cannot be promoted by descriptive means. Predeclare the block length, replicate count, RNG seed, finite-sample centering, zero-draw handling, and FWER level before D0; H41's validated `L=120h`, `B=10000` are a conservative **proposed starting method**, not successor authority automatically. Synthetic null/coverage tests and TRAIN-only dependence analysis must validate the final choice.

## 10. Support and power plan

Provisional **outcome-free** formal support floors for 24-month Discovery are `N≥200` event decisions, `D≥120` occupied UTC days, and events in `≥80` distinct 120h blocks. TRAIN-only projected support should exceed these with margin (`N≥300`, `D≥150`) after deterministic overlap/cooldown rules are fixed. These are proposed gates, not yet authority. Event overlap is retained in shared-clock resampling and reported via nonoverlapping-packing diagnostics; a deterministic earliest-trigger cooldown, if used, must be fixed from TRAIN-only event clocks before D0. Missing or partially finalized source buckets yield no event, never filled values.

Illustrative **synthetic sensitivity**, not a market estimate: 24 months gives roughly 146 non-overlapping 120h blocks. With an assumed block-level standard deviation of 60/100 bp and an illustrative joint critical value 2.5, the `2.5+0.84` 80%-power approximation requires about **17/28 bp net** respectively. At 12 months (~73 blocks), the same toy calculation is about **23/39 bp**. Real dependence and sparse conditioned events can be worse. Before D0, run a preregistered synthetic joint-null and correlated-alternative grid over K≤12, all frozen support patterns, 4h/8h horizons, and realistic friction. Require one-sided FWER≤5% in the null and ≥80% power for a prospectively justified economically meaningful after-cost effect (proposed 30bp). If that fails, extend the forward Discovery window or shrink the roster **before** D0; otherwise stop. Neither H41 calibration means nor sealed successor outcomes may set this target.

## 11. Negative controls and explicit failure modes

| Check | Interpretation / action |
| --- | --- |
| Inject impossible future bars or reference Opens into completed-bar API; perturb archive timestamps | Any feature/event change or accepted context with changed source bytes is PIT/source failure: stop before outcomes. |

| Compare historical archive finalization to forward receive timestamps and price/volume projection | Material source mismatch or missing/local-receipt ambiguity blocks D0; do not backfill, interpolate or relabel. |
| Event-mask aliasing, side agreement, occupied days/blocks, calendar span | Collapse pseudo-diversity or low-support modifiers before freeze; after D0, no roster rescue. |

Shared-clock dependence, serially overlapping outcomes and volatility clustering are handled by the prospectively validated block bootstrap, not by pretending N events are independent. Source survivor bias is checked by exact source membership and a missing-hour inventory; unqualified datasets cannot be silently dropped. Regime thresholds use only TRAIN features. H41 exploratory results may motivate a *question* about state conditioning but cannot choose the successor's asset, threshold, candidate winner or cost. Forward source revisions change content hashes and invalidate downstream authority. Historical-to-forward semantic parity is a hard pre-execution gate.
