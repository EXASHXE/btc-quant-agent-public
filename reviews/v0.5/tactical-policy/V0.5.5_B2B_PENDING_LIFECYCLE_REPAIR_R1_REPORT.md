## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the resolution of blocker `S08_PENDING_RESOLVE_BLOCKED_BY_CONFLICT` identified during independent validation of candidate commit `a32ab775ac2cc168c9f45a38a7b11fd5b9c85f37`.

Prior to this repair, `MarketWatchStateStore.save_grid_shadow_evaluation` unconditionally raised `TacticalGridShadowEvaluationConflictError` when encountering an existing natural key `(feature_evidence_id, evaluation_profile_version)` with a differing `evaluation_id`. As a result, when provisional evaluations (`PENDING_HORIZON` or `PENDING_DATA_GAP`) were updated as new forward market data arrived, the resulting new content-hash `evaluation_id` was rejected as a conflict, locking pending evaluations in SQLite permanently.

The repair implements the frozen persistence lifecycle:
1. **Provisional Replacement:** Provisional evaluations (`PENDING_HORIZON`, `PENDING_DATA_GAP`) may be atomically replaced by a newer provisional artifact or advanced to `RESOLVED`.
2. **Terminal Immutability:** Authoritative terminal evaluations (`RESOLVED`, `NOT_ACTIVE_PAUSE`) remain strictly immutable; any divergent replay under the same natural key fails closed.
3. **Forbidden Transitions:** Transition from provisional to `NOT_ACTIVE_PAUSE` is strictly forbidden (fails closed).
4. **Existing-Row Identity Verification:** Prior to mutation, existing SQLite rows are verified against their embedded canonical JSON; tampered data fails closed with `TacticalGridShadowEvaluationIdentityError`.
5. **Atomic Conditional Update:** Replacements use a single atomic `UPDATE ... WHERE feature_evidence_id = ? AND evaluation_profile_version = ? AND evaluation_id = existing_row_id` updating all 18 first-class columns and the canonical JSON, guaranteeing zero phantom or concurrent corruptions.

All 15 required adversarial tests (`R1-A01` through `R1-A15`) and the full regression test suite (2,009 passed) pass with zero errors.
