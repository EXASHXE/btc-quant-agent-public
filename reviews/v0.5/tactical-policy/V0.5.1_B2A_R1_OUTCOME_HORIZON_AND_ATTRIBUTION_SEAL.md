## 1. Executive Summary & Sealed Architectural Invariants

B2A-R1 repairs and seals the outcome horizon, attribution, funding ambiguity, and coverage authority of Stage B2A.

Key architectural repairs:
1. **Single Horizon Authority:**
   - Eliminated dual horizon authority (where `evaluation_horizon_ms` was playbook-derived but `evaluation_horizon_bars` defaulted to 16).
   - Both `evaluation_horizon_bars` and `evaluation_horizon_ms` are derived strictly from `get_playbook_evaluation_profile(playbook)`:
     - `TREND_PULLBACK`: 48 bars, 43,200,000 ms (12h)
     - `BREAKOUT_RETEST`: 48 bars, 43,200,000 ms (12h)
     - `FAILED_BREAKOUT`: 32 bars, 28,800,000 ms (8h)
     - `FAILED_BREAKDOWN`: 32 bars, 28,800,000 ms (8h)
     - `VOLATILITY_EXPANSION`: 32 bars, 28,800,000 ms (8h)
   - Strict invariant enforced: `evaluation_horizon_ms == evaluation_horizon_bars * 15 * 60 * 1000`. Disagreements fail closed with `TacticalShadowProfileConflictError`.
   - `evaluation_horizon_bars` is added to `TacticalShadowEvaluationV2` and persisted into SQLite `tactical_shadow_evaluations_v2`.

2. **Funding Fill-Boundary Authority:**
   - Replaced fragile `abs(funding_time - fill_time) < 60s` heuristic with authoritative fill uncertainty interval:
     - `fill_interval_start_ms`, `fill_interval_end_ms`, `fill_time_resolution` (`ONE_MINUTE_TOUCH_INTERVAL` or `FIFTEEN_MINUTE_FALLBACK_INTERVAL`).
   - Funding settlements occurring during the touch interval (`fill_interval_start_ms <= f_time <= fill_interval_end_ms`) are strictly classified as `AMBIGUOUS_FILL_BOUNDARY` with `funding_pnl_r = None`.
   - Settlements before fill interval are excluded; settlements strictly after fill interval and on or before `exit_time_ms` are settled.

3. **Attribution Cross-Check & Scale-Safe Floats:**
   - Authoritative fail-closed cross-check before B2A evaluation materialization:
     - Symbol, signal time, feature evidence ID, playbook, direction, signal identity, setup key, snapshot hash, policy version, config hash.
     - Directional risk plan bounds (`entry_zone_low`, `entry_zone_high`, `stop_loss`, `tp1`, `tp2`, `direction`) validated with deterministic scale-safe floating comparison: `math.isclose(a, b, rel_tol=1e-12, abs_tol=1e-12)`.
     - Discrepancies raise `TacticalShadowAttributionConflictError`.
   - B1 authority verification invoked on referenced `TacticalFeatureEvidenceV2` (`validate_tactical_feature_evidence`, `verify_tactical_evidence_identity`).

4. **Database Persistence Validation & Migrations:**
   - Altered `market_watch_shadow_records` table: added `fill_interval_start_ms`, `fill_interval_end_ms`, `fill_time_resolution`.
   - Altered `tactical_shadow_evaluations_v2` table: added `evaluation_horizon_bars`.
   - `save_shadow_evaluation`: validates referenced `tactical_feature_evidence_v2` row exists and is valid; validates source shadow record in `market_watch_shadow_records` exists, has `resolved = 1`, matches `feature_evidence_id`, and matches `evaluation_profile_version`.

5. **Diagnostic Checkpoints & Gap Handling:**
   - 4 standard descriptive checkpoints: 4h, 8h, 12h, 24h.
   - Continuous 15m coverage verified via `validate_time_coverage`. Intermediate data gaps transition checkpoint to `INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE` with `mark_price = None`, `mark_to_market_gross_r = None`, `mfe_r = None`, `mae_r = None` without fabricating prices.
   - Post-terminal candles are strictly excluded (`close_time_ms <= exit_time_ms`). Trades exiting prior to checkpoint record `TERMINATED_BEFORE_CHECKPOINT` with barrier status `TERMINATED_TP1` / `TERMINATED_STOP`.

6. **Pre-B2A Row Isolation:**
   - Shadow records lacking `DIRECTIONAL_OUTCOME_PROFILE_V1` are classified as `PRE_B2A_HORIZON` and yield no B2A evaluation in `tactical_shadow_evaluations_v2`.
