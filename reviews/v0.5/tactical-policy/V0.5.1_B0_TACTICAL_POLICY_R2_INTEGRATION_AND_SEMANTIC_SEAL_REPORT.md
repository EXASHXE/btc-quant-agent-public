### 3.2 True Elapsed Multi-Horizon Returns (`ELAPSED_TIME_RETURNS_V1`)
- Computed from closed 15m bars across 1h, 4h, and 12h horizons.
- Exact horizon anchor matching with `ReturnAvailability` statuses: `AVAILABLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`, `MISSING_HORIZON_ANCHOR`.
- No nearest-neighbor fallback.
- Forming candles (`closed=False`) strictly isolated.
- Benchmark 1h volatility shock evaluated on true 1h return (`return_1h`), strictly failing closed when unavailable with audit reasons `BTC_RETURN_1H_UNAVAILABLE` / `ETH_RETURN_1H_UNAVAILABLE` (no legacy ROC fallback).

### 3.3 Observational Slow EMA
- `ema_slow = 200` configurable under `MarketWatchThresholdsConfig`.
- Warmup requirement of `ema_slow + 50` confirmed closed bars enforced.
- Status set to `EMA_SLOW_INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY` when insufficient history is present.
- Strictly observational: does not participate in directional logic or playbook gating.
- Included in config hash and required fetch bar calculations.

### 3.4 Active Playbook Registry & Static Precedence (`STATIC_PRECEDENCE_V1`)
- Active: `TREND_PULLBACK`, `BREAKOUT_RETEST`, `FAILED_BREAKOUT`, `FAILED_BREAKDOWN`, `VOLATILITY_EXPANSION`.
- Reserved Inactive: `RANGE_MEAN_REVERSION` (never evaluated or selected).
- `evaluate_all_playbook_candidates` evaluates all active playbooks into `PlaybookCandidate` dataclasses.
- `select_playbook_candidate` selects strictly in precedence order: `TREND_PULLBACK > BREAKOUT_RETEST > FAILED_BREAKOUT > FAILED_BREAKDOWN > VOLATILITY_EXPANSION` (first present candidate is selected, without promoting `ACTIONABLE` over earlier `WATCH` candidates).
