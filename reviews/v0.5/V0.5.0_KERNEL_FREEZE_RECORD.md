## 3. Frozen Semantic Boundaries

The accepted kernel establishes and permanently freezes the following semantic boundaries:

- **PIT / Information-Set Semantics**: Strict point-in-time constraints where unclosed candles, missing availability timestamps, post-signal availability, product/interval mismatches, and cadence gaps fail closed. Future rows cannot enter candidate observation sets.
- **Deterministic Material-Input Reconstruction**: Candidate inputs cannot be selected arbitrarily by callers. The authoritative producer contract and decision timestamp uniquely determine the required lookback window, reconstructed independently during cold verification.
- **Producer-Contract Identity & Immutability**: Registered producer contracts are recursively immutable (`freeze_json` / `FrozenDict`), detached from caller-owned preimages, and bound to cryptographic hashes. Any modification or stale-preimage mismatch is detected and rejected.
- **Funding-Evidence Authority & Coverage**: Candidate qualification strictly requires cryptographic binding to authoritative funding evidence artifacts matching the exact data coverage interval. Funding rate and event modifications fail closed on independent replay.
- **Canonical Execution & Accounting Replay**: Order actions, fill prices, fees, slippage, equity curves, round-trip attributions, and terminal equities are verified by independent simulation replay. Forged ledgers cannot bypass verification via recomputed hashes.
- **CASH & RANDOM_MATCHED Benchmark Authority**: The CASH benchmark remains strictly zero-activity and constant-equity. RANDOM_MATCHED provides deterministic execution against preregistered seeds and opportunity sets. Candidate and benchmark producer roles cannot be swapped.
- **Qualification, Evidence-Chain & P5 Promotion**: Promotion to `ECONOMICALLY_QUALIFIED` requires an unbroken, complete evidence chain (dataset, funding, candidate run, benchmark suite, and evaluation result). Missing, truncated, or substituted evidence is rejected atomically without modifying registry state or generation.
- **Publication & Persistence Invariants**: Registry updates are strictly append-only, content-addressed, and protected by compare-and-swap (CAS) generation checks.
- **Formal vs. Legacy Boundary**: Legacy diagnostic routines (`shadow.update_shadow`, unverified summary wrappers) are non-authoritative and cannot grant economic qualification.
- **Read-Only Observational GET Behavior**: Retention of operational GET endpoints (such as `/health` and `/signals`) is verified strictly read-only, producing zero DDL, zero schema migrations, and zero sidecar additions.
- **Protected Surface Separation**: H39 research operational code and Final Holdout partitions remain strictly isolated and inaccessible to formal qualification jobs.
- **Execution-Disabled Boundary**: Order submission, execution routing, and live trading capabilities remain disabled by default (`default-deny`).

Future research may register new candidate protocols, hypotheses, or contracts on top of this baseline, but must never silently alter or weaken these frozen semantics under the same identity.
