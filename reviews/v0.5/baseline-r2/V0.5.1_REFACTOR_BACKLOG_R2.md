## 1. Governance and Refactor Discipline

Under the engineering governance rules defined in `AGENTS.md`, speculative abstractions, premature modularization, and cosmetic refactoring are strictly prohibited. Technical debt items are evaluated strictly by their impact on **correctness, Point-In-Time (PIT) integrity, scientific credibility, and research feasibility**.

### Classification Standard:
1. **`MAINLINE_BLOCKER`**: Issues that actively corrupt PIT causality, violate cryptographic provenance, invalidate statistical results, or prevent the execution of H40 Discovery on the 18 REGISTERED slots.
2. **`DEFER_UNTIL_AFTER_FIRST_PLAYBOOK`**: High-value structural improvements that do not affect the validity of the first 18 slots and must wait until `FIRST_VALIDATED_PLAYBOOK_V1` is achieved.
3. **`DEFER_UNTIL_M8_M9`**: Operational, deployment, or execution-layer integrations required only for paper or live production trading.
4. **`RETIRE_LATER`**: Obsolete v0.2.x / v0.3.x code paths that can be pruned during post-playbook codebase cleanup.
5. **`NO_ACTION`**: Architectural modules that are large or complex but thoroughly tested, cryptographically sealed, and best left intact to avoid regression risks.

## 4. TradingAgents Boundary and Isolation Architecture

As mandated by Section 13 of the project specification, **TradingAgents** is a completely separate, parallel research track.

### Strict Governance Constraints:
1. **Zero Dependency**: The quantitative core (`src/btc_quant_agent`) must NOT import, depend upon, or interface with any TradingAgents module.

4. **Future Integration Seam**: Any future integration of TradingAgents (e.g., in Milestones M8/M9) will occur strictly as an asynchronous signal advisory consumer through an external read-only adapter, without modifying the underlying quantitative and economic kernel.
