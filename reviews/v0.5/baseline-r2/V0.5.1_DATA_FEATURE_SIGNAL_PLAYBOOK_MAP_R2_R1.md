## 3. Operational Roles in Candidate Trading Playbooks

1. **Direction Source**: Generates trade side $\text{action} \in \{+1, -1\}$. Must exhibit net positive expectancy over the 12 bps hurdle (`COST_PROXY_12BPS_V1`).
2. **Mandatory Support Contracts**:
   - `R_VOL_RANGE_V1_24H`: Enforces volatility regime qualification.
   - `O_RANGE_EXPANSION_V1_24H`: Signals structural expansion setup.
3. **Crowding Filters**: Forbids trade initiation when market positioning is heavily one-sided (e.g. funding squeeze).
4. **Execution Timing Gates**: Evaluates sub-second L2 depth immediately prior to dispatch to prevent excessive slippage.
