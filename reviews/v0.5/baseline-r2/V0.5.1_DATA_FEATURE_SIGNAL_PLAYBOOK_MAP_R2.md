## 3. Playbook Role Archetypes for `FIRST_VALIDATED_PLAYBOOK_V1`

In constructing candidate trading playbooks, quantitative components must be assigned strictly distinct operational roles:

1. **Direction Source (Core Alpha)**:
   - Evaluates multi-hour price or momentum continuation/reversal (`D1`, `D2`, `D3`).
   - Generates candidate trade direction: $\text{action} \in \{+1, -1\}$.
   - Must demonstrate statistical edge exceeding the 12 bps cost hurdle across walk-forward splits.
2. **Regime Filter (Macro Context)**:
   - Evaluates macro volatility or compression state (`R_VOL_RANGE_V1`).
   - Switches the playbook between aggressive trend-following, mean-reversion, or total capital preservation.
3. **Opportunity Trigger (Timing)**:
   - Identifies structural volatility expansion (`O_RANGE_EXPANSION_V1`).
   - Dictates whether an actionable setup exists on the current closed bar.
4. **Crowding & Squeeze Filter (Risk Mitigation)**:
   - Detects extreme positioning in funding rates (`D5`) or top-trader long/short ratios.
   - Forbids trade entry in the direction of crowded consensus, eliminating severe liquidation risk.
5. **Entry Confirmation & Timing Gate (Microstructure)**:
   - Evaluates real-time L2 orderbook imbalance, spread, or microprice drift immediately prior to dispatch.
   - Blocks or delays market order submission when liquidity is vacuumed or spread exceeds 3 bps.
6. **NO_TRADE Abstention Policy (Capital Preservation)**:
   - Active whenever calibrated probability $P(\text{win}) < 0.55$ or adverse regime is detected.
   - Guarantees zero unnecessary turnover during choppy or non-directional market regimes.
