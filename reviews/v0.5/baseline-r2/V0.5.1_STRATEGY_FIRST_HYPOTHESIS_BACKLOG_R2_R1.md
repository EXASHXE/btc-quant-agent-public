## 2. Mandatory Question Q3 — Historical Hypothesis Families Audit (Reconciled)

The following audit reviews historically attempted families across v0.2.x–v0.3.x as **non-authoritative engineering observations and lessons learned**, providing empirical context rather than formal scientific falsification:

### 2.1 Family H-HIST-01: Naive Moving Average Crossover (v0.2.x)
- **Question**: Can fast/slow EMA crossovers (e.g., 9/21 EMA on 15m/1h) predict directional continuation in BTCUSDT?
- **Information Set**: High-frequency closed OHLCV.
- **Historical Engineering Observation**: In historical v0.2 testing, naive moving average crossovers suffered severe whipsaw losses during ranging markets. Moreover, unmodeled transaction friction (10 bps round-trip taker fees) created persistent negative expectancy.
- **Engineering Lesson**: Directional trend signals require explicit volatility regime conditioning and wide enough profit horizons to overcome exchange transaction costs.
- **Active Code Residue**: `src/btc_quant_agent/indicators.py:85-120`.

### 2.2 Family H-HIST-02: RSI & Bollinger Band Mean Reversion (v0.2.x)
- **Question**: Does extreme RSI (<25 or >75) or 2-sigma Bollinger band penetration predict mean-reverting price bounces?
- **Information Set**: 15m/1h OHLCV.
- **Historical Engineering Observation**: In historical v0.2 testing, unconditioned counter-trend entries into strong trending moves resulted in severe tail drawdowns ("catching falling knives").
- **Engineering Lesson**: Mean-reversion hypotheses must incorporate structural momentum exhaustion or failed-breakout price action rather than relying solely on unbounded oscillator levels.
- **Active Code Residue**: `src/btc_quant_agent/strategies.py:140-195`.

### 2.3 Family H-HIST-03: High-Frequency L2 Orderbook Skew (v0.3.15–v0.3.18)
- **Question**: Does top-5 bid/ask depth imbalance on 100ms WebSocket streams predict sub-minute price drift?
- **Information Set**: Binance Futures WebSocket depth diffs.
- **Historical Engineering Observation**: Empirical analysis revealed that L2 depth imbalance alpha decays within 1–3 seconds, well before retail REST/WebSocket round-trip order dispatch can execute without high-speed exchange colocation. Furthermore, local historical archives were forward-only.
- **Engineering Lesson**: Reclassified strictly as an **Entry Timing / Slippage Filter**, never as a standalone directional alpha source.
- **Active Code Residue**: `src/btc_quant_agent/microstructure.py:340-410`.

### 2.4 Family H-HIST-04: Funding Rate Extreme Crowding (v0.3.19)
- **Question**: Does extreme funding rate indicate market positioning exhaustion and impending squeeze?
- **Information Set**: Binance Futures 8h discrete funding payments.
- **Historical Engineering Observation**: v0.3.19 prototypes demonstrated promising contrarian risk mitigation, but were suspended due to the lack of cryptographic source manifests for raw CSV archives.
- **Engineering Lesson**: Retained in the formalized H40 protocol as the D5 family, awaiting P2 cryptographic source manifest sealing.
- **Active Code Residue**: `src/btc_quant_agent/h40/feature_contracts.py:250-290` (`D5_V1`, `D5_V2`).
