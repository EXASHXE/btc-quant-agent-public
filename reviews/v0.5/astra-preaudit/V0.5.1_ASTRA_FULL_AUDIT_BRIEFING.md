## 5. Critical Historical Failures That Shaped the Architecture

Astra, do not repeat historical mistakes. The following trading hypotheses were rigorously tested and **falsified**:

1. **Price-Only Heuristic Patterns (H11, H14)**:
   - Trend pullbacks and breakout continuations based purely on price action (EMAs, ATRs, pivots) yield zero statistical edge over a random walk ($p > 0.40$).
2. **Funding Crowding Direction (H18)**:
   - Extreme funding rate percentiles ($Q_{10}, Q_{90}$) do not reliably predict directional price reversals on BTC. Edge was non-stationary and regime-dependent.
3. **Cross-Asset Altcoin Breadth (H26)**:
   - Altcoin momentum (ETH, SOL, BNB, ADA) does not lead Bitcoin. Altcoins lag or reflect simultaneous market beta.
4. **Range Mean Reversion (H29)**:
   - Bollinger Band 2-sigma mean reversion in low-volatility ranges loses money after paying 5 bps taker fees and suffering adverse selection.
5. **Unconstrained Symbolic Search (v0.3.18/v0.3.19)**:
   - Genetic programming and DSL search over historical features produce curve-fitted formulas that collapse out-of-sample. Bounded search budgets are mandatory.
