### In-Depth Risk Mitigations

1. **Scientific Leakage & Outcome Contamination (BLOCKER)**:
   - *Mitigation*: Strictly enforced by `src/btc_quant_agent/h40/guards.py`. Any code path attempting to resolve paths containing `h39_validation`, `final_holdout`, or `confirmation_holdout` triggers an immediate `H40GuardError` and terminates process execution.
2. **Operational Resource Exhaustion (HIGH)**:
   - *Mitigation*: Merge branch `forward-evidence-health-lightweight` (`743c936`) to permanently decouple routine 15m health probes from deep multi-gigabyte forensic scans.
3. **Execution Modeling & Adverse Selection (HIGH)**:
   - *Mitigation*: The economic kernel simulator (`economic/execution_model.py`) applies conservative default penalties: 5 bps taker fee + 2 bps per-side slippage + worst-case intrabar path assumption. In v0.6, limit order modeling must require explicit queue simulation.
4. **Authority Bypass & Search Budget Creep (HIGH)**:
   - *Mitigation*: Governed cryptographically by `structural_ledger_hash` (`483f6850...`). The 168-slot budget cannot be expanded or recycled; any modification changes the root hash and invalidates the protocol authority.
