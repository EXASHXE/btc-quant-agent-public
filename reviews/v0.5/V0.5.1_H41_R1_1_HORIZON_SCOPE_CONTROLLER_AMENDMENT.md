# 6. Economic and Statistical Tradeoff Analysis

The Controller makes no claim that 24h is inherently superior to 12h. The pre-outcome justification is structural:
- **Potential Scientific Benefits**:
  - Allows slower multi-bar trend and breakout continuation to mature into a full diurnal cycle;
  - Reduces the economic dominance of the fixed 12bp friction proxy relative to larger cumulative price excursions;
  - Explores a genuinely different temporal regime than the 4h and 8h horizons.
- **Statistical Costs and Methodological Demands**:
  - Substantially increases overlapping-return serial correlation ($h=24$ generates up to 23 hours of overlap between consecutive events);
  - Reduces the effective independent sample size;
  - Exposes the event to greater regime shifts and macro shocks;
  - Increases the relevance of funding payments (funding remains excluded from the event-proxy endpoint and is deferred to playbook qualification);
  - Imposes stricter demands on block-bootstrap resampling length.
