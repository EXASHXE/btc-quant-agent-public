## 7. Configuration Ledger Specification

The configuration ledger is an append-only, content-addressed registry:
- **Maximum Budget**: Strictly 168 complete slots (`MAX_CONFIGURATION_SLOTS = 168`).
- **Slot Recycling**: Never recycled. Dropped or invalid slots remain recorded with status `DROPPED` and their `H40ReasonCode`.
- **Direction Family Authority**: Strictly limited to BASE D1–D5:
  1. `D1_TREND_CONTINUATION`
  2. `D2_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION`
  3. `D3_FAILED_MOVE_REVERSAL`
  4. `D4_BTC_ETH_CONFIRM_DIVERGE`
  5. `D5_FUNDING_DIRECTION_INTERACTION`
- **Authorized Depth-Two Combinations**: Strictly 4 authorized pairs:
  - `(D1, D4)`
  - `(D1, D5)`
  - `(D2, D4)`
  - `(D3, D5)`
  All other pairs (e.g. `D1+D2`, `D2+D5`) or depth >= 3 fail closed with `FAMILY_PAIR_RESTRICTED`.
- **Identity Fields**: Primary horizon (must be exactly one of `4h`, `8h`, `12h`), action threshold $\tau \in \{0.55, 0.60, 0.65\}$, and asset scope $\subseteq \{\text{BTCUSDT}, \text{ETHUSDT}\}$.
- **Prohibitions**: No performance metric fields (Sharpe, profit factor, return, pnl, drawdown, etc.) are allowed in pre-outcome hyperparameters.
