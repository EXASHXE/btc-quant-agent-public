## E01: economic boundary marks

The January 2021 source-only sample found `Open(B_k) != Close(B_{k-1})` at
**559/743 BTC** and **595/743 ETH** adjacent boundaries. These are counts,
not strategy outcomes or exposed prices. R1 therefore defines:

```text
P_entry(t)  = Open of the canonical Binance USD-M perpetual 1h kline
              whose open_time == t
P_exit(t,h) = Open of the canonical Binance USD-M perpetual 1h kline
              whose open_time == t+h, h in {4,8,24}
```

No adjacent-bar Open/previous-Close equality is assumed. The prior Close is
an independent economic field and cannot replace the reference Open. The
event mask and signal at `t` use only completed information through
`B_0(t)=[t-1h,t)`; they must not read `Open(B_entry(t))`. The Open is the first
economic price represented for its interval and is an event-study outcome
reference mark, not an executable fill. Historical archives establish
exchange-bucket bar semantics but not true forward local receipt latency.
