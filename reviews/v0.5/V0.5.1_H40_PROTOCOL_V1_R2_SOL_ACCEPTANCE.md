## Closure review

### R1-SOL-01 — direction-family authority

Closed. R2 explicitly voids the conflicting shorthand in R1 and restores BASE Section 5.3 as the sole authority for D1–D5:

- `D1_TREND_CONTINUATION`
- `D2_BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION`
- `D3_FAILED_MOVE_REVERSAL`
- `D4_BTC_ETH_CONFIRM_DIVERGE`
- `D5_FUNDING_DIRECTION_INTERACTION`

The only allowed depth-two combinations remain `D1+D4`, `D1+D5`, `D2+D4`, and `D3+D5`. Search budget remains 168 complete configurations.

### R1-SOL-02 — HAC time-lag semantics

Closed. R2 supersedes accepted-trade-index lags with exact UTC entry-time lag sets for `k = 1..24` hours. Simultaneous BTC/ETH entries remain lag zero and cannot manufacture one-hour adjacency. Bartlett weights, fail-closed behavior, clipping, occupied-day floor, and persisted reconstruction inputs are explicitly specified.

The chosen sparse-pair HAC definition is a preregistered deterministic statistic. This acceptance does not claim it is the only statistically valid estimator; it is accepted because its semantics are fixed before outcomes and will be used consistently with block-bootstrap uncertainty.

### R1-SOL-03 — bootstrap implementation determinism

Closed. R2 fixes:

- nearest-rank lower confidence-bound indices with explicit one-based and zero-based mapping;
- upper max-statistic critical-value indexing;
- exact centered one-sided marginal bootstrap p-values with `+1` correction and weak `>=` comparison;
- exact canonical-JSON seed preimage;
- SHA-256 first-eight-byte big-endian conversion;
- explicit `np.random.Generator(np.random.PCG64(seed))` construction;
- required persistence of seed/reconstruction metadata.

### Confirmation p0 boundary

Closed. Confirmation training, purge, and holdout are exact half-open UTC intervals. Confirmation label base rates come only from the pre-confirmation training interval; confirmation side weights may be computed from emitted confirmation actions, but confirmation labels cannot enter the training base rates.
