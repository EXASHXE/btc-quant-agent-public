### 5.4 Candidate Lock Rule & Fail-Closed Logic

  1. Highest full-precision simultaneous $\text{LCB}$;
  2. Larger event count $N$;
  3. Lexicographically lower `candidate_id`.
- **At most ONE candidate** may be locked: `H41_EXPLORATORY_DIRECTIONAL_CANDIDATE_LOCK_V1`.
- If zero candidates meet the threshold: declare **`H41_EXPLORATORY_NO_GO`**. The threshold must not be softened, and no secondary fallback candidate may be selected.
