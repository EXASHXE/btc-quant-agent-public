## 5. Canonical clock and feature notation

Let `t` be a UTC hour boundary.

For asset `a ∈ {BTC, ETH}`, define `B_a[t]` as the native 1h bar covering
`[t-1h, t)`. The latest decision input at boundary `t` is therefore the
completed bar `B_a[t]`.

Its fields are:

`O_a[t], H_a[t], L_a[t], C_a[t], V_a[t]`.

All prices and volume used below must be finite and strictly positive where the
formula requires positivity. Any missing/nonfinite/nonpositive required field is
`SOURCE_INPUT_UNAVAILABLE` and produces no event.

Define:

`r_a^m(t) = ln(C_a[t] / C_a[t-mh])`

for integer `m ≥ 1`.

Define 24h realized volatility feature:

`RV_a24(t) = sqrt(sum_{j=0}^{23} (r_a^1(t-jh))^2)`.

Define the current completed-bar volume ratio:

`VR_a24(t) = V_a[t] / median(V_a[t-h], ..., V_a[t-24h])`.

The median is the ordinary midpoint median of the 24 strictly prior completed
volumes.

### TRAIN-only quantile operator

The development TRAIN interval is:

`[2021-01-01T00:00:00Z, 2022-10-31T00:00:00Z)`.

For any scalar feature X and asset a, `Q_a,p(X)` is the deterministic
nearest-rank empirical quantile over every eligible TRAIN UTC hour:

1. sort finite eligible values ascending as `x_(1)...x_(n)`;
2. rank `k = ceil(p*n)`;
3. `Q_p = x_(k)`.

No return endpoint, PnL, future label, H41 calibration outcome or protected
successor outcome participates in quantile fitting.

All threshold values are mechanically materialized once from this exact rule and
the accepted TRAIN source, then hash-bound before D0. There is no alternative
quantile definition or outcome-based threshold selection.

## 6. Exact formal candidate ledger — K=8

All eight coordinates have:

- holding horizon `h = 4h`;
- symmetric long/short side semantics;
- candidate-local deterministic cooldown `4h`;
- the same paired BTC/ETH source-membership clock;
- no parameter grid;
- no alternative horizon;
- no per-regime separate claim.

`sign(x)` is +1 for x>0, -1 for x<0 and 0 for x=0. A required side of 0 means
no event.

### SCV1_A_TREND_BTC / SCV1_A_TREND_ETH

For target asset a:

`T_a(t) = r_a^6(t)`.

Composite gate:

`abs(T_a(t)) >= Q_a,0.80(abs(r_a^6))`

AND

`Q_a,0.20(RV24) <= RV_a24(t) <= Q_a,0.80(RV24)`.

Side:

`side = sign(T_a(t))`.

Economic mechanism: delayed continuation after a large six-hour displacement
that occurs outside both the lowest and highest 24h volatility states.

### SCV1_A_REVERT_BTC / SCV1_A_REVERT_ETH

For target asset a, define the prior four-hour displacement ending one hour
before the current decision bar:

`D_a(t) = ln(C_a[t-h] / C_a[t-5h])`.

Define current completed-hour reversal:

`u_a(t) = r_a^1(t)`.

Composite gate:

`abs(D_a(t)) >= Q_a,0.80(abs(D_a))`

AND

`RV_a24(t) >= Q_a,0.60(RV24)`

AND

`D_a(t) * u_a(t) < 0`

AND

`abs(u_a(t)) >= 0.25 * abs(D_a(t))`.

Side:

`side = -sign(D_a(t))`.

Economic mechanism: delayed continuation of a completed reversal after a large
prior displacement, not immediate fade at the original extreme.

### SCV1_C_VOLUME_BTC / SCV1_C_VOLUME_ETH

For target asset a:

`M_a(t) = r_a^2(t)`.

Composite gate:

`abs(M_a(t)) >= Q_a,0.60(abs(r_a^2))`

AND

`VR_a24(t) >= Q_a,0.80(VR24)`

AND

`RV_a24(t) <= Q_a,0.90(RV24)`.

Side:

`side = sign(M_a(t))`.

Economic mechanism: delayed continuation after an already completed directional
move accompanied by unusually high completed-bar participation, excluding the
most extreme volatility tail.

This is total native kline volume participation, not signed aggTrade flow/CVD.

### SCV1_D_LEAD_BTC / SCV1_D_LEAD_ETH

For target asset a and the other asset o:

`L_o(t) = r_o^2(t)`

`N_a(t) = r_a^2(t)`.

Composite gate:

`abs(L_o(t)) >= Q_o,0.80(abs(r_o^2))`

AND

`abs(N_a(t)) <= Q_a,0.50(abs(r_a^2))`.

Side:

`side = sign(L_o(t))`.

Economic mechanism: delayed target response after a large completed move in the
other asset while the target has not yet made a comparably large two-hour move.

No relative-spread alternative is retained in V1.

## 7. Event formation, ties, cooldown and alias policy

A formal trigger becomes an event only if:

1. the complete composite gate is true;
2. side is nonzero;
3. the paired forward source contract for that hour is valid and on time;
4. the candidate is not inside its own cooldown.

Cooldown rule:

after an accepted event at decision boundary `t0`, another trigger for that
same candidate with `t < t0+4h` is recorded as
`COOLDOWN_SUPPRESSED`. A trigger exactly at `t0+4h` is eligible.

Cross-candidate and cross-asset overlap is retained and handled by joint
inference; it is not silently deduplicated.

The formal family is exactly K=8. There is no low-support replacement candidate.

Before freeze, a structural alias audit is fail-closed rather than opportunistic:

- identical canonical formula hashes; or
- TRAIN event-mask Jaccard = 1.0 **and** side agreement = 1.0

between two distinct IDs causes `DESIGN_BLOCKED_ALIAS` and requires a new
reviewed design. The Controller does not simply delete one slot and continue.

Jaccard below 1.0 is descriptive dependence evidence, not proof of
“orthogonality”.

## 8. Forward source and causal-availability contract

The source contract is split into two distinct claims.

### 8.1 Historical economic-field authority

Historical accepted BTC/ETH 1h native economic OHLCV provides field/bucket
semantics and TRAIN development data.

It does not prove forward local availability.

### 8.2 Forward as-observed availability authority

For every expected UTC hour boundary t, the forward adapter must preserve for
both BTC and ETH:

- raw received payload bytes;
- canonical projected row;
- exchange event/close identifiers;
- local receive timestamp;
- ingest/finalization timestamp;
- revision/version identity;
- raw and canonical hashes;
- paired-hour membership status.

For successor scientific use, define:

`d_t = max(receive_time_BTC[t], receive_time_ETH[t])`.

The paired input is **ON_TIME** only if both bars exist and:

`d_t < t + 1h`.

If either feed is absent or this inequality fails:

- all eight candidate decisions at t are `NO_EVENT_LATE_OR_MISSING_SOURCE`;
- no event may be backfilled later;
- the missed hour remains in the denominator/source ledger.

Later official archive data is a reconciliation surface only. It may not replace
the as-observed decision input used by an already committed event.

A later revision that changes an OHLCV field used in the decision is logged as
`SOURCE_REVISION_INCIDENT`; it never silently rewrites the event history.

### 8.3 Pre-D0 source-closure acceptance

Before D0, independent source closure must observe at least 720 expected paired
hourly boundaries under the production forward adapter.

Requirements:

- zero unresolved canonical-field semantic mismatches;
- zero retroactively created events;
- zero silent revisions;
- paired on-time availability point estimate >= 99.5%;
- the 95% Wilson lower confidence bound for paired on-time availability >= 99.0%;
- all expected hours accounted for, including late/missing hours;
- replay from stored raw bytes reproduces the exact decision-input hashes.

The 720-hour requirement is an operational measurement window, not arbitrary
calendar padding: it supplies enough hourly trials to bound the forward
availability error rate. Source closure remains a separate independent
epistemic gate.

Failure returns `SOURCE_CLOSURE_NOT_ACCEPTED`; it never opens outcomes.

## 9. Delayed reference endpoint and cost contract

This V1 intentionally tests a **delayed** 1h-source mechanism.

At decision boundary t, the completed bar ending at t becomes available only
after the native Open at t has already occurred. Because V1 does not authorize a
finer-granularity execution source, the earliest native 1h Open strictly after
the receipt deadline is the Open at `t+1h`.

For target asset a:

- reference entry:
  native economic Open at UTC `t+1h`;
- reference exit:
  native economic Open at UTC `t+5h`;
- holding duration:
  4h;
- signal-to-exit duration:
  5h.

If the paired decision source is not on time before `t+1h`, the event does not
exist. The endpoint never pretends that the Open at t was tradable after receipt.

Primary gross directional return:

`G = side * (P_exit / P_entry - 1)`.

### Primary scientific reference friction

`C_primary = 12 bp` round trip for every event.

Primary endpoint:

`Y_primary = G - 0.0012`.

This 12bp value is a **reference research-friction budget**, retained
prospectively for comparability with the previously frozen pre-outcome H41
endpoint. It is not represented as the user's fee tier, actual funding cashflow
or guaranteed executable cost.

No funding series or settlement-count rule enters the primary V1 endpoint.

### Stress diagnostic

`C_stress = 24 bp`.

`Y_stress = G - 0.0024`.

Stress is diagnostic and can never replace the primary endpoint after outcomes.

The formal scientific claim is therefore only about mean return after the fixed
12bp reference budget. Any claim about executable all-cost PnL requires later
playbook qualification with independent fee/slippage/funding evidence.

A future change in real exchange fees, funding or execution quality does not
retroactively change this endpoint. It can block deployment or trigger a new
campaign, but cannot rewrite scientific history.

## 10. Economic-usefulness, planning effect and formal null

These are deliberately separate quantities.

### Formal null

For each candidate:

`H0: mu_primary <= 0`

versus one-sided:

`H1: mu_primary > 0`.

Passing the scientific gate proves neither actual executable profitability nor a
larger minimum effect.

### Economic-usefulness benchmark

`delta_useful = 12 bp` after primary reference friction.

Reason: an event with +12bp mean after the 12bp primary budget has zero mean
under the separately frozen 24bp stress budget, before execution-model error.
This is a transparent research robustness benchmark, not the formal null.

### Planning alternative

`delta_plan = 24 bp` after primary reference friction.

At this planning effect, the same gross return retains +12bp mean under the 24bp
stress diagnostic.

Synthetic planning must additionally report operating characteristics at:

`{12, 24, 36, 48} bp`.

The 24bp value controls duration selection; the other values expose sensitivity.
No H41 observed mean was used to choose these values.

## 11. Fixed-window lifecycle and full-pipeline power rule

R1.1 rejects sequential outcome looks for V1.

There is exactly one Discovery unblind and, if a candidate locks and passes the
independent lock audit, exactly one Validation unblind.

Candidate Discovery durations:

`D ∈ {6, 9, 12, 18, 24} calendar months`.

Candidate single-candidate Validation durations:

`V ∈ {3, 6, 9, 12} calendar months`.

Before D0, successor-specific synthetic validation evaluates every pair (D,V)
under the exact K=8 procedure.

A pair is admissible only if all of the following hold over the entire declared
synthetic DGP envelope:

1. global-null Discovery FWER one-sided 95% Monte-Carlo upper confidence bound
   <= 0.05;
2. single-candidate Validation false-positive rate one-sided 95% Monte-Carlo
   upper confidence bound <= 0.05;
3. at `delta_plan = 24bp`, the lower 95% Monte-Carlo confidence bound on
   **full-pipeline success** is >= 0.80 for every possible true coordinate;
4. full-pipeline success means:
   true coordinate is formally eligible, passes Discovery simultaneous LCB,
   is selected by the frozen lock rule, passes independent lock audit in the
   simulated state machine, and passes the later Validation gate;
5. source gaps/support screening/zero-variance states are included in the
   simulation rather than conditioned away.

Select the admissible pair minimizing:

`D + V`.

Tie-break:

1. smaller D;
2. smaller V.

If no pair is admissible:

`DESIGN_BLOCKED_INSUFFICIENT_POWER`.

There is no extension after seeing real Discovery outcomes.

This replaces the R1 round N>=200 / D>=120 / 80-block rule and the unconditional
24m/12m schedule.

## 12. Successor-specific inference contract

Formal family size is fixed:

`K = 8`.

Primary method:

`single-step shared-clock source-bundle-preserving studentized joint maximum`.

Fixed design constants:

- block construction: non-circular moving blocks on the common UTC hourly grid;
- block length: `L = 120h`;
- bootstrap replicates for formal inference: `B = 100000`;
- FWER: one-sided `alpha = 0.05`;
- no step-down fallback;
- no hierarchical fallback;
- no reseeding after results;
- no candidate deletion after D0.

Bootstrap seeds are derived mechanically after the semantic authority root is
materialized:

`seed_discovery = uint64_be(SHA256(root || ":DISCOVERY_BOOTSTRAP")[0:8])`

`seed_validation = uint64_be(SHA256(root || ":VALIDATION_BOOTSTRAP")[0:8])`.

For candidate k on the common hourly grid:

- `A_k,t ∈ {0,1}` is the frozen event indicator;
- `Y_k,t` is the frozen primary endpoint when A=1;
- `mu_hat_k = sum(A*Y)/sum(A)`.

Candidate eligibility is **not** determined by round N/D thresholds.

Mathematical minimums:

- at least two event observations;
- finite endpoint values;
- strictly positive estimable variance.

Concentration adequacy uses 120h calendar blocks. If `n_j` is the candidate's
event count in block j, define:

`B_eff = (sum_j n_j)^2 / sum_j(n_j^2)`.

Before D0, synthetic validation chooses one
`B_eff_min ∈ {12, 18, 24, 36, 48}` using the same strong-error/full-pipeline
criteria above. Choose the smallest passing value; if none passes, design is
blocked.

N, occupied UTC days and raw block occupancy remain reported diagnostics, not
formal gates.

An ineligible coordinate remains one of the eight family slots and cannot be
replaced. Its formal statistic is unavailable and it cannot lock. The synthetic
validation must include this support-screen behavior when establishing FWER and
power.

For eligible candidates, the formal Discovery gate is simultaneous lower bound:

`LCB_k = mu_hat_k - c_joint * SE_k > 0`.

At most one candidate locks.

Lock ranking:

1. highest positive LCB;
2. higher `B_eff`;
3. higher raw event count N;
4. lexicographically smaller candidate_id.

No runner-up substitution is permitted after the independent lock audit or
Validation begins.

Validation tests only the locked candidate with its frozen endpoint and a
one-sided alpha=0.05 gate validated prospectively as part of the full pipeline.

## 13. Synthetic validation envelope

No protected real successor outcome is used.

The validation harness must stress at least:

- multivariate Student-t innovations with df=5;
- serial dependence rho in `{0.0, 0.3, 0.6}`;
- cross-coordinate dependence in `{0.25, 0.60, 0.90}`;
- persistent low/high volatility states with state persistence in
  `{0.80, 0.95}`;
- candidate event-rate and burstiness patterns calibrated only from TRAIN
  **feature/event clocks**, never TRAIN return outcomes;
- event-rate perturbations of at least ±25% around TRAIN feature-clock rates;
- common paired-source gaps and candidate-specific cooldown;
- unequal candidate counts;
- clustered masks;
- exact/near alias adversarial cases;
- zero-event and zero-variance cases;
- one non-null coordinate at a time for every candidate ID;
- mixed alternatives with more than one non-null coordinate as a diagnostic;
- effect grid `{0, 12, 24, 36, 48}bp` after primary cost.

The harness must report Monte-Carlo confidence intervals for FWER, formal
eligibility, correct lock, Validation pass and end-to-end validated-playbook
success.

The exact DGP generator/version, RNG, replicate count and all parameter grids
become part of the prospective machine authority.

If L=120h, B=100000, the support rule, or the fixed duration grid fails the
declared operating-characteristic criteria, R1.1 does **not** authorize choosing
