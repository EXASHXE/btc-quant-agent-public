## 1. Decision and scope

**REDESIGN_REQUIRED_BEFORE_FREEZE**

The proposed new forward campaign is a credible direction for further design. Historical native BTC/ETH klines provide a real engineering foundation; volume participation and synchronized cross-asset hypotheses need no unauthorized derivatives or microstructure promotion. H41 remains immutable `ACCEPTED_TERMINAL_NO_GO`.

R1 is not ready for a mechanical preregistration/freeze step. Candidate equations and regime bindings, information availability, primary cost, support adequacy, and the duration/power tradeoff still require scientific decisions. Independent forward **SOURCE_CLOSURE_REQUIRED** is an epistemic gate. This is not `DATA_SOURCE_BLOCKED`: there is no demonstrated reason the price/volume thesis cannot be executed with prospectively closable sources. Nor is this a finding of a demonstrated successor trading edge or of deliberate outcome-selected design.

Recommended budget: **8 initial primary coordinates, reducible to 6 or fewer on a prespecified structural alias review; absolute ceiling 12 only with distinct hypothesis justification**. Recommended duration rule: select the **shortest fixed window** among 6/9/12/18/24 months that satisfies the revised, independently reviewed support/error/power contract before D0. On the draft's toy assumptions, 9-month Discovery / 6-month single-candidate Validation is the shorter defensible planning case; the higher-noise case requires about 24 / 12 months. Neither pair is established for actual successor events. A concrete 6–24-month sequential alternative is evaluated below; it is not automatically faster than a well-chosen short fixed design.

The audit does not authorize source download/closure, production implementation, roster freeze, Discovery, Validation, candidate selection, or execution. Only the two requested audit artifacts are published.

### SCV1-B01 — Exact hypotheses and regime binding must precede freeze

**Exact design claim:** Report §§5–6, lines 48–74, proposes six mirrored mechanisms, one regime gate per mechanism, and a next preregistration that supplies formulas, lookbacks, thresholds, cooldown and alias elimination. JSON `candidate_family_proposal[*]` contains `TO_FREEZE` / `TRAIN_FIT_TO_FREEZE`; `regime_proposal` lists 3×2×2 labels.

**Problem / scientific failure mode:** There is no mathematically defined family to count or validate yet. Expansion, trend and volume acceptance may be the same continuation rule with overlapping gates. Lead/nonresponse and relative-divergence/convergence may be reparameterizations of the same relative-return state. A single candidate ID can conceal multiple tried windows, formulas, regime choices, or cooldowns. TRAIN-only fitting prevents forward outcome use but does not itself select an economic hypothesis or define how many variants were considered.

**Minimum repair:** Supply exact symbolic equations and one deterministic regime mapping per coordinate, including all logical conjunctions, signs, equality, missingness, persistence/cooldown, thresholds and fitting rules. State the estimand as one marginal mean *conditional on the full fixed event rule*. No separate primary claims for LOW/NORMAL/HIGH, sides, months or diagnostic subgroups. Freeze the structural alias and low-support exclusion rule before applying it; record every removed slot and do not refill it. Treat “one gate” as one complete Boolean expression, not one adjustable label. Prefer the eight-coordinate shortlist in section 6; a retained 12 needs evidence of distinct hypotheses rather than available capacity.

**Another Astra audit:** Yes. New formulas/gates/horizons are substantive scientific authority, not clerical materialization. The repaired report should include symbolic counterexamples distinguishing proposed mechanisms, and synthetic side/event tests. Real outcomes are unnecessary.

### SCV1-H01 — Economic parity is not historical receipt-time parity

**Exact design claim:** Report §§3, 7, 9, 12 (lines 19–31, 76–80, 95–111, 129–141), JSON `unresolved_blockers[0]` and `partition_proposal.forward_source_receipt_rule`, require forward extension and “receive-time parity,” with entry Open(t+1h) after receipt of the completed signal bar.

**Problem / scientific failure mode:** The historical 14-field projection has no local receipt time. A forward signal bar can arrive after t+1h. A month-end archive can correct an earlier value, and re-hashing the corrected file does not make that value available at the original decision. Event-time equivalence therefore does not prove equal information sets. Current text does not fix the late-receipt deadline, as-observed versus revised version used for features, or whether a missing bar can later be backfilled into an event.

**Minimum repair:** Split the source contract into (1) archival economic-field/bucket equivalence and (2) forward availability evidence. At t, freeze the completed-bar input cutoff; commit a decision only at its actual processing time d_t after both required feeds arrive. Require d_t plus a frozen processing/entry margin to precede the reference entry boundary; otherwise record NO_EVENT/LATE_SOURCE with no retrospective recovery. Preserve raw payload hashes, receive/ingest/finalization times, sequence/revision identity, decisions and denominators. Later official archives reconcile values but cannot silently replace causal features. Freeze separate reference-outcome revision rules. Define actual first-economic-Open semantics and missing-hour handling for both feeds. Section 5 states minimum pre-D0 closure.

**Another Astra audit:** Yes on revised semantics; independent source authority acceptance must verify the prospective observed prefix and exact adapter. No historical latency reconstruction is possible or required.

### SCV1-H02 — Round-number support and toy SD do not determine required years

**Exact design claim:** Report §10, lines 113–119; JSON `support_power_proposal`: N≥200, D≥120, ≥80 occupied 120h blocks; proposed delta=30bp and power≥80%; 24 months Discovery, 12 Validation and ≥6 Qualification in §9.

**Problem / scientific failure mode:** Eighty fixed, complete nonoverlapping 120h blocks require at least 400 days before sparsity. If rolling blocks are meant, the same observations can populate many blocks and the floor ceases to count information. Block-return SD is not defined for an unequal-count event-ratio estimator. Occupancy is not independence. Thirty bp could be chosen because it makes a power calculation pass, while a smaller useful edge remains undetectable. Discovery and Validation each at 80% power do not imply 80% end-to-end success; under an illustrative independent two-stage model the product is 64% before qualification.

**Minimum repair:** Define calendar-block origin and treatment of partial blocks; distinguish occupancy/concentration from variance-based information. Use the ratio-estimator influence process or fully specified event DGPs for planning. Separate economically useful effect, planning alternative, and resulting MDE. Justify the first from a frozen cost/capital-use case without H41 means, and then choose duration using synthetic operating characteristics of the exact proposed method. Replace the universal 80-block floor with a validated support/precision envelope; keep N/D only where a documented validity role exists. Predeclare the whole pipeline's desired power and error claims. Section 10 supplies duration comparisons and an auditable selection rule, not a new numerical scientific freeze.

**Another Astra audit:** Yes. Repaired support, effect scale, horizon/cooldown and duration must be considered together, not independently stamped.

### SCV1-H03 — New inferential population requires new validation

**Exact design claim:** Report §8, lines 84–93, recommends shared-clock Studentized joint maximum, proposes L120/B10000 from H41, and excludes unsupported coordinates from formal claims. JSON `recommended_inference_design` and `inference_options_considered` leave numerical details open.

**Problem / scientific failure mode:** New state-conditioned masks, volume features, 4h/8h delayed outcomes and longer nonstationary windows change the joint estimator. A pre-outcome label-free support census is not automatically independent of returns: later prices used as features also contain earlier events' outcome prices. Zero-event/near-zero-variance handling and data-dependent family shrinkage can change coverage. A fixed-window 95% max is not valid for repeated optional looks. B=10,000 adequate for a 5% tail may be inadequate for early sequential spending tails.

**Minimum repair:** Freeze a source-bundle ratio estimator, one joint start-index array, centering, SE/replicate SE, edge weighting, RNG/version/seed, zero-draw rule, fixed K accounting, missingness policy and support-screen behavior. Validate the entire screening/selection process under joint nulls and mixed alternatives, with realistic *synthetic* persistence, tail behavior, regime durations, event overlap, feed gaps and asset dependence. No outcome-driven deletion, reseeding or method fallback. If sequential, jointly control all looks and candidates, including support eligibility at each scheduled look and terminal stopping. Keep unsupported/inference-failed reasons distinct from valid negative economic evidence.

**Another Astra audit:** Yes, including exact statistical contract and successor-specific synthetic validation. H41 acceptance remains historical, not revoked or silently extended.

### SCV1-H04 — One primary cost contract must define funding exposure

**Exact design claim:** Report §7, lines 76–80, proposes fixed C_primary comprising documented taker fees, spread/slippage and a fixed funding reserve “for any settlement crossing.” JSON `endpoint_proposal` has no numeric cost or crossing formula; formal funding source remains unavailable.

**Problem / scientific failure mode:** A universal constant, a horizon-specific constant, and a per-settlement deduction are different estimands. Settlement crossings can differ across entry hour, horizon and funding schedule. A fixed reserve is not observed cashflow or a guaranteed upper bound; it can systematically over-penalize a receiving side or understate adverse carry. Two unresolved numbers cannot be hidden in a later implementation choice. “Realistic friction” cannot be inferred from a URL template or an unpublished account fee tier.

**Minimum repair:** Choose one primary **reference-cost** interpretation, publish numeric components and dated non-private evidence, and bind fee-tier assumptions, notional convention, entry/exit spread/slippage accounting and horizon treatment. If actual funding authority stays absent, prefer an explicit nonnegative horizon reserve F_h charged by a frozen formula to every event of that horizon, labeled a planning reserve rather than actual funding. A crossing-count rule instead requires source-authorized settlement-calendar semantics and a frozen boundary-inclusion rule. Stress remains diagnostic. Unknown future fee/funding changes trigger a preregistered drift/failure policy, never retroactive cost switching. Actual all-cost or tradable-P&L claims wait for funding and executable cost evidence.

**Another Astra audit:** Yes on endpoint choice; numeric materialization may be checked mechanically only after the scientific reserve rule and evidence standard are fixed. No cost number is selected by this audit.

### SCV1-H05 — D0 needs a closed exposure ledger and continuing firewall

An obsolete H39 readiness status does not establish current non-unblinding. Unlogged tactical feedback can change filters, costs, support decisions, source exclusions or stopping while the campaign is running.

Seal an exposure inventory, roles/access rights, decision-change prohibition, stopping policy and incident handling. Quarantine all already observed fitting/outcome history; start the formal sample after the last permitted design decision and all prerequisites. Any later substantive redesign requires new independent authority and new forward start, not a “restart” using observed months. Continuing B-line collection need not cease if it cannot alter the frozen successor and candidate outcomes remain firewalled; unknowable current historical exposure must not be treated as clean.

## 4. A — Prospective thesis and H41 contamination

The new sample is intended to begin after a new freeze, and the design forbids using H41's near-positive coordinate, unsupported means or sealed validation. That materially improves the prospective posture compared with relabeling historical Discovery. The audit found **no demonstrated choice selected because a named H41/B-line result looked favorable**. It cannot certify the motivation of equations that do not yet exist.

Price continuation, failed-displacement reversal, participation and cross-asset response are plausible broad questions. BTC/ETH is supported by paired source feasibility; h4/h8 and a one-hour delay still need mechanism-specific rationale. Regime conditioning may answer a new conditional question, but it must not be a favorable historical subgroup attached to an H41 winner. The draft's explicit prohibition on BO24/D4 rescue is a policy, not mathematical evidence of non-aliasing. Require a rationale ledger naming considered formula alternatives and their non-outcome basis before the event-mask feasibility pass. No H41 effect estimate or best-coordinate identity was used in this audit's recommendations or calculations.

TRAIN is exposed development data, not pristine inference. Feature-only threshold fitting there is acceptable for a genuinely new forward test once the entire procedure is fixed. It does not demonstrate future support, stable quantiles, or adequate power. Avoid silently rolling/refitting historical 2021–2022 thresholds during the successor; a rolling unsupervised update would itself need a frozen causal algorithm and validation before D0.

## 6. D/E — Minimum roster and explicit regime multiplicity

Six mechanisms mirrored across assets are twelve coordinates, not twelve independent mechanisms. Mirroring is legitimate cross-market replication, but BTC/ETH dependence must be preserved. Different assets must not be collapsed solely because their event times coincide: target economic exposures differ.

| Pair | Main ambiguity / redundancy test |
|---|---|
| TREND | Specify directional slope and separate magnitude gate; distinguish persistence from a relabeled raw return |
| EXPAND | Must differ from TREND and volume continuation in event inputs, not just a cosmetic range label |
| REVERT | Define the failed displacement, re-entry clock and side; cannot simultaneously mean continuation of an expansion |
| VOLUME | Define the price-acceptance condition and participation normalization; volume is activity, not inherently directional signed flow |
| LEAD | Use strictly eligible lagged other-asset movement and target nonresponse; rule out contemporaneous correlation relabeled lead |
| RELATIVE | Define scale/beta normalization and convergence event; a spread opportunity does not automatically imply a profitable unhedged target leg |

**Recommended initial shortlist: 8 coordinates**—TREND, REVERT, VOLUME and **one** cross-asset mechanism, each mirrored BTC/ETH. Defer EXPAND unless its equation represents an additional thesis; choose LEAD versus RELATIVE from the scientific question and available timing semantics before any outcome examination. This is a scope recommendation, not a frozen winner. A deterministic structural audit may reduce to 6 or fewer; no slot replacement. Twelve can remain a ceiling if each additional pair earns a distinct hypothesis and adequate support. No omitted funding/OI/L2 family is scientifically necessary for this narrower price/participation/cross-asset thesis.

Define a single fixed gate G_i over the chosen regime labels for each surviving coordinate. A conjunction such as STRONG_TREND and NORMAL_VOL is one composite event rule if frozen as such. Each coordinate has **one** primary after-cost event mean. No separate formal regime, side or subperiod tests. With K surviving coordinates the primary family is K, not K×3×2×2. Regime summaries are diagnostic, with no rescue, ranking replacement or automatic next-campaign variant. TRAIN fitting must bind quantile algorithm, tie/zero/missing handling, minimum fit population and any asset normalization.

Symbolic equivalence is the strongest alias evidence. Jaccard and side agreement on exposed TRAIN are descriptive support checks, not proof of equivalence on future markets. Freeze the rule for pruning near aliases and tie-breaking between them before computing those diagnostics. Avoid selecting a numerical “low correlation” subset after multiple design iterations without recording the discarded variants. On a genuinely future test this need not add a retrospective correction for every development idea, but the final procedure and D0 must follow the last such choice.

## 7. F/G — Delayed endpoint, friction and funding

For the proposed delay, distinguish nominal signal boundary t from actual decision time d_t:

`feature bars end no later than t; d_t >= all required receipt times; d_t + margin < t+1h; entry reference Open(t+1h); exit Open(t+1h+h)`.

If this inequality fails, no event is committed. Open is a first economic mark, not a guaranteed fill at the exact nominal nanosecond. Signal-to-exit is **1+h hours**, holding horizon is **h hours**. Source/partition right-censoring must cover that delay and the actual availability of the exit mark, and freeze endpoint equality. Missing the exit mark is a data failure, not a discretionary censor. Inputs cannot include the new entry-bar Open or any revised signal bar arriving after commitment.

One hour is not mathematically required for causality. The existing native 1h reference source makes it a simple auditable convention. A post-receipt 1m/trade mark could shorten latency, but requires new source authority and a fixed timing/price functional. Same-boundary Open cannot be promoted to an executable fill merely by assuming a fast feed. Retain delayed entry in a narrow V1 if the mechanism predicts persistence over that delay; otherwise redesign the source/entry question before freeze. Do not discard a mechanism because a delayed backtest looked weak.

The primary estimand should remain an equal-entry-notional event-conditioned signed simple return less one prospectively specified reference-cost schedule. It is not capital-normalized portfolio P&L. Overlapping events can be analyzed jointly without pretending they are separate funded positions. A later policy needs position/netting, inventory, risk, funding and fill semantics; a “relative” candidate trading only one leg is not a market-neutral spread strategy.

Use dated, public or redacted source-only fee assumptions; do not inspect account credentials or infer VIP tier. Taker fees and spread/slippage reserves require unit/side/notional rules and no double-counting. A fixed funding reserve is coherent as a stated stress/planning deduction, not realized cashflow or guaranteed conservatism. F_h by horizon is simpler than pretending to know unclosed settlement crossings. If reserve choice materially determines which hypotheses could pass, that is exactly why it must be justified before Discovery. Unknown drift should be handled through a predetermined suspension/qualification policy. No primary cost number or switch is supplied here.

## 8. H — Inference family and alternative procedures

**Single-step shared-clock Studentized joint maximum is an appropriate V1 default**, conditional on new validation. It is transparent and directly supplies simultaneous LCBs for deterministic lock selection. It already exploits candidate correlation if the joint resampling is correct; it is not inherently conservative merely because two candidates are strongly correlated. Do not claim twelve independent chances to discover an effect.

For coordinate i and hour u, let A_iu indicate the immutable event and Z_iu=A_iu*Y_iu. Estimate `mu_i=sum(Z_i)/sum(A_i)`. Resample complete joint hourly bundles using the same indices for both assets and all coordinates; retain the authorized original event horizon outcome with its source anchor. Never compress away non-event hours or construct returns across artificial block joins. Account for unequal A counts, non-circular edge weighting, centering and replicate SE. Zero-event coordinates stay in the ledger with no formal positive claim; outcome-dependent inference failures cannot be hidden as exclusions.

For planning, the equal-information block influence is approximately `psi_ib=(Z_ib-mu_i*A_ib)/mean_b(A_ib)`. Its variance and serial covariance determine precision. An unweighted SD of nonempty block means generally does not. Studentization, null centering and support-screening must match this ratio estimand.

The H41 K20/T2184 implementation and its accepted synthetic grid do not certify K≤12 over a new conditioned future population. L120/B10000 are starting candidates only. Revalidate persistence beyond the horizon, regime run lengths, volume tails, clustered source failures, random support, mixed nulls, anti/correlated assets, zero counts and boundary events. Monte Carlo tolerances/intervals must be preregistered: an empirical rejection proportion below 0.05 is not proof that the true rate is ≤0.05, and 2,000 replicates have sampling uncertainty. Keep validation seeds independent of method-selection seeds; do not optimize for the quietest null result alone.

Hierarchical family gating is unnecessary for at most a few mirror pairs unless there is an independently justified family-level claim. An omnibus gate may discard a useful isolated coordinate. Step-down can reject additional hypotheses after the largest statistic passes, but for an “at least one candidate” objective its first gate is usually the same full-family max gate; it does not automatically shorten time to the first pass. Simultaneous bound inversion and ranking after step-down add complexity. The general joint-dependence/studentization basis is described by [Romano and Wolf (2005)](https://www.ssc.wisc.edu/~bhansen/718/RomanoWolf2005.pdf); applying it to this exact sparse serial setting still requires assumptions and evidence.

## 9. I/J — Support, useful effect and planning power

N protects against too few decisions, D against same-day concentration, and occupied fixed blocks against clustering across days. They overlap but are not identical. None is a universal effective sample size. Define a common UTC block origin and exclude or explicitly weight partial edge blocks. Counting every overlapping 120h start as an independent occupied block is invalid.

For fixed calendar blocks with counts n_b, report the concentration index `B_conc=(sum n_b)^2/sum(n_b^2)` and nonoverlapping event-interval packing. These are outcome-free support diagnostics, not independence proofs. Two candidates with the same N and D can have sharply different effective information. Long horizons and cooldowns change overlap; regime conditioning changes episode duration. Do not choose looser per-candidate floors to admit a favored realized support pattern.

**Recommended support policy:** A single prespecified method-adequacy contract maps each frozen horizon/gate/cooldown class and observed outcome-free mask to validated support conditions. At each authorized analysis, lock the receipt/mask before outcome statistics; carry the entire support-screen process through synthetic error/power validation. No universal N200/D120/80-block gate is endorsed without that evidence. Unsupported means are diagnostic; insufficient method information is not proof of no edge. Keep ledger slots fixed and distinguish SOURCE_FAILURE, SUPPORT_UNAVAILABLE, INFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE and valid NO_GO.

Separate three quantities:

1. **Economically useful net effect:** derived prospectively from a transparent deployment-use case, turnover/capital overlap, cost uncertainty and economic usefulness, not from inferential convenience.
2. **Planning alternative:** effect and persistence/support assumptions used in a synthetic power grid; 30bp is one scenario until independently justified.
3. **MDE:** a consequence of the design's precision and critical value at a specified power; it is not selected by naming a desired number.

The formal null may remain mu≤0 even if 30bp is the planning effect. Passing LCB>0 does not prove mu≥30bp. Requiring that stronger claim would change the null and sample requirement. Show power at a grid such as 10/20/30/50bp only as declared hypothetical alternatives, with no H41 estimates. Define whether power is per non-null coordinate, probability of any true discovery, probability of correct lock, or eventual validated-playbook success; these are different. Testing the global-null and all-positive alternatives alone is insufficient.

### Fixed-window recommendation

Use one pre-D0 decision rule: from 6/9/12/18/24 months, choose the earliest window whose **successor-specific** full-pipeline synthetic validation meets the prespecified error, adequacy and power objectives over the externally justified envelope. Freeze that duration. If none passes, reduce the scientific scope or stop before D0; do not open more variants or lengthen after inspecting results.

Under the draft's lower-noise toy case, **9 months** is the shortest tested fixed alternative reaching 80% at 30bp; 6 does not. Under the higher-noise case, **24 months** is the shortest listed alternative reaching it. These are conditional planning conclusions, not authority. The present R1 does not contain enough information to name a universally valid shortest numerical window.

### Concrete group-sequential alternative

A simple auditable alternative is frozen looks at months **6, 9, 12, 18, 24**, with per-look family error allocations:

`alpha = [0.001, 0.002, 0.002, 0.005, 0.040]`, sum=0.05.

At look j, use simultaneous one-sided bounds for the entire frozen family at alpha_j, including validated support screening. Stop and lock at most one primary passer under the already fixed ranking; stop terminal NO_GO at the final look if none passes. No return-driven timing changes, budget recycling, replacement candidates, or extension beyond the frozen maximum. Skip a look with inadequate source/support according to a fixed rule and spend/forfeit its budget as preregistered, rather than reallocating opportunistically. Data certification delays must not move sample endpoints.

If each full-family per-look procedure has strong FWER≤alpha_j, a union bound gives FWER≤0.05 over all looks without assuming independent looks. This is a conservative error-spending construction, **not** a claim that a fixed-window bootstrap automatically satisfies its premise. Preregister and validate it with the new serial data model. More efficient Lan–DeMets/O'Brien–Fleming-type calibration requires a justified joint information-time process; do not transplant clinical Brownian boundaries into sparse financial ratios without that work. [Lan and DeMets (1983)](https://eclass.uoa.gr/modules/document/file.php/MATH301/PracticalSession3/Lan_DeMets_1983.pdf) explain spending and the need to account for repeated analyses.

For a conservative normal K12 Bonferroni illustration, those boundaries are approximately 3.765/3.588/3.588/3.341/2.713. At the final look and SD100, marginal power for one 30bp coordinate is about **81.9%**; at month 6 it is only **2.5%**. At SD60, the month-9 marginal crossing probability is only **54.5%**, versus 88.5% for the draft's single-look c=2.5 scenario. These are per-look crossing probabilities, not cumulative stopping power or expected duration. Earlier looks can shorten large-effect campaigns but can be slower in expectation than a prospectively chosen nine-month fixed design. Simulate stopping-time distribution, correct-lock probability and expected calendar delay before preferring sequential monitoring.

At alpha=0.001, B=10,000 yields only about ten joint-max upper-tail draws. B and quantile uncertainty must therefore be revalidated/increased prospectively; a convenient seed is not precision. The 95% H41 bootstrap budget cannot be copied unchanged into this design.
