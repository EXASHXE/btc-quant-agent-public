     - **Total = 168 Slots**: Global research budget fully consumed and non-recyclable.
2. **Deterministic Semantic Feature & Governance Contracts**:
   - `H40FeatureContract`: Immutably specifies feature ID, lookback, strict closed-bar causality (`close_time < decision_t`), product scope, cross-asset/funding authority, and content hash.

3. **Hard Immutability & Hash Identity**:
   - `H40ConfigurationSlot.config_id` is computed over all semantic fields excluding `slot_index`, ensuring duplicate semantic configurations yield identical `config_id` and strictly trigger `CONFIG_IDENTITY_CONFLICT` upon duplicate registration.
   - All nested parameters are frozen into immutable tuples and mappings.
   - Performance field poisoning defense (`FORBIDDEN_PERF_KEYS`) actively rejects any outcome, label, return, MFE/MAE, or backtest metrics.

### 2.1 Budget Allocation and Distribution
The search budget of 168 slots is global across all assets and horizons. Under `H40_PROTOCOL_V1_R2`, the distribution is:

### 2.2 Direction Variants (10 Variants across 5 Families)
1. **`D1_TREND_CONTINUATION`**:
   - `D1_V1_RETURN_4H`: 4h closed-bar log return trend continuation.

   - `D2_V1_BREAKOUT_24H`: 24h Donchian channel breakout continuation.

3. **`D3_FAILED_MOVE_REVERSAL`**:
   - `D3_V1_FAILED_BREAK_24H`: 24h failed breakout and close-back mean reversion.

### 2.3 Depth-Two Pairs (4 Authorized Pairs)
1. **`PAIR_D1_D4_CONFIRM_4H`** (`D1 + D4`): D1 trend continuation confirmed by D4 cross-asset alignment (veto on cross-asset conflict).

4. **`PAIR_D3_D5_REVERSAL_FUNDING_24H`** (`D3 + D5`): D3 failed move reversal filtered by D5 funding crowding.

They are never silently upgraded, dropped, or replaced.

## 3. Semantic Contracts

### 3.1 Feature Contracts (`H40FeatureContract`)
- **Strict Closed-Bar Causality**: `closed_bar_rule` strictly enforces `<` (`bar_close_time < decision_t`) and rejects `<=` or future timestamps with `H40ReasonCode.PIT_UNAVAILABLE`.
- **Protected Surface Defense**: Any feature referencing labels, forward returns, MFE/MAE excursions, H39 results, Final Holdout, or unversioned historical research results raises `H40ReasonCode.FEATURE_CORRUPTED`.
- **Directional Ownership Defense**:
  - D5 without a base directional family in `{D1, D2, D3}` is rejected with `UNAUTHORIZED_FAMILY`.
  - TP/BR cannot supply direction; TP/BR is strictly magnitude/opportunity evidence only (`TPBR_DIRECTION_DISALLOWED`).

### 3.2 Geometry & Calibration Contracts
- **`H40GeometryContract`**:
  - Screening cost proxy: $c_{\text{rt}} = 0.0012$.
  - Median MFE gate: $\ge 1.5 \times c_{\text{rt}} = 0.0018$.
  - Ratio gate: $\text{MFE} / \max(\text{MAE}, c_{\text{rt}}) \ge 1.25$.
  - Cell sample floor: $\ge 30$ observations in training estimation cell.
  - Zero MFE/MAE calculation occurs in P2.
- **`H40CalibrationContract`**:
  - Allowed methods: `PLATT_LOGISTIC`, `ISOTONIC_IF_ELIGIBLE`.
  - Isotonic eligibility: $\ge 200$ calibration observations total, $\ge 40$ per predicted side; fallback to `PLATT_LOGISTIC` if ineligible.
  - Action thresholds: Strictly restricted to $\{0.55, 0.60, 0.65\}$; any other threshold is rejected.
  - Zero calibrators fitted in P2.

### 3.3 Hash Preimages & Immutability
- `H40ConfigurationSlot.config_id`: SHA-256 computed over canonical JSON of all semantic fields:
  ```text
  scope, primary_horizon, direction_family, direction_variant,
  regime_contract_id, opportunity_contract_id, direction_contract_id,
  geometry_contract_id, calibration_contract_id, action_threshold,
  abstention_policy, required_feature_ids, feature_params,
  lookback_windows, availability_rule, cost_proxy_id,
  mechanical_refit_policy, protocol_hash, source_manifest_hash,
  split_manifest_hash, ledger_schema_version, frozen_kernel_sha,
  p1_code_baseline_sha
  ```
- `slot_hash`: SHA-256 over `slot_index` + `config_id`.
- `ledger_hash`: SHA-256 over sorted list of all 168 `slot_hash` values.
- Full round-trip serialization (`to_dict` / `from_dict`) verified to preserve exact hashes and immutability.

## 7. Protocol Alignment Note: R3 Repair Prompt

During P2 implementation, a docs prompt [`prompts/v0.5/research/v0.5.1_H40R3_EXACT_P2_GRID_AND_FEATURE_SEMANTICS_PREREGISTRATION_REPAIR.md`](file:///root/workspace/project/Quant-agent/prompts/v0.5/research/v0.5.1_H40R3_EXACT_P2_GRID_AND_FEATURE_SEMANTICS_PREREGISTRATION_REPAIR.md) was registered in commit `18932b91fda432faa435da955102885bea40e9cd`.

The prompt observes that while `H40_PROTOCOL_V1_R2` bounds family counts, variant counts, lookback sets, and pair families, the exact 12-configuration assignment per variant could introduce unpreregistered degrees of freedom if chosen arbitrarily.

In this P2 implementation, the 168-row grid was constructed using a strictly symmetric, uniform grid:
1. Symmetrically allocating 3 horizons (`4h`, `8h`, `12h`) × 3 scopes (`BTC_ONLY`, `ETH_ONLY`, `POOLED_BTC_ETH`) at threshold `0.55` and Platt logistic calibration (slots 0..8);
2. Symmetrically allocating 3 diagnostic/higher-threshold variations across horizons (`4h/0.60/Isotonic`, `8h/0.65/Platt`, `12h/0.60/Isotonic`) on `POOLED_BTC_ETH` (slots 9..11);
3. Replicating this identical template across all 10 single-family variants and all 4 depth-two pairs.

Independent review by Sol High should assess whether this constructive symmetric materialization fully satisfies pre-outcome determinism or whether it should be formally codified in an R3 protocol amendment (`V0.5.1_H40_PROTOCOL_AMENDMENT_R3.md`).
