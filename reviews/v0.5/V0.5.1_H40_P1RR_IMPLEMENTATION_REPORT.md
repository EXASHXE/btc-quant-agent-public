## 3. Detailed Remediation of Finding P1R-F1 (HIGH)

### 3.1 Elimination of Forgeable Split Paths

Previously, `H40SplitManifest.build_materialized` accepted raw timestamp arrays and returned `is_authoritative=True` without cryptographic linkage to verified source records. Furthermore, `assert_authoritative()` could be invoked without arguments, accepting any split manifest whose boolean flag was `True`.

**Changes implemented**:
1. **`build_materialized` Downgraded to Non-Authoritative**:
   - `H40SplitManifest.build_materialized(...)` accepting caller timestamp arrays now unconditionally sets `is_authoritative = False` and `attestation = None`.
   - Raw caller arrays cannot confer authoritative split status under H40 protocol authority.
2. **Strict Authoritative Builder (`materialize_authoritative`)**:
   - Requires `protocol_identity_hash`, a verified `H40SourceManifest`, and `repo_root`.
   - Verifies that BTC and ETH source records exist, have `status == H40SourceStatus.VERIFIED`, and contain verified `H40SourceValidationReceipt` objects.
   - Extracts timestamps from local disk artifacts through `extract_verified_source_timestamps()`.
   - Constructs and signs an immutable cryptographic `H40SplitAttestation`.
3. **Cryptographic Split Attestation (`H40SplitAttestation`)**:
   - Binds:
     - `protocol_identity_hash`
     - `source_manifest_hash`
     - `split_hash` (canonical SHA-256 over unattested partition structure)
     - `btc_source_id`, `btc_locator`, `btc_file_sha256`, `btc_membership_sha256`, `btc_timestamp_count`
     - `eth_source_id`, `eth_locator`, `eth_file_sha256`, `eth_membership_sha256`, `eth_timestamp_count`
     - `attestation_hash` (RFC-8259 canonical SHA-256 self-hash over attestation payload).
4. **Rigorous Authority Assertion (`assert_authoritative`)**:
   - `assert_authoritative(source_manifest: H40SourceManifest, repo_root: Path | str | None = None)`:
     - Fails closed with `SOURCE_UNVERIFIED` if `is_authoritative` is False, `attestation` is None, or `source_manifest` is None.
     - Verifies attestation self-hash and partition `split_hash`.
     - Cross-validates attestation hashes against the supplied verified `H40SourceManifest`.
     - When `repo_root` is provided, performs full cold validation on disk artifacts, ensuring bytes and timestamps have not drifted or been tampered with.
