1. **Family-specific support semantics**
   - Trend continuation, breakout continuation, and failed-move reversal must not automatically share the same mandatory support conjunction.
   - Support filters may be family-specific, stratification variables, or abstention gates, but the choice must be frozen before outcomes.

2. **Calibration/testability contract**
   - Separate `NOT_TESTABLE` from weak edge.
   - Avoid calibration methods whose minimum support is structurally incompatible with sparse/binary feature families.
   - Do not choose an alternative calibrator merely because it would rescue the observed M3B data.

3. **Family availability semantics**
   - The all-members family-completeness rule prevented the sole complete D1 fit from entering scientific evaluation.
   - A successor may retain this conservative behavior or preregister an alternative that accounts for unavailable members without survivor selection.
   - This choice must be prospective.

4. **Information-set expansion**
   - After scientific semantics are fixed, prioritize new independent information rather than hundreds of threshold variants.
   - Baseline R2 ordering remains useful:
     - trend/cross-asset BTC + D4,
     - derivatives positioning/crowding D5 and related PIT-authorized signals,
     - microstructure primarily for execution timing / filtering.

5. **B-line isolation**
   - Tactical MarketWatch can supply hypothesis ideas and forward evidence engineering patterns.
   - Its heuristic thresholds or observed shadow outcomes must not be imported into the successor scientific campaign as post-hoc frozen parameters.
