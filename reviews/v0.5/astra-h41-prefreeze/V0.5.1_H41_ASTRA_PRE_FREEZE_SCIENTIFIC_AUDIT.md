## 2. Scientific Strengths

- Economic mean return is more aligned with asymmetric payoffs than imposing a win-rate gate.
- Calibration availability is separated from directional testability. Binary or sparse event signals need not satisfy a continuous-score positive-slope Platt fit merely to enter research.
- Family-specific events describe recognizable hypotheses. Removing universal volatility/range gates avoids silently testing a different intersection of hypotheses.
- The ledger is small, symmetric across BTC and ETH, and forbids recycling slots or deleting losses.
- One primary endpoint avoids unnecessary co-primary constraints. Secondary diagnostics cannot rescue failures.
- A jointly calibrated simultaneous lower bound can support deterministic selection from a fixed family without a second selection penalty for choosing among covered coordinates.
- Candidate Lock is explicitly upstream of validation and playbook qualification. Source authority and B-line separation are recognized prerequisites.

None of these strengths proves statistical power, independence of support selection, or generalization to a new period.

## 3. Blocking Issues

### H41-B01 — Historical outcome exposure survives campaign renaming

**Current proposed rule:** Sections 1, 4, and 17 (lines 21–44, 93–142, 609–628) redesign after H40 and reuse WF1_CALIBRATION while calling H41 prospective and correcting across the H41 testable universe only.

**Why it fails or is weak:** R1, “Independent Controller verification” and “Scientific interpretation,” publicly reports positive-slope calibration failures, degeneracy, and a completed fit. Positive-slope improvement and label degeneracy are outcome-dependent information, even without a published P&L. The design follows those observations. H40 being NOT_TESTABLE is not evidence that its data remained statistically unobserved. BTC addition does not make correlated ETH/BTC history a fresh sample. New hashes preserve provenance; they do not restore statistical independence.

This is a contamination **risk**, not an accusation that Q80 or any other number was deliberately optimized. The provided documents do not contain a complete exposure log or show how each H41 value was selected. A nominal 95% simultaneous procedure covering 20 fixed H41 hypotheses does not also cover unknown adaptive choices across H40 and H41. Merely counting H40 plus H41 slots would not reconstruct that adaptive search. The distinction follows established adaptive-data-analysis results; see [Dwork et al., Generalization in Adaptive Data Analysis and Holdout Reuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629).

**Minimum required revision:** Prefer the least disruptive route: classify reused WF1_CALIBRATION as **historically informed exploratory Discovery**; report its adjusted statistics as exploratory and do not call them confirmatory 5% FWER evidence. A lock may be a frozen *directional research candidate* only. Preregister the next genuinely unexposed, separately authorized validation and policy-qualification sequence before opening it. If H41 insists on a formal first-use Discovery claim, Controller must instead establish a genuinely unexposed H41 sample or accept a defensible selective-inference design and exposure accounting. This audit allocates no protected partition and does not authorize either route.

**Candidate universe change:** No. **Statistical authority change:** Yes—data role, claim, exposure accounting, and later confirmation budget.

**Required regression/evidence test:** A signed exposure inventory with released artifact types and chronology, parameter-rationale ledger, immutable discovery-status label, and a lifecycle test preventing exploratory evidence from being presented as confirmation. No raw historical outcomes are required for the inventory.

### H41-B02 — “No label API read” is not outcome-independent screening

**Current proposed rule:** Sections 8, 14–16 (lines 305–357, 537–607) allow a full-calibration event census before any calibration return/label is read, remove untestable candidates from multiplicity, and describe this as preventing survivor selection.

**Why it fails or is weak:** Every event may be causally valid at its own timestamp, yet the full event calendar depends on the entire price path. A price used to form a feature at a later time can also be the exit price of an earlier event. Across a 91-day window, a process inspecting all OHLC feature inputs has observed many earlier events' outcome-bearing prices, even if no function called `future_return` was invoked. Event counts, gaps, and clustered support need not be ancillary to the test statistic. A receipt created before a result file establishes workflow order, not statistical independence.

**Minimum required revision:** Replace the impossible blanket interpretation with two explicit contracts: (a) a sealed feature/event processor may read only causally permitted data per decision and may emit a precommitted support receipt, without exposing prices, labels, P&L, or ranking to the designer; (b) the complete fixed screening-and-testing rule must have a justified inference model. Keep all 20 semantic hypotheses in the audit ledger. Do not claim that a random screened subset automatically inherits fixed-family coverage. Validate screening as part of the statistical procedure; use fixed-20 Bonferroni marginal bounds as a fallback only if the marginal tests themselves are valid under the event process. A fixed denominator alone does not repair invalid marginal inference or B01.

Freeze support, horizon availability, and missing-data masks before result calculation. An unexpected post-receipt missing outcome is a data/inference failure, not permission to omit that event or shrink the family. Real source failure and zero testable candidates must remain distinguishable from measured EDGE_FAIL.

**Candidate universe change:** No. **Statistical authority change:** Yes—screening, access semantics, missingness, and family accounting.

**Required regression/evidence test:** Synthetic sequential-price examples in which later features share earlier outcome prices; verify per-event causality and prohibited output separation. Under preregistered null processes, assess FWER of the entire support-screen-plus-inference procedure, including candidates near support cutoffs, outcome-correlated missingness, and cross-asset clusters. A same-event future perturbation must not change that event's features; it may legitimately change later events.

**Minimum revision:** Freeze one **single-step standardized joint maximum** procedure, or another fully specified method justified before outcomes. Use a shared UTC-hour resampling index for all assets and all candidate coordinates, preserving event masks and source-anchored outcome bundles. Define ratio-of-sums estimation, centering, SE estimation, bootstrap centering at finite-sample expected weights where needed, finite-replicate quantile convention, terminal truncation, zero-count/zero-SE handling, and deterministic RNG/version identity. Freeze the block rule using analytic checks and preregistered synthetic null/power evidence; do not choose L from H41 LCBs. Section 9 below gives a concrete formulation and a fixed-family fallback. Unknown inference failures must not become removed candidates.

**Candidate universe change:** No. **Statistical authority change:** Yes.

**Required regression/evidence:** Full-pipeline null FWER and simultaneous coverage checks with mixed true/false nulls, correlated assets, sparse masks, overlap, heavy tails with finite variance, volatility persistence, and near-cutoff support. ### H41-H03 — Support minima do not certify power or valid asymptotics

**Current rule:** Section 8, lines 338–355: 60 events and 30 occupied days for every candidate.

**Why weak:** Many events can be overlapping copies of a few episodes. Thirty days may be consecutive, heavily imbalanced, or linked by a persistent market state. These thresholds do not bound long-run variance, tail risk, effective sample size, or MDE. Conversely, arbitrarily increasing every threshold could extinguish the reversal hypothesis without improving the study.

**Minimum revision:** Retain 60/30 only as a provisional operational floor. Add outcome-free support diagnostics (horizon-interval overlap, nonoverlapping-event packing, per-day/per-block concentration, calendar span) and an independently justified method-adequacy rule. Before real access, specify a minimum economically relevant net effect and a volatility/dependence stress envelope using external assumptions or synthetic models, not H40/H41 measured effects. Evaluate power and coverage over that envelope. Freeze an adequate duration/method/support rule, or explicitly label sparse candidates exploratory/inference-unavailable without making a scientific no-edge claim. Do not select lower thresholds because observed support just misses them.

**Candidate universe change:** No. **Statistical authority change:** Yes—testability/adequacy and failure taxonomy.

**Required regression/evidence:** Synthetic supports with identical N/days but different concentration, overlap, and serial persistence; distinguish their adequacy. Power curves with stated effect/variance assumptions and confidence intervals for simulated error rates. All-untestable and numerical-inference-failure runs must differ from all-testable EDGE_FAIL runs.

**Why weak:** USD-M perpetual funding, spread, slippage, latency, and overlapping position financing are not modeled. Passive/side-matched exposure comparisons can be preregistered diagnostics; do not turn them into unbudgeted rescue tests. Actual all-cost claims would instead require changing the endpoint and source authority prospectively.

**Required regression/evidence:** Synthetic long/short cash-flow checks; one round-trip subtraction; exact 12bp difference between base/stress means; entry timing; overlapping events showing event mean differs from a funded portfolio return. ## 6. Testability Contract Review

The prohibition on label balance, fit convergence, precision, expectancy, p-values and LCBs deciding *scientific availability* is sound. Failure to reject is EDGE_FAIL, not NOT_TESTABLE. Calibration failure is CALIBRATION_UNAVAILABLE.

Freeze three distinct artifacts and reasons:

1. **Source/feature support:** deterministic event-generation and horizon-availability masks; no event-level outcomes or outcome summaries in the designer-visible receipt.
2. **Method adequacy:** a fixed, outcome-free support screen plus preregistered evidence that the inference procedure works for that support class. This is not a fit-convergence gate.
3. **Outcome inference:** all previously eligible events enter; numerical failures fail the run closed as INFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE rather than silently deleting the affected candidate. Keep its ledger slot.

Materialize `H41_TESTABILITY_RECEIPT_V1` before outcome evidence; bind ledger, source bytes/rows, split, Q80 artifacts, event-mask digest, method contract and run identity. The processor's authorized feature reads must be explicitly separated from human/designer visibility. Do not promise that the physical OHLC bytes are globally outcome-free.

Predeclare censoring from the known right boundary before counting support. An event whose exit would cross the boundary never qualifies. Intrawindow gaps need a deterministic presence-only policy; market-dependent missingness can bias economic inference and must be assessed, not merely hashed. No “only complete cases after observing returns” shortcut.

## 8. Primary Endpoint / Cost Review

**Keep the event-level arithmetic mean.** It is compatible with asymmetric payoff distributions and answers an interpretable event-study question. The short return is `-(P_exit/P_entry-1)` for equal entry notional in a linear contract; it is not `exp(-r_h)-1`. Charge the round-trip proxy once per emitted event.

The mean is tail-sensitive. Do not quietly winsorize, trim, remove “outliers,” or switch to the median after viewing outcomes. Require finite valid source values and a prospective tail/dependence stress analysis for uncertainty. A descriptive tail-contribution analysis can explain fragility without changing the primary decision. No method promises distribution-free confidence for an unbounded mean from a tiny sample.

Overlapping event returns estimate an event-conditioned mean, not a tradable equity curve. Correlation-aware uncertainty addresses repeated information; it does not supply capital, prevent contradictory simultaneous positions, or determine a portfolio return. Different horizons can be compared under this event estimand, but the resulting winner is not the best return-per-hour or capital-efficiency policy.

Keep 12bp/24bp as named base/stress proxies. No current venue fee tier, spread, slippage, or funding estimate was fetched; none is claimed. Stress positivity need not be a new gate when the question is explicitly 12bp-proxy edge. With the same event set, `mean(net24)=mean(net12)-0.0012`; it is not an independent replication. Under translation-equivariant inference, its LCB shifts by the same amount. Requiring positive stress LCB would change the tested economic hurdle to 24bp, which must be a deliberate prospective objective, not added conservatism for its own sake.

## 9. Dependence / Multiple-Testing Review

Max-T is not intrinsically invalid for unequal counts. The resampling unit and estimand must match. [Romano and Wolf (2005)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2005.00615.x) describe asymptotic familywise control using joint dependence and discuss studentization and power. Those results are not a certificate for an unspecified sparse event bootstrap.

### Concrete proposed formulation, subject to pre-freeze validation

For each source hour u and fixed candidate i, store a mask `A[i,u]` and, only for an event with authorized complete support, return `Y[i,u]`. Let `Z[i,u]=A[i,u]*Y[i,u]`, with zero accounting contribution at non-events (not a zero-return trade). Estimate `mu[i]=sum(Z[i,u])/sum(A[i,u])`.

Resample the joint hourly vector of masks and source-anchored event/outcome bundles using one shared list of non-circular block indices. Use `mu*[i]=sum(w[u]*Z[i,u])/sum(w[u]*A[i,u])`. This preserves unequal candidate counts and contemporaneous/cross-lag asset dependence inside blocks. Never rebuild a horizon across a synthetic block join. An original event's already authorized outcome stays attached even when its exit lies beyond the selected block; it must still lie within the actual authorized source/partition boundary.

Freeze a consistent long-run SE rule for the ratio estimator, including the role of the influence series `A[i,u]*(Y[i,u]-mu[i])/mean(A[i,u])`. One feasible single-step design standardizes bootstrap estimation errors by a consistently estimated original SE: `T*[i]=(mu*[i]-center[i])/SE[i]`; the 95th percentile of `max_i T*[i]` gives `LCB[i]=mu[i]-c95*SE[i]`. Specify `center[i]` to account for the actual resampling weights and non-circular edge bias. A bootstrap-t variant with reestimated replicate SE is also possible, but cannot remain an open implementation option. Invalid/zero-count replicates require a fixed conservative rule or run-level failure; never redraw until a favorable valid sample appears.

This is a proposed algorithm skeleton, not a completed calibration theorem. Freeze exact weighting, centering, studentization, finite-replicate quantile, support-selection treatment, RNG/seed preimage, array shape/order, numerical precision and replay version after synthetic verification. Retain the fixed 20-ID family ledger. If valid joint support-screen inference cannot be established, a concrete fallback is one-sided marginal block-bootstrap bounds at `1-0.05/20=0.9975`, with untestable slots ineligible and a fixed Bonferroni budget. That fallback still requires valid marginal event-process inference and does not cure historical data reuse. It must be chosen before outcomes, not after seeing which method passes.

### Why L=24 cannot be accepted solely because h<=12

For a transparent analytical check, suppose hourly increments are independent with variance sigma² and every hour emits a fixed-side h-hour *linearized* return. Its lag-k covariance is `(h-k)*sigma²` for k<h. A length-L block bootstrap has an approximate Bartlett taper `1-k/L`. Ignoring finite-window edges, the resulting long-run variance ratio is:

`estimated / true = 1 - (h²-1)/(3*L*h)`.

At L=24 the ratios are **0.9479 (h=4), 0.8906 (h=8), 0.8345 (h=12)**. This is an analytical toy check, not a measured H41 bias and not the exact event estimator. It shows why “block longer than horizon” is insufficient even before event clustering. Fixed-length block choices involve a bias/variance tradeoff. Classical block-bootstrap consistency is asymptotic under dependence conditions; see [Künsch, 1989](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176347265).

Test predeclared candidate block rules on synthetic processes with persistent volatility, clustered events and asset dependence. Choose using coverage and power constraints fixed before that exercise, not using H41 returns. Do not automatically demand a week-long block: only 13 nominal weeks fit in this window. A separate post-hoc sensitivity pass cannot repair the primary rule; sensitivity outcomes must not rescue a failed primary result.

10,000 joint replicates are a reasonable initial Monte Carlo budget. A 95% empirical CDF tail has binomial standard error about 0.00218 in probability scale; this is not an LCB standard error. Freeze the quantile/tie convention and no-reseed rule. The Bonferroni fallback's 0.25% tail has only about 25 exceedances at this budget, so it may need a larger prospectively fixed simulation count.

## 10. Sparse-Event / Power Review

The public partition constants at implementation SHA `407dabc…`, `src/btc_quant_agent/h40/split_manifest.py:44–49`, define WF1_CALIBRATION as `[2022-11-01, 2023-01-31)` UTC: **91 days / 2,184 hours**. These are interval metadata, not observations.

Under a continuous stable score distribution with complete hourly coverage, a Q80 rule suggests approximately `0.2*T=436.8` events before horizon censoring, roughly 434–436 after a simple h-hour right trim. These are planning approximations; frozen TRAIN quantiles and Discovery regime shifts can produce far fewer or more. D4 partitions the synchronized subset; it does not double its information. With no zeros/missingness, confirm and diverge counts sum to the parent count. Correlated assets can make divergence substantially rarer; no empirical frequency is assumed.

There is no distribution-free expected breakout or failed-break count. As an explicitly unrealistic exchangeable-level illustration, the chance that a new value is either extreme among W+1 values is `2/(W+1)`, implying about 175 events for W=24 and 60 for W=72 over 2,184 hours. Crypto prices are serially dependent and Donchian high/low/close definitions differ from this model; these numbers are not forecasts or threshold evidence. A failed break additionally requires immediate re-entry; its rate cannot be inferred from the draft. H40 D3's zero filtered fit population does not imply zero raw H41 reversal events.

### MDE illustrations, derived without market observations

For independent Gaussian candidate means, one-sided Bonferroni across 20 gives `z(0.9975)=2.807`; approximate 80% power requires:

`net MDE ~= (2.807+0.842)*sigma_event/sqrt(n_eff) = 3.649*sigma_event/sqrt(n_eff)`.

This is a planning yardstick; correlated max-T can have a smaller critical value, while dependence/heavy tails can make this approximation optimistic. With perfect cross-candidate dependence the normal critical value approaches 1.645, whereas independent 20-way maxima are close to 2.8. Correlation should be modeled rather than charging 20 independent tests mechanically when valid joint inference is available.

These supply a useful stress grid, not a market volatility assertion.

At event SD=100bp, 20bp net edge needs approximately **333 independent-equivalent events** for 80% power under the Bonferroni yardstick; 10bp needs about **1,332**. At n_eff=60, a 47.1bp net MDE means approximately 59.1bp gross after adding the 12bp hurdle. Cost is economically consequential even when small relative to individual-return volatility. A 12bp cost is 24% of a hypothetical 50bp event SD and 6% of a 200bp SD; a 24bp proxy doubles those ratios.

### Prospective adequacy rule

Retain 60 events / 30 days as provisional basic support only. Before freeze, Controller must name a decision-relevant net effect delta, desired power (80% is a reasonable planning convention), and externally justified variance/dependence envelope. Use `ceil((3.649*sigma_star/delta)²)` as an independent-equivalent planning target, then validate the actual event estimator with full synthetic serial/cross-asset dependence and the support screen. Do not equate raw N with that target or make observed effect size a gate. If the fixed duration cannot support useful inference for a family, declare the limitation prospectively; lengthen only a newly authorized sample, or keep that family exploratory. Do not relax its p-value because it is sparse.

No new numerical support threshold, block length, or volatility estimate is frozen by this audit. Choosing such values without the required evidence would recreate the researcher's discretion being audited.

## 11. Cross-Asset D4 Review

CONFIRM and DIVERGE are coherent conditional versions of a base hypothesis. They are not standalone directions and should not flip the base side. Zero other-asset sign or unavailable synchronization produces no D4 event. Both assets must share the same decision clock and strictly eligible availability cutoff; no forward-fill from a future record, nearest-timestamp matching, or revised archive state.

Require D4 RET4 to inherit the **entire** Q80 parent. Otherwise “BTC RET4” could silently mean all nonzero returns and add a new hypothesis. For every synchronized base event with nonzero other sign, exactly one of confirm/diverge must hold. Verify mutual exclusion, conservation of membership, and inherited horizon/side/entry rules.

Conditional profitability is not a proof that the modifier improves the base or that cross-asset information is independent. The two assets can share the same market factor. Parent and children are highly dependent; preserve that dependence in resampling. Keep the current eight slots and narrow the claim. An incremental interaction or child-minus-parent test would be a different primary question requiring explicit support alignment and a new multiplicity budget; it is not required to answer V1's narrower event-edge question.

## 12. Calibration Separation Review

Making calibration secondary is scientifically appropriate. A direction rule already determines sign and does not need a probability estimate to measure equal-notional event expectancy. A failed probability model must not invalidate an observed directional event population.

For optional TRAIN calibration, require every training label to mature within authorized TRAIN label support, with boundary purge and no calibration-window leakage. Fit only on the appropriate event population. D2/D3 calibration should state whether it estimates up probability, directional success, or another quantity; Beta-Binomial smoothing is not a universal probability-calibration guarantee under serial dependence.

Comparing Platt versus isotonic on WF1_CALIBRATION and carrying the winner into policy is a new selection operation even if it does not affect Candidate Lock. Best V1 scope: omit optional calibration from the formal lock path entirely, or freeze one descriptive profile per family and mark it non-authoritative for sizing. Later policy calibration must have its own training/evaluation plan and cannot inherit the directional LCB as probability validation.

## 17. Residual Risks

- A 91-day period may be too short for small net edges, especially sparse reversals and divergent cross-asset subsets. No resampling method manufactures independent market regimes.
- Strong nonstationarity, very heavy tails, or informative data gaps can defeat a locally valid stationary-block approximation. Synthetic success covers specified scenarios, not every crypto process.
- A positive event mean may be transient market exposure and may disappear after funding, capacity, overlapping positions or execution delay.
- Diagnostics and later playbook engineering can reopen the search space unless their data budgets and decision rights are frozen separately.
- An exposure inventory cannot erase known history. Subsequent genuinely unexposed evidence is the practical safeguard for adaptively developed hypotheses.
