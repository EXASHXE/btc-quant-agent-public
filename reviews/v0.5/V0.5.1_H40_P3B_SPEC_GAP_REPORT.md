## Missing accepted authority

The candidate-lock **scientific coverage hard gate** has the accepted interval `[0.25%, 10.0%]` and coverage is defined as accepted LONG/SHORT decisions divided by base-eligible decisions. The accepted documents do not uniquely specify which candidate-lock population must be tested against this interval:

1. WF1 Training proposed-side A..F rows before probability calibration and confidence threshold;
2. WF1 Calibration final calibrated LONG/SHORT actions;
3. both populations as separate coverage gates.

These choices can give different gate results for the same sealed candidate. A candidate may emit many proposed sides during training but few final calibrated trades, or the reverse. Selecting one option would change the representative universe and potentially the sole locked candidate. It cannot be resolved by treating the Discovery *ranking* partition as a definition of every hard-gate population.
