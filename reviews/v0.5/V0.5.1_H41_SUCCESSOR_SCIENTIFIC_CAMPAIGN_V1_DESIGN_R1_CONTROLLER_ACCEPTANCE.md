The design correctly:

1. preserves H41 as immutable terminal `NO_GO`;
2. avoids rescuing H41 near-positive descriptive coordinates;
3. keeps H41 WF1_VALIDATION sealed;
4. refuses to promote OI, funding, mark/index/premium, aggTrade or L2 without new source authority;
5. limits V1 to a compact deterministic regime-conditioned architecture with at most 12 proposed primary coordinates;
6. distinguishes historical TRAIN/development exposure from future forward Discovery/Validation;
7. treats the roster as `PROPOSED_NOT_FROZEN`;
8. requires a new forward source extension/parity contract before scientific use;
9. retains explicit multiple-testing control and pre-outcome synthetic power validation;
10. separates formal gates from diagnostics;
11. contains no successor outcome access and no production-code mutation.

## Controller concerns for independent audit

### C1 — Research latency / fixed-window duration

The proposed lifecycle implies roughly:

```text
24 months Discovery
+ 12 months Validation
+ >=6 months Qualification
```

before a fully qualified Playbook.

Astra must determine whether this duration is actually required by the declared
power/support target, or whether a shorter prospectively frozen design can
retain valid FWER/power and independence.

The audit should compare fixed-window alternatives such as 6/9/12/18/24 months
and may discuss a rigorously preregistered group-sequential alternative if it
preserves error control and cannot become post-outcome optional stopping.

No duration may be shortened merely for convenience.

### C2 — Proposed support/power gates

`N>=200`, `D>=120`, `>=80` occupied 120h blocks, and the 30bp after-cost
effect scale are not yet scientifically justified authority.

Astra must distinguish:

- formal adequacy requirement;
- heuristic safety margin;
- synthetic design assumption;
- economically meaningful effect target.

### C3 — Source transition semantics

Historical accepted kline authority ends in 2023, while the successor is
prospective.

The design proposes a new exact-byte forward extension plus receive-time parity.
Astra must determine whether this contract is coherent and sufficient, especially
because historical exchange archives do not prove local receive latency.

### C4 — Entry-reference semantics

The proposed event decision at boundary `t` uses completed bars through
`[t-1h,t)` and delays reference entry to `Open(t+1h)`.

This is causally conservative but may alter the economic mechanism by adding a
full-hour delay.

Astra must determine whether the reference is scientifically defensible,
playbook-relevant, and consistently separable from the feature path.

### C5 — Hidden multiplicity through regimes

The proposed volatility/trend/participation labels must not implicitly multiply
12 formal candidates into a large post-hoc regime grid.

Astra must require one explicit regime/candidate mapping and a clear count of
formal hypotheses entering FWER control.

### C6 — Candidate specificity before freeze

Design R1 intentionally leaves exact formulas, thresholds, support aliasing and
cooldown unresolved.

Astra must determine whether these can be closed mechanically in the next
preregistration/freeze stage or whether a Design R1.1 repair is required first.

### C7 — Forward exposure quarantine

H39, H35/H36 and B-line observations overlap the current forward era.

Astra must determine what exact timestamped exposure ledger/quarantine is
required so the successor's forward Discovery and Validation remain credible.
