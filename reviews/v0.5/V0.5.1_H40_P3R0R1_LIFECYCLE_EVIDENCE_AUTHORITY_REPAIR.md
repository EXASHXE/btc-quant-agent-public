## 3. Canonicalization and receipt rules

Every object marked `CANONICAL CHILD PREIMAGE` below is normative in full. The
hash is repository `canonical_sha256`: recursively JSON-normalize, reject
non-string object keys and NaN/Infinity, serialize UTF-8 with
`ensure_ascii=False`, `allow_nan=False`, `sort_keys=True`, and
`separators=(",", ":")`, then SHA-256 the bytes.

Type strings and verifier-rule strings inside a child object are semantic
content. Receipt/evidence objects reject missing or extra fields. A receipt's
`receipt_sha256` is the canonical hash of exactly the named receipt fields and
is stored in an envelope, never inside its own preimage. Evidence uses the same
rule with `evidence_sha256` outside its preimage. A path is only a locator; the
referenced bytes must canonicalize and hash to the bound content hash.

`audit_timestamp_utc` has one representation only: exactly 20 ASCII characters
matching `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ`, with a calendar-valid Gregorian date, 24-hour
time, no leap second, no fractional seconds, and the literal `Z`. Parsers must
reject offsets and non-canonical equivalent spellings. Timestamps are hash-bound
audit metadata but never authorization-critical: no ordering, freshness,
future/stale, or wall-clock-tolerance test may authorize or reject a transition.
Authorization derives only from identities, evidence, lineage, and verifier
results.

## 6. Deterministic verifier and failure obligations

1. Discovery entry verifies the accepted governance and implementation
   authorities, recomputes `run_authority_id`, and seals the roster before any
   outcome access.
2. Candidate result evidence contains the complete sealed REGISTERED universe.
   The verifier loads all content-addressed input evidence, applies the accepted
   `DISCOVERY_SELECTION_V1` hash, derives gates/corrections/order, and only then
   constructs the candidate-lock authorization receipt.
3. Within-family correction universe is the REGISTERED rows of that family in
   the sealed snapshot. PRECISION and NET_EXPECTANCY max-statistic corrections
   are separate. Cross-family Holm applies to family representatives.
4. Final candidate order is exactly: all hard gates; highest adjusted LCB net
   expectancy; highest adjusted LCB precision; lower complexity; ascending
   canonical structural configuration hash/configuration ID. No runner-up may
   be promoted.
5. WF evidence contains the exact candidate, split/attestation, ordered fold
   identities, accepted contract hashes, and content-addressed fold inputs. The
   verifier derives pass/fail. No boolean can grant authority.
6. On restore, authorization receipts are not trusted merely because their own
   hashes are valid. Their bound evidence and deterministic verifier result are
   recomputed before use.
7. Any cross-run, cross-candidate, cross-split, roster, authority, content,
   schema, or lineage mismatch fails closed without advancing state.
8. `H40_CONFIRMATION_READY -> H40_CONFIRMATION_EVALUATED_ONCE` is prohibited.
