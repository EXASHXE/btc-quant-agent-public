### ASTRA-B-01 — Expected WF authority can be shrunk together with its evidence

- **Severity:** BLOCKER.
- **Exact owner:** `h40/lifecycle_authority.py:1641 :: H40ExpectedSplitAuthority`; `:2710 :: H40LifecycleAuthorityService.authorize_wf_validation`; `:3481 :: H40LifecycleArtifactStore._resolve_authority_context`.
- **Attack:** Keep the genuine run's split-manifest and attestation hash strings, but replace the expected fold array with a proper subset; give the verifier the same subset of evidence. The constructor accepts nonempty unique caller-supplied folds/contracts. Authorization checks evidence against that object, not folds and accepted contracts derived from the sealed runtime split. Cold resolution verifies the object's content address and its two advertised split hashes, not the truth of its fold derivation. A hash of a fabricated expectation does not make that expectation authoritative.
- **Observed synthetic result:** Using existing `_build_wf_chain`, `replace(w.split, folds=w.split.folds[:1])` plus the corresponding one-fold evidence returns `H40_WALK_FORWARD_VALIDATED`. Persistence and cold restoration also succeed. No real fold was evaluated. This exercises the shared lifecycle path behind the presently closed production gate.
- **Why tests miss it:** `test_t20_incomplete_wf_fold_set_denied` changes evidence against unchanged expectations; `test_t22_cross_split_wf_replay_denied` changes one side; `test_a18_cold_restore_detects_split_authority_substitution` checks a different context identity. None proves that a self-consistent pair derives the complete accepted fold universe. The synthetic fixture itself constructs expected folds/contracts directly.
- **Blast radius:** Complete-WF claim, gate-set completeness, cross-split lineage, Confirmation Ready. Fresh authorization additionally lacks the cold resolver's explicit seal/split equality check.
- **Required closure:** Resolve complete ordered folds, partition identities, split-definition hashes, and the exact accepted validation-contract map from verified seal-bound authority. Do this at issuance and restoration; reject caller-selected subsets, extra contracts and fabricated fold definitions even when every supplied hash is internally consistent. Test paired tampering, not just one-sided tampering.
- **Docs-first authority amendment:** No change to accepted science is needed. P3R0R1 §4.3 already requires deriving the complete ordered fold set from split manifest/attestation and the exact contract set from accepted P2 semantics. Any proposed weakening requires a separate amendment and is not recommended.

### ASTRA-B-02 — Terminal absorption is process-local, not durable run authority

- **Severity:** BLOCKER.
- **Exact owner:** `h40/lifecycle.py :: H40LifecycleStateMachine.transition_with_verified_authority`; `h40/lifecycle_authority.py :: authorize_termination, H40LifecycleArtifactStore._path, persist_authorization, restore_authorization`.
- **Attack:** Persist `H40_NO_GO` for a locked candidate. Retain its earlier Candidate Lock, issue/persist a successful sibling, and restore that sibling into another process. Storage keys are caller-selected names satisfying a regex; create-if-absent protects each filename, not one canonical transition/head per run. Restoration follows ancestors of the requested receipt, never checking a terminal descendant or competing branch.
- **Observed synthetic result:** A terminal receipt was persisted as `04_terminal`; a successful WF receipt from the same candidate was then persisted as `03_wf_validation`. A fresh resolver/service restored the latter as `H40_WALK_FORWARD_VALIDATED`. An earlier probe also advanced a fresh synthetic state machine through the successful branch despite the persisted termination. This is not a real WF run.
- **Why tests miss it:** `test_t16_no_runner_up` and `test_t29_terminal_states_absorbing` exercise one in-memory state machine. `test_t26_idempotent_persistence_revalidates` exercises one key, not concurrent/sibling transitions or stale-head restart. A correct predecessor chain proves ancestry, not that it is the only permitted history.
- **Blast radius:** Terminal decisions, immutable Candidate Lock, no-runner-up semantics, repeat selection under one deterministic run identity. The observed proof establishes terminal bypass; it does not claim a real second candidate was selected.
- **Required closure:** One durable authoritative head/transition relation per run; atomically bind allowed predecessor to unique successor, including terminal outcomes. Restoration and transition consumption must reject stale or competing heads. Add restart and concurrent-writer tests, including two candidate locks, failure followed by success, and attempted runner-up promotion. Filename choice must confer no authority.
- **Docs-first authority amendment:** A narrow docs-first persistence/concurrency clarification is needed to specify canonical transition ownership and crash/race semantics. It must preserve existing P3R0R1 §§4.2, 4.5–4.7 obligations: one lock, absorbing termination, no runner-up, no caller anti-replay namespace. Do not add a nonce to evade an existing terminal run.

### ASTRA-B-03 — Cached downstream authorization is not revalidated against current source truth

- **Severity:** BLOCKER.
- **Exact owner:** `h40/lifecycle_authority.py:2520 :: _require_prior`; `:2915 :: revalidate_authorization`; `:2369 :: VerifiedLifecycleAuthorization.assert_valid`; `h40/lifecycle.py :: transition_with_verified_authority`.

After the source becomes missing or changes, reuse its in-memory Candidate Lock or later authorization. `_require_prior` checks issuer and receipt/state lineage, not current seal truth. Candidate/WF/Confirmation revalidation reconstructs its own step from the cached prior, without recursively revalidating Discovery/seal. The state machine's authorization consumption also does not itself perform a cold source check.

- **Required closure:** Bind every transition-use/revalidation boundary to current verified source authority, or to an independently protected immutable byte snapshot whose identity is verified at the required boundary. ### ASTRA-B-04 — Reconciliation can cross testnet/live boundaries without plan-mode agreement

- **Severity:** BLOCKER (reachable exchange-write boundary; disabled defaults remain effective).
- **Exact owner:** `execution/service.py:324 :: ExecutionService.reconcile`, `:96 :: _signed_client`; `cli.py:473 :: execution reconcile dispatch`; `execution/binance_signed.py :: place_protective_order, cancel_order, place_order`.

If the live account has a matching order ID/symbol, it can cancel that order or install close-position protective orders with old plan prices. ### ASTRA-H-01 — Receipt create-if-absent is not crash-atomic publication

- **Severity:** HIGH.
- **Exact owner:** `h40/lifecycle_authority.py:3342 :: H40LifecycleArtifactStore.persist_authorization`; `:3443 :: _find_transition_key_by_receipt_hash`.
- **Attack:** `os.open(final_path, O_WRONLY | O_CREAT | O_EXCL)` exposes the final filename before bytes are written. A crash or write failure leaves an empty/partial immutable artifact. Retrying the same valid receipt sees different bytes and refuses. File `fsync` does not provide the missing atomic publication or directory durability. A malformed sibling artifact can also prevent predecessor scanning for otherwise intact receipts.
- **Observed synthetic result:** A temporary-store probe injected failure in `os.fdopen` immediately after final-path creation, closing the descriptor. - **Why tests miss it:** `test_t24_persistence_tamper` and `test_t26_idempotent_persistence_revalidates` start with complete writes; they do not inject interrupted creation, partial writes, fsync failures or concurrent readers.
- **Blast radius:** Restart availability and immutable evidence recovery. This fails closed, so it is not itself a false acceptance; manual recovery creates further governance risk.
- **Required closure:** Atomic publication of a completely written/fsynced artifact with create-if-absent semantics, durable directory handling, and a specified recovery/quarantine policy. Test crash points and concurrent publication. Coordinate with B-02's durable-head transaction rather than solving file atomicity independently of transition atomicity.
- **Docs-first authority amendment:** P3R0R1 §4.7 already requires atomic create-if-absent canonical JSON. No scientific amendment needed; recovery behavior must be documented without silently deleting or replacing accepted evidence.

## 7. PIT and temporal-leakage audit

### 7.1 What is established

## 17. v0.6 validated-playbook / Trading Policy assessment

### 17.1 Design decision

| Option | Strengths | Scientific/engineering risks | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Sequential H41/H42/H43 campaigns only | Clear local falsifiability and campaign audit trail | Repeated plumbing; cross-campaign multiplicity can be forgotten; weak operational composition | Preserve as research units, not the whole product architecture |

The proposed roadmap is directionally sound only under C. A registry must record validation scope, source/feature/cost identities, allowed states, abstention conditions, expiry/drift policy and capital constraints; it must not infer portability to another asset, timeframe or regime. Cross-playbook interactions and the router need evaluation as a combined policy. Neither architecture nor a collection of individually accepted playbooks guarantees profitability.

### 17.2 Market State Engine

Start with deterministic, PIT-available continuous descriptors and a small preregistered state partition. “Trend”, “range”, “compression”, “expansion”, “breakout”, “failed breakout”, volatility, crowding and cross-asset divergence are candidate representations, not nine free knobs to optimize after outcomes. Freeze transformations, lag/availability, boundaries, overlap, missingness and state priority. Report sample-size limitations and state uncertainty.

Keep feature/state computation shared; let each playbook declare a bounded applicability predicate. The router consumes those predicates and a deterministic conflict policy. Avoid two independent regime classifiers in playbook and router. A separately validated probabilistic classifier may follow if it adds measured value; latent-state models introduce alignment/label-switching/drift and researcher degrees of freedom and should not be the default foundation.

### 17.3 Playbook and policy abstraction

Trend Continuation, Pullback Continuation, Breakout, Failed Breakout Reversal and Mean Reversion are research-family names until separately validated. NO_TRADE is the default policy result, not a failed prediction to eliminate. Generalize H40's structural ledger, exact dependency projection and complete-universe evidence into versioned family registries with finite campaign budgets. Do not create an unconstrained Cartesian product of every state, asset, exit, threshold and model.

Initial policy routing should be deterministic and auditable: validated applicability, explicit precedence/conflict handling, portfolio exposure constraints, then NO_TRADE on ambiguity or missing evidence. Learned gating/contextual bandits require a separate exploration, off-policy/online evaluation and safety protocol; they are not a shortcut around independent validation. Even deterministic routing can overfit if its rules are repeatedly tuned against the same holdout.

### 17.4 Risk, execution and LLM roles

Reuse causal economic replay, cash ledger, fee/funding ownership, order identity, bounded request handling, immutable source evidence and guarded execution only after the applicable defects are closed. Before Future Shadow: immutable approved policy, allowed data capabilities, read-only routing and no order credentials. Before Paper/Testnet: environment binding, restart/reconciliation recovery, realistic fill/account assumptions and explicit authorization. Before Tiny Live: account/position reconciliation, kill switches, operational limits, margin/liquidation and fee/funding parity as applicable, and separate human approval. None is authorized here.

An LLM can help plan research, summarize accepted evidence, explain NO_TRADE, diagnose failures and orchestrate already-authorized jobs. It can propose playbooks for preregistered testing. It must not supply expected returns, override numerical gates, change the candidate universe, unseal partitions, allocate live capital or resolve risk conflicts by unverifiable intuition. Early conflict resolution belongs in deterministic policy; LLM explanations may describe it, not replace it.
