## Why a hybrid route is preferred if feasible

The scientific question is not whether observations occurred after today's
calendar date. The relevant question is whether their candidate-aligned outcomes
were used to choose formulas, thresholds, costs, hypotheses, duration, or
selection rules.

A historical interval may therefore provide strong out-of-sample evidence if:

1. source bytes and timestamps are authoritative;
2. the interval was not used for candidate-aligned model/strategy selection;
3. the interval is governance-masked until a preregistered one-shot gate;
4. no result-driven partition choice occurs;
5. the remaining forward phase independently checks real-time availability,
   latency, revision, execution and operational parity.

Historical masked evidence is not identical to true prospective evidence.
Therefore the final playbook still requires a short forward confirmation, but
the forward period need not carry the entire burden of candidate discovery and
statistical validation.

## Critical distinction

The project must distinguish:

- `DEVELOPMENT_EXPOSED`
- `RETROSPECTIVE_MASKED_HOLDOUT_CANDIDATE`
- `CANDIDATE_ALIGNED_OUTCOME_CONTAMINATED`
- `FORWARD_OPERATIONAL_ONLY`
- `UNKNOWN_FAIL_CLOSED`

A period is not declared pristine merely because no current report cites it.

Public knowledge of BTC/ETH price history is not by itself contamination.
Candidate-aligned outcome exposure to people with scientific change rights is
the primary concern.

## R1.1 items retained into the hybrid route

Unless a later audit rejects them, retain as the starting design:

- deterministic K=8 candidate family;
- exact candidate equations and composite gates;
- 4h holding horizon;
- 4h candidate-local cooldown;
- 12bp primary reference-friction endpoint;
- 24bp stress diagnostic;
- one-sided after-cost mean null;
- single-step shared-clock source-bundle-preserving joint-max inference;
- no sequential outcome peeking;
- no runner-up substitution;
- paired BTC/ETH source semantics;
- explicit source revision/no-backfill incident handling.

These remain proposals, not frozen authority.

## R1.1 items to redesign for hybrid use

The following parts must be amended if historical holdout is feasible:

1. Discovery/Validation are no longer necessarily future-from-D0 windows.
2. Historical archive causality and forward receive-time causality become
   separate contracts.
3. Historical masked holdout does not require reconstructed local receipt
   timestamps; forward confirmation does.
4. Synthetic duration/power selection applies to historical masked window
   lengths as well as any fallback forward windows.
5. D0 is redefined as the authority/mask-unseal boundary, not necessarily the
   beginning of all scientific observations.
6. Exposure ledger must identify which historical periods are admissible before
   exact partition bounds are frozen.
7. Playbook qualification and forward confirmation may overlap when neither can
   change the locked scientific candidate.

- shadow/paper execution;
- operational stability;
- regime/event incidence;
- implementation drift.

The forward duration should be the shortest prospectively justified information
window, preferably an information/event-count/readiness rule with a hard minimum
calendar span rather than an arbitrary multi-year wait.

No scientific candidate retuning is permitted.

## Time-compression objective

The hybrid route targets:

```text
development/design/source closure:
immediate engineering work

historical Discovery:
one-shot after freeze

historical Validation:
one-shot after lock audit

forward confirmation:
weeks to a few months, if event/source information is sufficient
```

rather than requiring 24+12+6 months by default.

The exact forward duration is not frozen here.

## Route-selection rule

Use hybrid route only if H0 demonstrates a defensible historical holdout.

Do not force the hybrid route merely because it is faster.

Fallback remains the repaired R1.1 forward-only architecture.
