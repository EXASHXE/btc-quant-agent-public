## 8. Root cause classification

Each failure has been classified into the five specified categories:

### Category A — EXPECTED_FROZEN_FIT_GUARD (11 candidates)
- **Slots**: 1, 4, 7, 13, 16 (D1 family) and 25, 28, 31, 37, 40, 43 (D2 family).
- **Mechanism**: The Platt calibrator contract `CALIBRATION_PLATT_LOGISTIC_V1` models P(y=1|s) = sigmoid(a * s + b) and enforces a strict positive slope constraint a > 0 (a directional signal must increase the probability of a positive outcome as its score increases).
- When the sample covariance/correlation between raw scores and forward returns is non-positive or slightly negative during the calibration window, unconstrained maximum likelihood estimation produces a negative slope (a < 0).
- Under the contract Newton solver with backtracking line search, no step a > 0 produces a negative log-likelihood strictly lower than the baseline flat prior (a = 0, b = logit(mean_y)).
- The calibrator correctly and defensively rejects the fit with `Platt fit has no finite positive-slope improvement`. This is the intended mathematical behavior of the guard to prevent inverted betting on noise.

### Category B — PRODUCER_VERIFIER_MISMATCH (0 candidates)
- 100% agreement. Deterministic re-execution of the calibrator on raw decision logs matches the stored `fit_status` and `fit_failure_reason` in every candidate.

### Category C — DATA_OR_PARTITION_IDENTITY_DEFECT (0 candidates)
- The partition `WF1_CALIBRATION` has exactly 2,184 hours, strictly chronological, zero gaps, zero duplicates, and identical source data SHA-256 (`563a1a4d...`).

### Category D — SCIENTIFIC_CONTRACT_DESIGN_LIMITATION (6 direct candidates + 1 propagated candidate)
- **Direct Slots**: 49, 52, 55, 61, 64, 67 (D3 family).
- **Mechanism**: In `btc_quant_agent.h40`, a decision row is marked `prefit_eligible` only if:
  `regime_state == "REGIME_VOL_MID"` AND `opportunity_state == "O_ELIGIBLE"` AND `raw_score != 0.0`.
- During the 2,184 hours of `WF1_CALIBRATION`, the D3 failed-move reversal feature generated 29 non-zero raw scores.
- However, 28 of those events occurred when volatility was classified as `REGIME_VOL_LOW` by CAP-FLT-01 (`R_VOL_RANGE_V1_24H`), and the remaining 1 event occurred in `REGIME_VOL_MID` but was classified as `O_WATCH` by CAP-FLT-02 (`O_RANGE_EXPANSION_V1_24H`).
- As a consequence, exactly **0 calibration events** satisfied the conjunction of both mandatory support contracts and the D3 trigger. With N = 0, the calibrator threw `Platt calibration population is degenerate`.
- **Propagated Slot**: Slot 19 (`D1_V2_RETURN_12H`, 12h horizon) achieved a complete, valid Platt fit (N = 41, a = 0.9907, b = -0.4485). However, because family-level availability requires all 6 members of D1 to be valid, Slot 19 was dropped by family propagation.
