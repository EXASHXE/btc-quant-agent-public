sealing B-line Live V1.

## Observation

The exact H40 scientific graph golden test has produced two different projection hashes on unchanged
H40 source across GitHub-hosted CI runs:

```text
accepted golden:
d5a962cd9c9556b573a890b6af8cbeb09d7201187511f477d727c8670bf0b2e0

recurring alternate:
ee185f1fce50bdf710295a23506a5c29d83a70c361aff72e2b6be9e54526643d
```

A prior exact B-line SHA failed with the alternate hash and then passed on a same-SHA rerun. R5
produced the alternate hash on both attempts. Direct H40 source diff remains zero.

The projection includes raw binary64 values reconstructed with operations such as:

`math.log(last.close / first.open)`

and other prefit floating calculations before canonical hashing.
