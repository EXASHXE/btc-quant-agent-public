## Mathematical and causal checks

For df=5, a t innovation has variance 5/3. The factor sqrt(3/5) gives variance one; common normal loading sqrt(gamma) and shared independent chi-square mixing preserve covariance gamma. AR scaling sqrt(1-rho²) gives limiting unit variance. The two volatility scales have (1+9)/(2*5)=1 mean squared scale, and high/low ratio three. The finite 4096h zero-state AR burn-in is explicitly nonstationary initialization, not a hidden exact-stationarity assertion.

The mean calibration is an exact constrained minimum-norm problem, not a profitability fit. Uniform phase yields E[A_k G_k]/E[A_k]=sum(A_k s_k m)/n_k=delta_k+0.0012. Noise symmetry removes the conditional innovation mean; the empty modes contribute no event mass. This establishes the population event-weighted estimand, not each finite sample’s mean. The constrained zero-variance tables retain all other coordinate mean equations and fail closed if inconsistent or unbounded.

Four interleaved positive chains give P_a(t+5)/P_a(t+1)-1=R_a(t+1) exactly. With B_a[t] covering [t-1,t), its Open is P_a(t-1), so there is no one-hour endpoint indexing shift. All same-asset candidates share these marks; overlapping endpoints are not separately assigned independent candidate returns. Exact cancellation and one final binary64 endpoint rounding avoid fabricated zero-control variance. Raw C/V phase clocks are independent of the innovation process by design: this preserves the specified event clock and source lineage, but not arbitrary feedback of future Open noise into real completed-C features. Acceptance is therefore limited to that declared conditional envelope.

The source rule excludes an hour at equality to the t+1h deadline. A late bar may enter later lookbacks after receipt, while a never-received bar cannot. Economic reconciliation marks remain separate and do not backfill missed events. Warmup, latent AR/volatility, phase and cooldown carry across the 48h audit interval and Validation. Delayed endpoint tails do not enter stage denominators. Gregorian anchors cover leap/non-leap lengths; D+V is the declared month budget, with 48h overhead separately added to elapsed time.
