`H41_D2_ETH_BO24_H4`, `H41_D4_BTC_BO24_CONFIRM_H4`, and
`H41_D4_ETH_BO24_CONFIRM_H4`, but their simultaneous lower confidence bounds
remained below zero. Their 24bp stress descriptive means were also negative.

Several unsupported coordinates show large descriptive means, especially some
24h and DIVERGE variants, but they fail the frozen support gate and cannot be
promoted into evidence, Candidate Lock, or validation candidates.

Accordingly:

1. do not open WF1_VALIDATION;
2. do not lower support thresholds or multiplicity protection;
3. do not retune the H41 roster, horizons, Q80, friction, block length, seed,
   source or inference after seeing these outcomes;
4. do not convert descriptive unsupported means into tactical or scientific
   authority;
5. preserve H41 as immutable exploratory evidence.
