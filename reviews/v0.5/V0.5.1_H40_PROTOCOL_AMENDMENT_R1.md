## 1. R1 — First Candidate-Lock Information Boundary (Closing H40R-01)

### 1.1 Sole Pre-Validation Discovery & Selection Boundary
To eliminate any ambiguity regarding multi-fold overlap or retrocausal selection bias, the candidate configuration search across the 168 pre-registered rows and the initial candidate lock are bound strictly to **Walk-Forward Fold 1 (WF1)** pre-validation intervals:

```text
WF1 TRAINING (Fit / Search Space Parameter Estimation):
2021-01-31T00:00:00Z <= t < 2022-10-31T00:00:00Z

PURGE WINDOW (Inter-interval label separation):
2022-10-31T00:00:00Z <= t < 2022-11-01T00:00:00Z

WF1 CALIBRATION / DISCOVERY-SELECTION:
2022-11-01T00:00:00Z <= t < 2023-01-31T00:00:00Z

PURGE WINDOW (Pre-validation label separation):
2023-01-31T00:00:00Z <= t < 2023-02-01T00:00:00Z
```

### 1.2 Information-Flow & Selection Invariants
1. **Pre-Outcome Ledger Hashing**: All 168 configuration rows (hyperparameters, feature specifications, horizons, regimes, and gate sequences) must be enumerated, validated, and content-hashed before computing the first H40 market label or backtest metric.
2. **Training-Only Model Fitting**: All feature distributions, directional filters, regime classifiers, and geometric excursions are fit exclusively on the WF1 Training interval (`2021-01-31` to `2022-10-31`).
3. **Dedicated Calibration Fitting**: Probabilistic calibrators (e.g. isotonic regression / Platt scaling) are fit exclusively on the WF1 Calibration interval (`2022-11-01` to `2023-01-31`).
4. **Prohibition of Downstream Contamination**: Selection of the sole winning candidate must use only WF1 Training and WF1 Calibration data according to their designated roles. Zero outcomes from WF1 Validation (`2023-02-01` to `2023-07-31`), WF2–WF4, Confirmation (`2025-02-01` to `2026-01-31`), 24h diagnostic rescue, H39 holdouts, or Final Holdout may be read or computed prior to locking the candidate.
5. **Single Candidate Lock**: The deterministic selection rule defined in Section 9.2 must identify at most one `H40-CANDIDATE_LOCKED` identity.
6. **Locked Identity Invariance**: The candidate lock permanently binds: asset scope, primary horizon, feature definitions, configuration ID, numeric hyperparameters, confidence thresholds, calibration method, abstention rules, and mechanical refit algorithm.
7. **Later Walk-Forward Refit Semantics (WF2–WF4)**: WF2, WF3, and WF4 evaluate the **already locked** candidate. At each simulated fold boundary, parameters may be re-estimated using that fold's chronologically preceding training and calibration windows because those observations are in the simulated past. However, refitting is strictly mechanical: it cannot alter configuration identity, select alternative hyperparameters, change thresholds, or swap horizons.
8. **Asymmetric Reuse of Historical Intervals**: Earlier validation observations (e.g., WF1 Validation from `2023-02-01` to `2023-07-31`) may only enter later training sets (such as WF3 Training) after the simulated clock has naturally advanced past those dates. They can never retroactively influence the first candidate lock.
9. **Fail-Closed Walk-Forward Policy**: If the locked candidate fails any numeric gate in walk-forward validation, the runner-up is **not** evaluated or promoted. H40 V1 immediately transitions to `H40_NO_GO` or `NOT_TESTABLE`. Any subsequent attempt requires an entirely new protocol authority and fresh confirmation partition.

## 2. R2 — Fully Specified Dependence-Adjusted Effective Sample Size ($n_{\text{eff}}$) (Closing H40R-02)

To make Section 9.1 and Section 11 sample-floor checks bitwise reproducible across independent implementations, the HAC effective sample size estimator is deterministically locked as follows.

### 2.1 Input Series Definition
Let an evaluated candidate on partition $\mathcal{P}$ emit decisions at hourly timestamps $t$. An accepted trade occurs whenever the emitted action $a_t \in \{-1, +1\}$ (LONG or SHORT), abstaining when $a_t = 0$ (NO_TRADE).

Let $N$ be the total count of accepted trades in $\mathcal{P}$. Let the ordered sequence of accepted trade entries be $i = 1, 2, \dots, N$.
For each accepted trade $i$:
1. **Directional Correctness Series** $y_i \in \{0, 1\}$:
   $$y_i = \mathbf{1}_{\{\text{trade } i \text{ direction matches realization}\}}$$
2. **Net Return Series** $x_i \in \mathbb{R}$:
   $$x_i = r_{\text{net}, i}$$
   where $r_{\text{net}, i}$ is the cost-adjusted fractional return of trade $i$ under the frozen 12 bps round-trip friction proxy.

### 2.2 Chronological Ordering and Simultaneous Decisions
- Trades are ordered chronologically by entry timestamp $t_i$.
- If both `BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT` trigger an accepted trade at the exact same hourly timestamp $t$, they are ordered deterministically by symbol string: `BTCUSDT` at index $i$, followed by `ETHUSDT` at index $i+1$.

### 2.3 Mathematical Formula for HAC Variance Inflation
For an arbitrary real-valued trade series $z = (z_1, z_2, \dots, z_N)$:
1. **Sample Mean**:
   $$\bar{z} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N z_i$$
2. **Sample Autocovariance** at lag $k \ge 0$:
   $$\hat{\gamma}_k = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N-k} (z_i - \bar{z})(z_{i+k} - \bar{z})$$
3. **Sample Autocorrelation** at lag $k$:
   $$\hat{\rho}_k = \begin{cases} \frac{\hat{\gamma}_k}{\hat{\gamma}_0}, & \text{if } \hat{\gamma}_0 > 0 \\ 0, & \text{if } \hat{\gamma}_0 = 0 \end{cases}$$
4. **Bandwidth and Truncation Lag** $K$:
   $$K = \min(24, N - 1)$$
   (Maximum lag is strictly bound to 24, consistent with the maximum diagnostic horizon).
5. **Bartlett Kernel Weights**:
   $$w_k = 1 - \frac{k}{K + 1}, \quad k = 1, 2, \dots, K$$
6. **Autocorrelation Summation**:
   $$S_K = 2 \sum_{k=1}^K w_k \hat{\rho}_k$$
7. **Variance Inflation Factor** $f(z)$:
   $$f(z) = 1 + S_K$$

### 2.4 Effective Sample Size Conversion and Edge Cases
The raw effective sample size for series $z$ is:
$$n_{\text{HAC}}(z) = \begin{cases} \frac{N}{f(z)}, & \text{if } f(z) > 0 \text{ and } \hat{\gamma}_0 > 0 \\ 1.0, & \text{if } f(z) \le 0 \text{ or } \hat{\gamma}_0 = 0 \text{ or } N < 2 \text{ or } f(z) \text{ is non-finite} \end{cases}$$

To prevent variance underestimation from spuriously inflating sample counts, $n_{\text{HAC}}(z)$ is strictly clipped:
$$n_{\text{HAC, clipped}}(z) = \operatorname{clip}\left(n_{\text{HAC}}(z), \, 1.0, \, N\right)$$

### 2.5 Conservative Joint Floor
Because H40 evaluates both directional precision and net expectancy, the governing effective sample size must be conservative across both dimensions:
$$n_{\text{HAC}} = \min\left(n_{\text{HAC, clipped}}(y), \; n_{\text{HAC, clipped}}(x)\right)$$

The final integer effective sample size $n_{\text{eff}}$ for all Section 11 sample floors is:
$$n_{\text{eff}} = \min\left(N, \; N_{\text{24h\_blocks}}, \; \lfloor n_{\text{HAC}} \rfloor\right)$$
where $N_{\text{24h\_blocks}}$ is the number of distinct occupied UTC calendar days (`YYYY-MM-DD`) containing at least one accepted trade entry.

All intermediate values ($N, N_{\text{24h\_blocks}}, \hat{\gamma}_0(y), \hat{\gamma}_0(x), K, S_K(y), S_K(x), n_{\text{HAC}}(y), n_{\text{HAC}}(x), n_{\text{eff}}$) must be persisted in evaluation output artifacts for bitwise verification.

## 3. R3 — Moving-Block-Bootstrap Execution Semantics (Closing H40R-03)

### 3.1 Bootstrap Setup and Start Universe
Let the evaluation partition $\mathcal{P}$ span a continuous half-open interval of $H$ hourly timestamps: $[t_0, t_H)$.
- **Block Length ($L$)**: Fixed at 168 hours (7 calendar days).
- **Number of Replicates ($M$)**: Fixed at 10,000.
- **Start Universe**: The set of valid block start indices is strictly:
  $$\mathcal{U}_{\text{start}} = \{j \in \mathbb{Z} \mid 0 \le j \le H - 168\}$$
- **Non-Circular**: Circular wrapping across the partition boundary is strictly **prohibited**. No block may wrap from the end of the partition back to the beginning.
- **Number of Blocks Drawn**:
  $$B = \left\lceil \frac{H}{168} \right\rceil$$
  For each replicate $m \in \{1, \dots, 10000\}$, draw $B$ start indices $j_1, \dots, j_B$ independently and uniformly with replacement from $\mathcal{U}_{\text{start}}$.

### 3.2 Inseparable Joint-Asset Blocks & Trimming
1. **Inseparable UTC Blocks**: Each drawn block of 168 consecutive hours contains the aligned market observations and candidate decisions for both `BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT`. Cross-asset cross-sectional dependence is preserved by sampling entire joint UTC hours.
2. **Missing / Gapped Hours**: If a market gap exists within the historical partition, missing hours remain missing in the drawn block; no synthetic rows or artificial interpolations are introduced.
3. **Trimming**: Concatenate the $B$ blocks sequentially to form a sampled timeline of length $168 \times B$ hours. Trim this timeline to exactly the first $H$ hours.

### 3.3 Deterministic Pseudo-Random Number Generation
The random seed for each bootstrap evaluation is deterministically computed as:
$$\text{seed} = \text{uint64}\left(\text{SHA-256}\left(\text{protocol\_id} \parallel \text{candidate\_id} \parallel \text{partition\_id} \parallel \text{metric\_id}\right)[:8]\right) \pmod{2^{63} - 1}$$
The generator must use the standard 64-bit Permuted Congruential Generator (PCG64 / NumPy default RNG) initialized with this exact seed.

### 3.4 Replicate Metric Recomputation and Degenerate Cases
Within each replicate $m$:
- Extract all accepted trades from the resampled timeline.
- Recompute precision $\hat{p}^{(m)}$, net expectancy $\hat{\mu}^{(m)}$, and profit factor $\widehat{\text{PF}}^{(m)}$.
- **Degenerate Handling**:
  - If accepted trade count $N^{(m)} = 0$: $\hat{p}^{(m)} = 0.0, \hat{\mu}^{(m)} = 0.0, \widehat{\text{PF}}^{(m)} = 0.0$.
  - If gross losses $= 0$ and gross gains $> 0$: $\widehat{\text{PF}}^{(m)} = 100.0$ (clipped maximum).
  - If a replicate contains only one active side (e.g. LONG only): side-specific SHORT metric is recorded as `NaN`; pooled metric evaluates on the available trades.

### 3.5 Exact Quantile Convention for Lower Confidence Bounds
For a resampled metric vector sorted in ascending order $v_{(1)} \le v_{(2)} \le \dots \le v_{(10000)}$:
- **95% Lower Confidence Bound** (one-sided $\alpha = 0.05$):
  $$\text{LCB}_{0.95} = v_{(500)}$$
- **97.5% Lower Confidence Bound** (one-sided $\alpha = 0.025$, confirmation co-primary gate):
  $$\text{LCB}_{0.975} = v_{(250)}$$
- **90% Lower Confidence Bound** (one-sided $\alpha = 0.10$, secondary diagnostic):
  $$\text{LCB}_{0.90} = v_{(1000)}$$
Integer rank indexing (0-indexed rank $\lfloor 10000 \cdot \alpha \rfloor$) is standard and exact.

### 3.6 Within-Family Max-Statistic and Holm Adjustment
Within each direction family containing $K$ candidate configurations ($k = 1, \dots, K$):
1. Compute the point estimate $\hat{\theta}_k$ on the observed partition for metric $\theta$ (e.g., net expectancy).
2. For each replicate $m \in \{1, \dots, 10000\}$, compute the centered test statistic:
   $$T_k^{(m)} = \hat{\theta}_k^{(m)} - \hat{\theta}_k$$
3. Form the family max-statistic for replicate $m$:
   $$M^{(m)} = \max_{1 \le k \le K} T_k^{(m)}$$
4. The family-adjusted critical value $q_{1-\alpha}(M)$ is the $(1-\alpha)$ quantile of $M^{(m)}$ ($v_{(9500)}$ for $\alpha=0.05$). The family-adjusted lower bound for configuration $k$ is:
   $$\text{Adj\_LCB}_k = \hat{\theta}_k - q_{1-\alpha}(M)$$
5. For Holm-adjusted p-values across family representatives, marginal p-values $p_{(1)} \le p_{(2)} \le \dots \le p_{(K)}$ are adjusted via:
   $$p_{(j)}^{\text{Holm}} = \min\left(1.0, \; \max_{1 \le r \le j} \left[(K - r + 1) p_{(r)}\right]\right)$$

## 4. R4 — Exact Empirical Directional Null ($p_0$) (Closing H40R-04)

### 4.1 Definition and Formula
The operative directional null threshold $p_0$ is defined as:
$$p_0 = \max\left(0.50, \; \text{base\_precision}\right)$$
where $\text{base\_precision}$ is the precision achieved by a random guesser matching the candidate's emitted side mix against the empirical training-set label distribution:
$$\text{base\_precision} = w_{\text{LONG}} \cdot \pi_{\text{LONG}}^{\text{train}} + w_{\text{SHORT}} \cdot \pi_{\text{SHORT}}^{\text{train}}$$

### 4.2 Component Definitions
1. **Training Partition Source**:
   - For Walk-Forward Fold $k$ ($k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$): $\pi_{\text{LONG}}^{\text{train}}$ and $\pi_{\text{SHORT}}^{\text{train}}$ are computed strictly on **Fold $k$'s Training Partition**.
   - For Confirmation: $\pi_{\text{LONG}}^{\text{train}}$ and $\pi_{\text{SHORT}}^{\text{train}}$ are computed strictly on the **Pre-Confirmation Training Set** (`2021-01-31` to `2025-01-30`).
   - Zero evaluation, validation, or confirmation labels may enter the calculation of $\pi^{\text{train}}$.
2. **Label Base Rates** $\pi_{\text{LONG}}^{\text{train}}, \pi_{\text{SHORT}}^{\text{train}}$:
   Let $N_{\text{eligible}}^{\text{train}}$ be the total count of base-eligible decisions across the candidate's active asset scope in the designated training partition:
   $$\pi_{\text{LONG}}^{\text{train}} = \frac{\sum_{t \in \text{train}} \mathbf{1}_{\{\text{label}_t = +1\}}}{N_{\text{eligible}}^{\text{train}}}, \quad \pi_{\text{SHORT}}^{\text{train}} = \frac{\sum_{t \in \text{train}} \mathbf{1}_{\{\text{label}_t = -1\}}}{N_{\text{eligible}}^{\text{train}}}$$
   (Note: neutral rows $\text{label}_t = 0$ enter the denominator $N_{\text{eligible}}^{\text{train}}$, as non-directional market intervals reduce the hit rate of an unselective directional guesser).
3. **Candidate Emitted Side Weights** $w_{\text{LONG}}, w_{\text{SHORT}}$:
   Let $C_{\text{LONG}}$ and $C_{\text{SHORT}}$ be the number of accepted LONG and SHORT decisions emitted by the candidate on the evaluation partition.
   If total accepted trades $N = C_{\text{LONG}} + C_{\text{SHORT}} > 0$:
   $$w_{\text{LONG}} = \frac{C_{\text{LONG}}}{N}, \quad w_{\text{SHORT}} = \frac{C_{\text{SHORT}}}{N}$$

### 4.3 Edge-Case Handling
- **Single-Sided Emissions**:
  - If $C_{\text{SHORT}} = 0$ and $C_{\text{LONG}} > 0$: $w_{\text{LONG}} = 1.0, w_{\text{SHORT}} = 0.0 \implies \text{base\_precision} = \pi_{\text{LONG}}^{\text{train}}$.
  - If $C_{\text{LONG}} = 0$ and $C_{\text{SHORT}} > 0$: $w_{\text{LONG}} = 0.0, w_{\text{SHORT}} = 1.0 \implies \text{base\_precision} = \pi_{\text{SHORT}}^{\text{train}}$.
- **Zero-Trade Degeneracy**:
  - If $N = 0$: The candidate fails the mandatory sample floor ($N=0 < 100$), yielding `NOT_TESTABLE`. By convention, $p_0 = 0.50$.
- **No Smoothing**:
  - No Laplace smoothing, Bayesian priors, or ad-hoc adjustments are permitted. The frequencies are strictly empirical and deterministic.

## 5. R5 — Preservation of All Base Protocol Invariants

1. **Instrument Scope**: `BTCUSDT` and `ETHUSDT` perpetual contracts on Binance.

10. **One-Shot Confirmation**: The confirmation partition (`2025-02-01` to `2026-01-31`) is evaluated exactly once without retuning.
11. **Canonical Replay Authority**: Canonical economic simulation under the frozen kernel is the final economic qualification authority.
