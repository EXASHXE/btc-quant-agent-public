### Strongest counterexample to an ungrounded "crowding" mechanism (conceptual, not market-data scoring)

Suppose the same causally closed BTC price shock, completed-bar taker imbalance, volatility state and F3 basis feature vector **X** accompany either a reduction in **total contracts** due to balanced long/short position closures or a rise in contracts from simultaneous new long/short openings. Both states can have the *same* price path and taker-flow sign, but OI differs. Thus **Z=OI change is not a mathematical function of X** and is *potentially* new incremental information. However, a fall in OI still does not identify whether the long or short side initiated the closure, whether exits were voluntary, or whether liquidations drove the move. The only supported potential hypothesis is **`OI_CONDITIONED_FAILED_AUCTION_REVERSAL` (working taxonomy, not an authorized new tradable candidate)**; claims of measured "crowded long flush" or "short squeeze" are unverified causal stories.

**Adversarial stop:** If the proposer only offers wick+volume+flow+funding/Mark states, an alias or new thresholds/horizon, **retire that branch as `HISTORICAL_OVERLAP_RETIRED` immediately**. If proposer claims Z without genuine independent OI evidence and a distinct conditional null, **do not label it a qualifying new scientific mechanism**.
