## Exact static A/B/C wire blockers observed on frozen source (new factual findings)

The **schema envelope names** largely match `MarketEventV1`, but the **payload semantics and book fields do NOT**. No claim of executable A↔B↔C integration is allowed before independent negative tests:

1. **A Mark ticks ≠ B Mark OHLC bars.** A `connector.py` `event_type == markPriceUpdate` forwards raw provider payload `{e,E,s,p,i,r,T,...}` under `channel=mark_price`; B `adapter.py` on that channel calls `parse_kline_payload(payload)`, which takes `payload.get("k", payload)` then seeks `t,T,x,o,h,l,c`. For raw Binance mark tick, `x` absent => `closed=False`; B never accepts a matching mark bar and P1 paired-minute feed stalls. Never invent an official Mark 1m kline from a single arbitrary tick. A future derived Mark 1m close must be a distinct `DERIVED_MARK_1M` provenance type with full within-window data coverage and only causal close/ingress timing, otherwise UNKNOWN.
2. **A best-book receipt ≠ B required 10-level executable quote depth.** A `contracts.py` `BookIntegrityV1` serializes IDs, `best_bid/best_ask`, `observed_depth_levels`, stale, quality only; no price/size `bids[]/asks[]`. New SHA-bound read-only depth exposure + atomic same-book receipt needed before P2 is eligible.
3. **A official settled Funding history payload ≠ B settlement parser.** A `connector.py` recognizes `{fundingRate,fundingTime,...}` and labels settled; B `contract.py` `parse_funding_payload` reads `r` and `T` with zero defaults (and `p/markPrice`).
