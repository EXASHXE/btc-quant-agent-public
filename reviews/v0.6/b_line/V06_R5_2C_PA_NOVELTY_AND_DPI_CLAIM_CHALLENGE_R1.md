## 2. Priority 0 — PA-A DPI zero increment is a false unconditional inference

**Frozen disputed assertion:** Gemini lines **28–34**, **169–232**: a PA categorical-state path has “strictly zero orthogonal predictive information beyond ATR, EMA slope, Momentum and Kaufman ER”; lines **175–193** present a finite indicator vector (including a mean `OverlapFrac`) and claim the original PA states are a deterministic partition of those selected indicators; lines **195–219** assert `T=g(X_[t-k,t])` and invoke DPI. The **mathematical theorem** is valid **conditional on that exact deterministic sufficiency premise**; its claimed applicability to the actual PA text is **unproven**.

Define **P** as the complete causally available OHLCV path, **W=f(P)** as the *particular frozen finite indicator/summary vector or trajectory*, **T=g(P)** as the full path/pivot/cycle-structured PA state, and **Y** a future outcome (not observed here).
- **`CLAIM_VALID / CONDITIONAL`:** If `T=h(W)` almost surely, then `I(T;Y|W)=0`; `I(T;Y)≤I(W;Y)≤I(P;Y)` by data processing, assuming the required well-defined mutual informations. Equally, if T is a deterministic transform of the **entire** raw P, then `I(T;Y|P)=0`. This says **no new raw external data**, not absence of feature or policy increment over a smaller W.
- **`FALSE_AS_GENERAL_CLAIM`:** `I(T;Y|P)=0` **does not imply** `I(T;Y|W)=0` whenever `σ(W)⊊σ(P)`. Gemini's actual original PA source requires **successive pivot formation, swing order/leg count, wedge line convergence, double-testing and channel breakout/retest order** (e.g. PA `文件14...` lines 7–21 and `文件15...` lines 34–71). These are path-dependent predicates. The six listed W summaries do **not** come with any exact reconstructibility proof for all pivot/line/order details. The author simply *defines an alternative* discretized channel heuristic from W; equivalence to the source text/LLM is unverified.
- **Independent invented mathematical countermodel (NOT BTC returns):** Let B,N be independent fair binary bits; define a toy price-path observation `P=(B,N)`, a lossy indicator `W=N`, a structural state `T=B` and synthetic future label `Y=B`. Then `I(T;Y|P)=0`, yet `I(T;Y|W)=H(B)=1 bit`. This **refutes the universal implication**, without asserting any PA state actually predicts BTC.
- **`FALSE_AS_GENERAL_CLAIM`:** Gemini lines **224–226** state discretization **strictly** destroys predictive information except future returns being piecewise-constant per cell. DPI is **non-strict**. Equality holds whenever `Y ⟂ W | T` (T is a sufficient statistic), including the trivial `Y⊥W` case; it does **not** require deterministic or piecewise-constant future returns. Furthermore, the expression with `H(W|T)-H(W|T,Y)` for continuously valued W uses **differential entropy** and is not a generally safe proof when those quantities are undefined; the chain-rule identity in terms of mutual information avoids this problem.
- **`CONDITIONAL`:** A stable LLM-produced T, if it truly reads only P and independently generated randomness, cannot reveal an *unobserved market source* outside P by DPI. But that does not establish that T is **deterministic h(W)**. LLM prompt, version, prior training and decoding introduce reproducibility/model-state issues; if separate contextual features exist they must be explicitly included in P. No fact about conditional real-market predictive information or utility follows until a separate admitted prospective comparison.

**Adversarial required correction:** Replace “PA-A proven zero orthogonal predictive information relative to the six indicators” by **`NO_NEW_VENUE_SOURCE; OVERLAP_WITH_PRIOR_PRICE_GEOMETRY; CONDITIONAL_INCREMENT_VS_FINITE_W_UNKNOWN`**.
