## 2. Independent quantitative and causal counterexamples (invented only)

### C01 HIGH — conditioning on timely OI availability changes the target population

**Frozen target:** sidecar lines **78–95**, especially `C={e in E_X: all X and Z sources qualified by deadline}` at line 93. The author **correctly** keeps all missing OI records as explicit statuses in `E_X`; no row loss in the master ledger. Nevertheless, a matched effect in `C` is a **conditional estimand**, not the full `E_X` policy effect.

**Invented no-price witness:** 100 canonical price events; 60 calm events have timely, verified OI, 40 volatile-shock events suffer a 15m-statistics ingest/clock delay and become `Z_status=LATE`. `C` contains the 60 calm events, despite the original economic objective covering all 100. A scientific conclusion learned only in `C` cannot be generalized to 100 without an independent missingness/transport argument; conditioning on data delivery may act as a *selection/collider*, since both market regime and network/source health can influence what arrives on time. This example assigns **no returns**, no invented win rate and no empirical market-frequency claim.

**Mandatory bounded next gate:** preregister the `E_X` population and the `C` conditional target distinctly, missingness by side/regime/UTC week/source epoch, an independent minimum coverage and precision threshold, and an outcome-independent fallback/NO_ACTION policy for `OI_UNKNOWN`. For a claim about deployable 48h portfolio opportunity rate or strategy utility, require a **full `E_X` intention-to-treat-like policy comparison**, not just selected `C`. A no-data branch cannot be silently dropped from the net-cash denominator or assigned a fictional zero market return.

### C02 HIGH — common delayed decision isolates feature information but not practical latency cost

**Frozen target:** sidecar lines **84–95**. Matching f(X) and f(X,Z) at exactly the same `t_decision_common` *when Z has arrived* is a good **conditional incremental-information** contrast. It does **not**, by itself, answer whether a source-gated live-style policy improves over X-only trading that was already eligible **before** OI publication/receipt.

**Invented clock-only witness:** shock and X confirmation close at **12:15:00 UTC**; qualified closed-price X is receiver-known by **12:15:02** and would permit a fixed X-only decision at **12:15:05**. The same episode's 15m OI bucket is first safely received at **12:19:40**, so X+OI can decide no earlier than **12:19:45**. The paired test that forcibly evaluates both policies at 12:19:45 removes 4m40s of original execution-timing advantage for X-only. Even if Z were independently informative, the actual policy improvement may be offset by staleness, changed quote and additional entry latency. **No future price movement or PnL is assumed here.**

**Mandatory bounded next gate:** freeze **two distinct prospective comparisons** under later authority: (A) matched common-clock `f_X^{delayed}` vs `f_XZ^{delayed}` to identify conditional **Z information**; (B) practical full-population `f_X^{natural_early}` vs fixed-deadline `f_XZ^{wait-or-fallback}` with identical portfolio/cost accounting, to include **waiting opportunity cost**, unknown-OI no-action/fallback, nonfills, quote age and occupancy. Do not substitute result (A) for operational result (B), or allow an outcome-selected wait time.
