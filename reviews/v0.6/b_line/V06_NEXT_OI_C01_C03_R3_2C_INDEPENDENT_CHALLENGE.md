## 1. C01 — missingness, denominators and conditional estimand

**What R3 substantively corrects (PASS in design scope):** R3 §1.1–1.3 (lines 19–56) separates scheduled UTC slots, valid-X slots, X-source-missing slots, stable economic-episode `E_X`, timely and verifiably known OI subset `C_Z`, and jointly delayed quote/execution-eligible `C_EXE`; it requires typed absent/late/rights/clock/version/units/epoch reasons and blocks retroactive admission. It explicitly says `theta_A(C_Z)` does **not** identify full-policy `theta_B(E_X)`; proposes outcome-blind source availability by side/regime/week/epoch, coverage/precision and 20-week preliminary support gates. This is a meaningful correction of R2's C01 objection, **not** a demonstrated measurement of current source coverage.

**[2-C-C01-A] HIGH — stratification does not identify missing-not-at-random or positivity beyond registered cells.** Source availability may depend on an omitted interaction (e.g. session × volatility × receiver epoch), an exchange/publication backlog, or a shock condition unobserved when X ingress is down. Overall coverage and side × volatility quartile margins can pass while a decision-critical joint cell has `P(C_Z | X)=0`. Counts by UTC month/week and epochs are necessary diagnostics, **not proof** of conditional exchangeability, positivity or transport. R3 correctly disclaims unconditional transport; the remaining threat is an **unidentified full-population information effect** and a potentially unrepresentative trading policy. Invented counterexample with no returns: in each side×volatility cell 80/100 episodes are timely, 20 are not; every miss falls in one particular narrow UTC-session/receiver-epoch combination. All eight side×volatility margins are 80%, yet there is zero OI evidence for that joint stratum. A model fit only on `C_Z` cannot establish its utility there. This is **not** an empirical missing-rate claim.

**[2-C-C01-B] HIGH — source-neutral parent `E_X` is conditional on observable X, not the 24/7 latent opportunity universe.** R3 explicitly tracks `N_X_source_missing_slots` separately. But X-source blackouts can hide price shocks entirely, so `E_X` is not equal to all possible 24/7 opportunities. Calling B a full-`E_X` comparison is valid **only for the X-observable parent population**, not an all-calendar market-benefit or one-per-48h true-opportunity rate. Future permissions must predeclare the observable-population target and report source exposure/invalid slots without coding unknown episodes as zero opportunities. Source identity, X PIT and expected source coverage are not proven. If X itself includes still-unadmitted aggressor-flow data, no prospective `E_X` can be claimed source-admitted.

**[2-C-C01-C] HIGH — effective information for coverage is independent calendar clusters, not raw episodes.** The proposal uses ≥20 distinct weeks with events as a **preliminary** support floor and separately asks for two-sided 95% coverage half-width ≤0.10 / lower bound ≥0.80 overall and ≥0.60 within side×volatility cells (R3 §1.3). These are appropriately labeled proposals, yet no specific coverage interval method with validated finite-sample dependent-week behavior is frozen. Invented perfect-within-week dependence: 20 nominal weeks, 50 episodes each, only 18 weeks fully timely and 2 fully absent. Event-level 900/1000 (=90%) looks precise under false IID. **If** the 20 week statuses were genuinely independent Bernoulli, the illustrative 95% Wilson interval for 18/20 is approximately **[0.699,0.972]**, with half-width ≈0.136, so the quoted lower-0.80 / half-width-0.10 gates would **fail**. More week-to-week dependence or zero-event weeks worsen uncertainty. This is a *counterexample*, not a calculation from BTC records or a criticism that the author already claimed 20 weeks were sufficient.

**Required later gate:** Freeze the exact source-only cluster estimand and dependence-robust interval/uncertainty procedure with its non-IID limits; min eligibility and missingness thresholds *jointly* over critical conditioning factors or justified source-transport model; a registered sensitivity/bounding policy when positivity fails; calendar-slot and episode denominators including X outages and no-event weeks; no replacement of unknowns by zeros, no retrospective REST repairs. The 72h OI-only permit **cannot satisfy 20-week support** and has no authority to infer `E_X` BTC signal frequencies.

## 2. C02 — information estimand A vs full-policy/account estimand B

**What R3 substantively corrects (PASS in design scope):** R3 §2.1–2.5 (lines 58–118) binds `t_X_safe`, **fixed** `D(e)=t_X_safe+W_fixed` rounded to scheduler tick independent of OI arrival/price results, `t_early` and `t_delayed`, and makes two non-interchangeable targets:
- **A (conditional information):** paired `theta_A(C_Z)=E[U_D(pi_XZ)-U_D(pi_X)|C_Z]`, identical delayed quote guard and frozen X, with NO_ACTION and inability to transport to full `E_X`.
- **B (natural timing/policy):** `theta_B(E_X)=E[V_portfolio(pi_W;T0:T1)-V_portfolio(pi_E;T0:T1)]`, on one chronological raw `E_X` stream, including missing OI, naturally early X decision versus **wait-or-NO_ACTION** OI policy, separate positions, fills/costs, occupancy/cooldowns/funding, with full account-level utility needed. The fallback is explicitly a different registered arm. This resolves the principal *conceptual conflation* in R2.

The 12:15:02 X-safe, 12:15:05 early, 12:19:40 OI receipt, 12:20:05 fixed delayed example is internally time-ordered: 12:15:02 plus 5m rounds to 12:20:05 at a 5s scheduler; the deadline is **not** `OI_arrival+5s`. This is an invented illustrative clock only.

**[2-C-C02-A] HIGH — A is not the incremental *source-admission* claim and is potentially delivery-conditioned information.** Even when `pi_X` and `pi_XZ` are evaluated at common D, conditional selection by `Z_status_at_D=TIMELY_VERIFIED` means its effect is for that delivery cohort. If Z feature construction inadvertently includes receipt latency or correction status, the experimental change from X to X+Z includes **source-health information**, not necessarily the proposed OI quantity itself. Freeze **Z as only the independently observed numerical OI predicate** (with provenance/latency used as availability gates), or register a separately labeled latency/source-health arm consuming multiplicity. A falsifiable **value-of-OI-only ablation** must not sneak in clock/censoring features; X must remain frozen at t_X. Even a positive A result says nothing about net wait costs.

**[2-C-C02-B] HIGH — B’s policy valuation functional is not sufficiently identified for an economic claim.** `V_portfolio` is symbolic without a precisely frozen **cashflow/equity/mark-to-market/risk-unit** definition, accounting horizon, treatment of inventory open at T1, opportunity overlap and fixed capital normalization. The design wisely marks incomplete fills/settlement as `COST_INCOMPLETE`, so this is **NOT yet a validated net utility**. A later experiment must freeze mark vs executable liquidation value, partial fills/nonfills, tick/lot, fees/queue/slippage/stop-gap, signed settled funding known-at, last observation and purge/terminal valuation, whether the primary objective is terminal wealth, risk-adjusted growth or constrained account utility. This target cannot be retrospectively chosen after seeing outcomes. Merely using the same simulated cost *function* for early/late does **not** imply equal measured prices, fees, executable size or queue fill.

**[2-C-C02-C] HIGH — policy interference and calendar exposure require pathwise B comparison, not paired episodic uplift.** Let one invented event e1 be X-eligible early, e2 occur before e1's hypothetical 4–24h exit; `pi_E` may occupy the one-position book while `pi_W` is flat because its OI was UNKNOWN. The same e2 can then be admitted only under `pi_W`. This is not unfair selection—it is real **portfolio-policy interference**. B must process both policies on the same *exogenous* chronological `E_X`/calendar sequence with independent evolving account/occupancy state, and report opportunity suppression, no-action, clock-eligible quotes and end-of-window inventory separately. The R3 text already acknowledges this, but the S01–S18 synthetic cases contain no actual pathwise account-state demonstration. No source-only 72h QC can prove it. An estimated `theta_A` summed over events is mathematically not `theta_B`.

**Required later gate:** A (conditional feature information) and B (operational economic utility) are **two primary selection/test families**, jointly controlling all side/clock/fallback/horizon/multiplicity inherited from H32–H34, R3, Goal-B and F1–F3. Freeze B outcome scale, account path semantics, natural-X quote at t_early and OI fixed deadline, fallback/no-action and gap behavior; ensure no modeled fill/cost or success inferred from missing exchange receipts. Design power by independent calendars and realistically supported δ_net only *after separate exact-SHA source/outcome authority*. Do not treat B as admissible in the current SOURCE_NOT_ADMITTED state.

## 3. C03 — synthetic time/payload witness versus authenticated source

**What R3 substantively corrects (PASS in design scope):** R3 §3.1–3.3 (lines 120–178) distinguishes Binance OI `timestamp` (period end), snapshot `time` (transaction time), gateway publication (UNKNOWN), body-received timestamp, parser, **durable** raw-byte commit, external UTC discipline and epoch, original first-seen/append-only revisions, receipt ordering and authenticated rights. S01–S18 are explicitly *future expected synthetic test results*, not executed tests. R3 uses `K(v)=max(provider_period_end_safe, durable_receiver_complete_UTC_hi, authenticated_publish_hi_if_any)`, strict predecision use, no later backdating and fail-closed on clock/source ambiguity. This is an important correct **contract**, not a verified source.

**[2-C-C03-A] HIGH — self-computed raw SHA-256 and journal sequence do not authenticate exchange origin.** `raw_response_bytes_sha256`, `config_digest`, `endpoint="/futures/data/openInterestHist"` and a local signed journal prove internal byte/version identity **if** the witness is trusted; they do **not** prove the bytes actually came from an authentic Binance USD-M endpoint. Invented malicious/no-origin fixture: a local program emits a syntactically valid fake OI JSON with accurate period end, a perfectly matching raw SHA-256, proper monotonic timestamps, contiguous sequence and a valid local journal signature. Every self-consistency check could pass while source provenance is false. Source admission therefore needs separately auditable authenticated **transport/origin** evidence (verified TLS peer/hostname, trust chain and collector/ingress build provenance, request/response binding, immutable channel/receipt witness and storage attestation), not merely a claimed URL and digest. Even such TLS evidence cannot establish a provider *publication instant* or nonexistence of earlier revisions; preserve those unknowns.

**[2-C-C03-B] HIGH — true earliest usable receipt is after the last required durability + clock-certification boundary, not just last HTTP byte.** Invented failure: body completes at **12:19:40.000**, UTC-safe upper is **12:19:40.050**, but its WAL fsync is not durably acknowledged until **12:20:08** and an OI decision deadline is **12:20:05**. The body was seen before D but was not reproducibly committed by D; an authorized deterministic replay must refuse it at D. R3 explicitly recognizes this rule in §3.2, but none of S01–S18 explicitly demonstrates this *positive response plus delayed durable ACK* race. Add the independent negative fixture with externally attested UTC bound of fsync/commit: **`LATE_DURABLE_RECEIPT / NO_OI_ACTION`**, and crash after receiving but before journal commit. Distinguish worker payload visibility from durable evidence publication; fail closed if clock discipline unavailable.

**[2-C-C03-C] HIGH — future expected results are not observed results and remain cross-module integration obligations.** R3 S01–S18 merely enumerate expected assertions. No receiver implementation, no independent tests/CI, actual raw stored payloads, external clock logs, commercial/legal rights, shared-IP budget lease, source units, revision observations or authorized acquisition exist. A synthetic fixture with `external_clock_attestation_id=null`, `raw_blob_ref=null`, `rights_receipt_id=null` is correctly labeled `REJECT_SYNTHETIC_ONLY`. Another invented adversarial test should verify *same provider logical key with byte-different but numerically equal payload* (raw receipts remain separate, normalized version shared), and *numerically conflicting correction first-seen after decision* (prior v1 preserved as-known). Tests must check cross-epoch monotonic resets, wall rollback, 429/418 stop and explicit source-rights rejection. **An independent verifier must execute and freeze test receipts before an implementation can be called ready**, and a separate Controller permit is still needed before any collection.

**Required later gate:** Authenticated channel/collector/time-discipline/rights evidence and durable first-known receipts, with explicit negative tests for fake-origin hash, partial body, delayed WAL ACK, rollback, cross-epoch ambiguity, revision after D, missing predecessor and invalid unit specs. The current R3 is a **synthetic contract**, not a source certification and not license to poll an exchange. Different authorization is necessary to build a receiver, and an even more specific source-rights/time/resource approval is necessary to use market endpoints.
