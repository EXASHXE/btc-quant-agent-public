## 2. Marginal genuinely distinct information frontier (none presently source-feasible)

**One conceptual incremental information dimension is independently identifiable in *principle*, not currently usable:** a truly measured, causally available **new venue-native positioning/forced-position event source**, such as genuine aggregate BTCUSDT USD-M OI *change* as separate information `Z` alongside a fixed previously exposed price/flow/volatility/funding baseline `X`. The identity question would be whether `Z` exists at a known permissible decision time and carries reproducible incremental information **conditional on X**, not whether an OHLC wick plus a new name produces new Alpha. Existing OI/fakeout documents had already proposed this distinction, so it is **not claimed here as newly invented, independently trial-ready, or an unspent fresh hypothesis**. A venue-native forced-liquidation/side claim would require distinct original signed liquidation evidence; aggregate `delta OI` by itself cannot prove the liquidated direction or forced order identity. Mere price, aggressive trade proxy or funding sign does not measure this state.

**Grade: `FRONTIER_EXISTS_CONCEPTUALLY_BUT_SOURCE_BLOCKED`.** The necessary falsification/dependency witnesses, none currently admitted:
1. **Source identity and purpose-right:** named authoritative BTCUSDT USD-M OI/forced-event feed or qualified archival supplier, exact schema/contract unit/coverage/correction/lineage, controlling allowed nonproduction research ingestion+storage+derived use (not inferred from an endpoint).
2. **Real causality/PIT:** independently recorded original complete arrival, immutable payload/vintage/hash, monotonic receiver clock with UTC-safe-high bound, durable write/ACK, native sequence/gaps, first legally eligible decision tick; period-end, close time or retrospective ZIP checksum **cannot** replace this.
3. **Mechanism/increment:** pre-outcome version of truly independent `Z` and same eligible `X` base, time-aligned no-leak matched original episodes, provenance of missing/invalid signals, and falsification when `Z` adds no robust conditional information. Distinguish observed aggregate OI from inferred liquidations.
4. **Full economic utility:** authentic executable bid/ask/size, latency/impact/spread, fee-role and filters, stop gaps, quantity held at Funding/Mark settlement, rejects/nonfills/partials/ACK, NO_ACTION/blocked capital, risk/liquidation and marked terminal wealth; hypothetical maker queue remains unidentifiable from public historical tape.
5. **Independent effectiveness:** eligible-calendar and source-missingness counts, predeclared cluster/week support, power against minimum net economic effect, multiplicity accounting across the entire inherited search family, unchanged exposure/holdout mask and separate prospective independent review. No fixed event-count heuristic is a power proof.

This is an **information-source frontier**, not proof of directional prediction or a recommended policy; a new ML predictor built only on legacy `X` would not create `Z`, restore first-known timestamps, or identify own fills. A hypothetical marketable/taker policy could remove *passive queue* uncertainty but still requires rights, actionable quote/latency/impact/cost and full portfolio accounting; it is a different economic estimand, **not** a permitted T6 repair.
