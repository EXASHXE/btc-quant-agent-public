bar's high or low can no longer authorize a LIMIT fill timestamp at bar open.

## 2. Exact defect and old unsafe path

The old LIMIT branch consulted a completed bar's `low` for BUY orders or `high`
for SELL orders. On a touch/through it assigned
`max(earliest_fill_ms, bar.open_time_ms)` as the fill time and charged a maker
fill at the limit. The extremum established only that a crossing occurred
somewhere in the interval; it did not timestamp the crossing. Consequently, a
future intrabar extremum could be backdated to the open and appear before a
1 ms expiry, funding settlement, stop/target, or terminal boundary.

This was symmetric across BUY/SELL and exact-touch/through branches. Checking
the fabricated timestamp against expiry did not repair the causality error,
because the timestamp itself had been derived from information unavailable at
that time.

## 3. Repaired causal model

### Observable marketable-at-open

The first executable price whose timestamp is actually known may authorize an
opening LIMIT fill when all of the following hold:

1. the order has arrived by the observation;
2. the observation is at the bar's open timestamp;
3. BUY limit is at or above the executable price, or SELL limit is at or below
   it; and
4. the executable timestamp is within the inclusive expiry boundary.

The caller may supply an executable opening quote only as a validated
price/timestamp pair. That explicit quote is used only when its timestamp equals
the bar open. Otherwise the existing opening-price contract uses `bar.open`.
No later `high`, `low`, or bar suffix is consulted to establish opening
marketability. The fill occurs at the observable executable price, is classified
as taker liquidity, uses the taker fee, and remains capped by the reserved
`max_fill_notional`.

### Unresolved intrabar touch

When the order is not marketable at the observable open, a later OHLC
touch/through has no timestamp. The execution model therefore returns an
unfilled result with the typed rejection reason
`LIMIT_INTRABAR_TOUCH_AMBIGUOUS_NOT_TESTABLE` and ambiguity-window metadata.
The simulator promotes that result to `AmbiguousEntryRejectionError`, a subtype
of the common `AmbiguousExecutionRejectionError`, before any portfolio, ledger,
funding, exit, or terminal mutation.

For an exact touch where the declared queue probability is below certainty, the
pre-existing queue non-fill remains a plain non-fill. A through, or a touch that
would otherwise claim certain execution, is rejected as ambiguous. No path is
moved to bar close, converted to MARKET, or assigned a favorable timestamp.

Arrival after open cannot consume the opening observation or earlier extrema
from that same bar. Gap/open crossings remain executable because the crossing
is observable at a timestamp, rather than inferred from a later extremum.

## 4. Timestamp and expiry contract

The lifecycle is now explicit:

`observation -> decision -> order submission -> exchange arrival/fill -> terminal`

`decision_timestamp_ms = signal_timestamp_ms + decision_latency_ms` and
`order_timestamp_ms = decision_timestamp_ms + order_submission_latency_ms`.
The new submission latency defaults to zero, preserving the historical default
identity and timestamp behavior. When nonzero, it is bound into execution
identity/provenance, and acceptance verification recognizes the optional field;
old artifacts without it continue to mean zero.

Expiry remains the existing inclusive TIF contract:

- `expiration_ms = order_timestamp_ms + time_in_force_ms`;
- `fill_time == expiration_ms` is valid;
- `fill_time > expiration_ms` never fills.

An unresolved extremum does not receive a synthetic time merely to apply this
comparison; it fails closed.

## 5. Funding and downstream safety

If funding lies inside the possible intrabar touch window, the OHLC bar cannot
show whether the position existed at funding time. The ambiguous entry is
rejected before position creation, so it earns or pays no fabricated funding.
It also cannot trigger a same-bar stop, target, or terminal close, and cannot
alter holding time or exit ordering.

G09's no-funding lifecycle creates no synthetic funding event. Its simulator
regression binds the golden timestamps directly: observation `1000`, decision
`1001`, order `1002`, fill `2000`, terminal `3000`. When a real funding event and
fill share a timestamp, the existing `FUNDING_BEFORE_FILL` tie-break remains in
force; the tie-break does not invent a funding event when none exists.
