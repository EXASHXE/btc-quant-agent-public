## 1. Batch Objective & Unifying Semantic Invariant

Batch B-A converges three tightly coupled correctness requirements into one unified concept:
$$\text{information\_set}(t)$$
A component acting at time $t$ may only observe and execute against information proven causally available at or before $t$:

$$\text{market\_observation\_availability} \le \text{signal\_observation\_timestamp} \le \text{decision\_timestamp} \le \text{order\_timestamp} \le \text{fill\_timestamp}$$

with the equal-time event ordering invariant:
$$\text{FUNDING\_BEFORE\_FILL} \quad \text{when } t_{funding} = t_{fill}$$

## 2. Component Repairs & Findings Closure

### 2.1 B02 / P7 H3 — Causal Liquidity for Opening Fills
- **Defect**: Opening MARKET and marketable LIMIT-at-open orders previously accessed the current bar's completed volume ($V_{bar}$ formed at $t_{close}$) to calculate market impact and participation slippage at $t_{open}$, leaking future volume across the opening execution boundary.
- **Repair**:
  - Refactored [`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/execution_model.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/economic/execution_model.py) and [`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/simulator.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/economic/simulator.py).
  - In `ExecutionModel`, opening taker execution uses strictly causal liquidity available at or before fill time (via `CausalLiquidityObservation` bound to order/signal metadata).
  - Suffix-volume invariance: Post-fill candle changes (volume, high, low, close) leave opening fills, prices, fees, slippage, and notional completely invariant.
  - Fail-closed adverse policy: When causal liquidity is missing or unverified, an explicit conservative adverse fallback or typed refusal (`INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY`) is enforced; completed current-bar volume is never used at open.
  - Completed bar volume is only checked for close-time fills or historical completed-bar trades.
  - Consumed A04 Golden: `D-H3-FUTURE-VOLUME-INVARIANCE` strictly verified.
  - Preserved: B01 observable marketable LIMIT-at-open behavior, unresolved touch ambiguity rejection (`D-B2`), max-slippage bounds, fee models, and funding order.
- **Closure Status**: **`B02_H3_CLOSED`**

### 2.2 B03 / P7 H4 — Formal Candle PIT Availability Boundary
- **Defect**: The formal economic pipeline previously dropped the `closed` state and availability timestamp during serialization and validation, allowing incomplete candles (`closed=False`) to share formal economic identity with completed candles and bypass point-in-time checks.
- **Repair**:
  - In [`src/btc_quant_agent/domain.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/domain.py), added `closed: bool = True` and `available_at_ms: int | None = None` to `Candle` with validation for boolean types, finite OHLCV, valid bounds, and positive availability timestamps.
  - In [`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/acceptance_verifier.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/economic/acceptance_verifier.py), unified `bind_runtime_market_data` and formal candle verification:
    - Enforces `closed=True` for completed inputs; rejects `closed=False` inputs.
    - Validates product identity, interval match, chronological ordering, continuous coverage without gaps, absence of duplicate/overlapping timestamps, finite values, and $t_{available} \le t_{decision}$.
    - Legacy candles without availability metadata fail formal verification rather than being silently upgraded.
    - Preserved replayability of historical closed data without contaminating event time with current wall-clock freshness.
  - Consumed A04 Goldens: `D-H4-CLOSED-FALSE-NOT-COMPLETE`, `PIT-01-CLOSED-POSITIVE`, `PIT-02-PRODUCT-MISMATCH`, `PIT-03-INTERVAL-MISMATCH`, `PIT-04-OVERLAP`, `PIT-05-GAP`, `PIT-06-NONFINITE`, `PIT-07-NOT-YET-AVAILABLE` all strictly verified.
- **Closure Status**: **`B03_H4_CLOSED`**

### 2.3 B08 — Clock-Domain & Configuration Fail-Closed Behavior
- **Defect**: Mixing monotonic elapsed time with wall-clock time in timeouts, allowing silent fallback on invalid or missing config paths, and permitting permissive defaults for typoed sections.
- **Repair**:
  - Implemented [`src/btc_quant_agent/time_boundary.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/time_boundary.py) providing canonical clock boundaries (`MonotonicClock`, `SystemClock`, `bounded_skew_check`, `ReceiptTimestamp`).
  - In [`src/btc_quant_agent/service.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/service.py) and [`src/btc_quant_agent/execution/service.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/execution/service.py), migrated lease/timeout/retry loops to monotonic counters (`time.monotonic()`), preventing NTP jump/rollback vulnerabilities.
  - In [`src/btc_quant_agent/config.py`](file:///src/btc_quant_agent/config.py):
    - Explicitly requested missing config paths (`BTC_QUANT_CONFIG`) fail immediately with `FileNotFoundError` instead of falling back to default configuration.
    - Malformed TOML raises `ValueError` / `TOMLDecodeError`.
    - Typo/unknown top-level sections and invalid fields in critical sections fail validation.
    - Environment overrides follow deterministic documented precedence and validation.
    - Execution defaults remain permanently `disabled`.
- **Closure Status**: **`B08_ACCEPTED`**
