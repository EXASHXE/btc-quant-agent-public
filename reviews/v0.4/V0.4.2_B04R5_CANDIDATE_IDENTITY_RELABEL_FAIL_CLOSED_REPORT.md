## Root cause and repair

B04R4 rejected direct RANDOM_BENCHMARK consumption but did not bind a candidate's
producer identity back to frozen protocol authority. EconomicRunResult also
allowed its outer identity to disagree with the originally persisted identity.
Recomputing outer run/result hashes did not resolve that semantic inconsistency.

The canonical EconomicRunResult constructor now requires exact full-dictionary
equality between `identity.to_dict()` and `accounting.formal_run_identity`, after
freezing accounting. Missing or changed embedded identity fails with ValueError.
This covers role, producer, bundle/signal/decision-input hashes and every other
identity field. execute_bound_run already embeds the identity before construction,
so there is no circular construction dependency.

The shared `_validate_candidate_run_binding` funnel retains the CANDIDATE role
requirement and explicitly rejects CANONICAL_RANDOM_BENCHMARK_V1 producer identity.
For a nonempty signal-set digest, both identity and protocol producer contracts
must exist and match by VersionedIdentity equality (ID/version/content hash).
Both supported protocol representations, VersionedIdentity and producer contract,
are normalized to that same existing identity type. Authority is never inferred
from bundle contents. This protects benchmark-suite construction and qualification.

Existing role-aware replay/persisted verification remains unchanged: relabelling
both outer and embedded producer fields to the candidate producer still cannot
make a random replay bundle match candidate producer authority. Random trials
retain their random producer and role, and cannot authorize candidate evidence.
Empty-signal/CASH candidates retain the existing no-producer-contract allowance.
