## 3. Supported Product, Sizing, and Risk Scope

- **Supported Instrument**: BTCUSDT linear perpetual with one-way netting semantics.
- **Position Direction**: Long (+1) or Short (-1); simultaneous opposite positions are forbidden.
- **Position Sizing Modes**:
  1. `FIXED_NOTIONAL`: Quantity computed as $\min(\text{target\_notional}, \text{equity} \times \text{max\_leverage}) / \text{price}$.
  2. `RISK_FRACTION`: Requires explicit positive `stop_loss_pct`. Quantity computed as $\min(\text{equity} \times \text{risk\_fraction} / \text{stop\_loss\_pct}, \text{equity} \times \text{max\_leverage}) / \text{price}$. Fails closed with `ValueError` if stop loss is absent or non-positive.
  3. `VOLATILITY_SCALED`: Requires explicit finite positive `current_atr` passed causally via signal metadata. Quantity computed inversely scaling with ATR percentage. Fails closed with `ValueError` if ATR is missing, zero, negative, or non-finite.
- **Risk Scope**:
  - Finite positive initial cash required.
  - Zero margin liquidation or multi-asset cross-margin engines are introduced (unsupported; linear perpetual one-way isolated accounting only).

## 5. Pending-Order Exposure Reservation Rule

With causal execution latencies (P2/P3), orders may become pending fills scheduled for future simulation timestamps.

**Chosen Reservation Rule: Conservative Pre-allocation & Post-fill Verification**:
1. **Slot Reservation**: Each pending open fill (`OPEN_LONG` or `OPEN_SHORT`) is counted toward `max_open_positions`. Candidate orders are rejected if:
   $$\text{active\_open\_count} + \text{pending\_open\_count} \ge \text{max\_open\_positions}$$
2. **Gross Notional Reservation**: Committed gross exposure includes both active positions and all pending open fills:
   $$\text{committed\_gross} = |q_{\text{active}}| \cdot P_{\text{current}} + \sum_{\text{pending}} q_{\text{pf}} \cdot P_{\text{pf}}$$
   A new order is rejected pre-submission if $\text{committed\_gross} + \text{order\_gross} > \text{max\_gross\_exposure\_usdt}$.
3. **Post-fill Verification**: Upon fill maturity (both immediate and pending execution), actual fill notional is re-checked against $\text{max\_gross\_exposure\_usdt}$ and $\text{max\_open\_positions}$. If slippage or adverse price drift causes a hard cap breach, the fill is rejected fail-closed and dropped from the ledger.

## 6. Drawdown-Halt Semantics

1. **Causal Tracking**: Peak equity is initialized to `initial_cash` and updated monotonically using only causal portfolio equity:
   $$\text{peak\_equity} = \max(\text{peak\_equity}, \text{current\_equity})$$
   $$\text{dd\_pct} = \frac{\text{peak\_equity} - \text{current\_equity}}{\text{peak\_equity}}$$
2. **Evaluation Points**: Evaluated after every trade settlement (realized PnL recognition), funding cashflow settlement, and at each bar's close mark-to-market.
3. **Sticky Halt**: Once $\text{dd\_pct} \ge \text{max\_drawdown\_stop\_pct}$, `drawdown_halt` transitions to `True` and remains permanently `True` for the duration of the simulation replay.
4. **Asymmetric Actionability**:
   - New order submissions and pending open fills are strictly blocked.
   - Existing open positions retain full ability to exit via Stop Loss, Take Profit, Trailing Stop, Holding Duration Expiry, or Reversal Exit.

---

## 7. Market-State Availability and Fail-Closed Semantics

1. **Spread Filter**: `current_spread_bps` is read from `signal.metadata["spread_bps"]` or defaulted to 0.0. If `current_spread_bps > max_spread_bps` or is non-finite / negative, the signal is rejected.
2. **Volume Filter**: `current_volume_usdt` is causally sourced from `signal.metadata["volume_usdt"]`, `bar.quote_volume`, or `bar.volume * bar.open`. If $0 < \text{current\_volume\_usdt} < \text{min\_volume\_usdt\_15m}$ or volume is zero/non-finite, entry is rejected.
3. **Regime Filter**: If `allowed_regimes` is specified, `current_regime` must be present in `signal.metadata["regime"]` and must belong to `allowed_regimes`. If absent, empty, or unlisted, entry fails closed.
4. **No Future Lookahead**: No regime, spread, volume, or volatility is ever inferred from future candles.
