# BTC Quant Agent v0.4.1F — Passive Benchmark Provenance Report

**Date:** 2026-09-10  
**Branch:** `v0.4`  
**Scope:** B5-P only — persisted `PASSIVE_PERPETUAL` provenance binding  
**Baseline:** `a3123aaab0de24fcc1f03669eac2a0c5dd07c767`

## 1. Problem statement and root cause

P6 already content-addressed the passive benchmark and independently replayed its
ledger arithmetic against the persisted candle payload. That proved that the
accounting was internally consistent, but not that it came from the economic models
declared by the comparison contract.

The passive record did not retain replayable preimages for the fee, execution, and
funding model identities or the funding-event input set. Its `same_cost_model`,
`same_execution_model`, `same_funding_model`, and `same_terminal_policy` diagnostics
were persisted booleans. The acceptance verifier checked those claims only indirectly
through the record hash and therefore could not distinguish an internally consistent
replay produced with a different fee, fill, timing, funding, or terminal model.

## 2. Repair

Formal passive accounting now carries a versioned `passive_provenance` envelope with:

- the fixed passive entry/holding/exit policy and order types;
- canonical fee, execution, and funding model preimages;
- the exact canonical funding-event sequence;
- terminal-policy and metrics-contract preimages; and
- a formal run identity derived from the candidate identity, with a distinct passive
  policy hash and the passive replay's completeness.

`validate_passive_benchmark_provenance()` performs the acceptance check. It reuses the
B5-R critical identity comparator, binds the passive identity to the candidate and
comparison contract, verifies every model preimage against its declared SHA-256,
verifies the exact dataset evidence identity and bytes, reconstructs the passive
entry/holding/funding/exit ledger, and compares the complete expected accounting to
the persisted accounting. It then recomputes the legacy `same_*` diagnostics instead
of trusting them.

The passive generator and verifier use one deterministic replay primitive so the
policy has one canonical implementation. Verification remains independent of all
persisted fills, fees, quantities, timestamps, funding cashflows, accounting totals,
and comparability booleans: these are reconstructed from bound model and data inputs.

## 3. Enforced invariants

1. Dataset evidence ID, content hash, interval start/end, observation count, single
   product scope, and candle bytes match the candidate and comparison contract.
2. Economic-policy, cost-model, execution-model, funding-model, terminal-policy,
   metrics-contract, input-contract, protocol, capital, code-revision, and comparison
   identities match through the reused B5-R critical-field binding.
3. Fee/execution/funding/metrics preimages hash to those declared identities; the
   execution model also binds the exact fee-model hash.
4. The passive policy opens one long market position from the predefined first-candle
   start using full initial capital and the declared fill/cost model.
5. Holding admits only the canonical funding sequence plus the passive entry and, when
   required, terminal exit. Funding cashflows are recalculated from quantity, mark,
   rate, and the declared funding model.
6. `REQUIRE_FLAT` schedules one market exit from the penultimate close into the final
   candle and recomputes its latency, fill, slippage, fee, and timestamp.
7. The complete ledger, curves, exposure, round trips, metrics, identities, provenance,
   and diagnostics must equal the deterministic replay. Hidden or altered events fail.

Pre-repair P6 files are not rewritten and the P6 public dataclasses and schema version
remain readable. A legacy passive artifact that lacks the new replay preimages cannot
authorize a new economic promotion: acceptance fails closed rather than silently
trusting historical `same_*` booleans.

## 5. Adversarial matrix

Each attack recalculates the passive result ID, benchmark suite ID, qualification
result hash/ID, local evidence hashes, and attestation references before entering the
registry. The fee/fill/timing/funding/terminal cases use complete alternate passive
replays, so their ledgers are mathematically self-consistent.

| Attack | Independent rejection boundary | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Add `0.01 USDT` to the passive entry fee via a valid alternate fee-model replay | Fee preimage does not hash to the comparison cost identity | PASS — registry rejected |
| Change passive fill price via a valid nonzero-slippage replay | Cost/fill provenance differs from the declared models | PASS — registry rejected |
| Change passive entry timestamp via a valid nonzero-latency replay | Execution preimage does not hash to the comparison execution identity | PASS — registry rejected |
| Remove a funding settlement via a valid replay with an empty funding set | Funding-event-set hash differs from the candidate run | PASS — registry rejected |
| Modify the passive execution identity and rehash all enclosing artifacts | Reused critical identity binding disagrees with candidate/comparison | PASS — registry rejected |
| Change terminal treatment to `MARK_TO_MARKET_OPEN` with a valid open-position replay | Provenance and accounting terminal policy disagree with `REQUIRE_FLAT` | PASS — registry rejected |

The dedicated P6 suite result after these additions is `39 passed`.

## 7. Remaining risks and non-claims

- Integrity depends on canonical JSON and SHA-256 collision resistance, as does the
  existing P6 evidence system.
- The supported formal scope remains one `BTCUSDT` linear perpetual product and the
  existing P6 terminal semantics; this repair does not broaden the architecture.
- Generator and verifier intentionally share the canonical passive replay primitive.
  This removes semantic drift but does not eliminate common-mode implementation bugs;
  the adversarial suite and execution-model unit tests are the current controls.
- Historical artifacts without replay preimages remain available for audit but cannot
  be used for a new promotion under this repaired fail-closed boundary.
- Synthetic profitability is not evidence of strategy profitability. H39 remains
  sealed, and no alpha research or execution authorization is introduced.
