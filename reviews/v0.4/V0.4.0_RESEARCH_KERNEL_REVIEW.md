# BTC Quant Agent v0.4.0 Research Kernel & Economic Validation Review

## 1. Executive Summary

This deliverable concludes the foundation milestone of **BTC Quant Agent v0.4.0: Research Kernel & Economic Validation**.

Prior to v0.4, research and statistical validation protocols (culminating in the frozen v0.3.26 baseline) operated under tight coupling between prediction and execution: signals bundled fixed bracket prices and notionals, backtests assumed instantaneous price-touch fills without queueing or latency, and transaction costs were estimated statically rather than modeled through evolving cash balances.

v0.4.0 resolves these structural issues by introducing a decoupled, modular pipeline from research evidence to economically validated trading policy:

$$\text{Data} \longrightarrow \text{Input Contract} \longrightarrow \text{Information Signal} \longrightarrow \text{Statistical Validation} \longrightarrow \text{Trade Policy} \longrightarrow \text{Economic Simulation} \longrightarrow \text{Qualification}$$

### Strict Boundary Certification
- **Zero Alpha / Feature / Parameter Search**: No search, data mining, or parameter tuning was conducted.
- **Zero H39 Unblinding**: Hypothesis H39 blind ledger, validation rules, and unblind gatekeeper remain completely untouched and unblinded.
- **Zero Forward Label Inspection**: No future returns or labels were read for candidate evaluation.
- **Zero Live / Testnet Execution**: Live execution remains strictly `DISABLED`.
- **Zero Modification of Historical Artifacts**: All deliverables and manifests across v0.3.x remain immutable.

## 2. Architectural Changes

### 2.1 Research Contract Module (`src/btc_quant_agent/research_contract/`)
- Introduced `ExperimentMetadata`, `FeatureDefinition`, `PredictionTarget`, and `EvaluationMethod`.
- Explicitly mandates that every future alpha candidate must define:
  1. `input_contract`: Causal prerequisites and completeness criteria.
  2. `feature_definition`: Mathematical formulation and causal window bounds.
  3. `prediction_target`: Horizon, continuous target definition, and reference timing.
  4. `evaluation_method`: Formal statistical test (FWL conditional OLS with Bartlett HAC and FWER control).
  5. `economic_policy`: Bound `TradePolicy` configuration.
  6. `cost_model`: Bound fee and slippage model.
  7. `benchmark`: Comparative benchmarks.
  8. `decision_status`: Life-cycle state (`PROPOSED` $\to$ `REGISTERED` $\to$ `STATISTICALLY_QUALIFIED` $\to$ `ECONOMICALLY_QUALIFIED`).
- Provided `ResearchContractRegistry` with persistent JSON storage without modifying legacy registries.

### 2.2 Decoupled Information Signal & Trade Policy (`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/`)
- **`InformationSignal` (`signal.py`)**:
  - Represents pure directional information ($+1, -1, 0$), magnitude/z-score strength, confidence interval, and target horizon.
  - Inviolate invariant: Carries zero execution semantics (no stop loss, no take profit, no notional, no fee estimates).
- **`TradePolicy` (`policy.py`)**:
  - Formalizes execution rules independently:
    - `EntryRule`: Signal strength thresholds, market vs. limit order type, limit offset bps, time-in-force.
    - `ExitRule`: Percentage stop loss, percentage take profit, trailing stop, holding duration decay exit, and signal reversal exit.
    - `PositionSizing`: Sizing algorithms (`FIXED_NOTIONAL`, `RISK_FRACTION`, `VOLATILITY_SCALED` with ATR).
    - `RiskBudget`: Maximum gross portfolio exposure, position limits, drawdown halting.
    - `MarketStateFilter`: Spread thresholds and regime gates.

### 2.3 Economic Validation Layer (`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/`)
- **`FeeModel` (`fee_model.py`)**:
  - Parameterized maker/taker fee schedules (e.g. 2 bps maker, 5 bps taker).
  - Multi-regime slippage: `ZERO`, `FIXED_BPS`, and `SPREAD_AND_IMPACT` (half-spread crossing plus volume participation impact).
- **`FundingModel` (`funding.py`)**:
  - Point-in-time perpetual funding settlement cashflow calculations:
    $$\text{Cashflow} = -\text{PositionQuantity} \times \text{MarkPrice} \times \text{FundingRate}$$
  - Window accumulation tracking settlements occurring strictly during position holding duration.
- **`ExecutionModel` (`execution_model.py`)**:
  - Strictly disaggregates four sequential timestamps:
    $$t_{\text{signal}} \le t_{\text{order}} \le t_{\text{fill}} \le t_{\text{exit}}$$
  - Accounts for decision latency ($\Delta t_{\text{decision}} = 500\text{ms}$) and exchange network latency ($\Delta t_{\text{exchange}} = 150\text{ms}$).
  - Rejects naive price-touch-equals-fill: limit orders require price to trade through with queue fill uncertainty; expired limit orders cleanly return `LIMIT_EXPIRED_UNFILLED`.

### 2.4 Unified Portfolio Simulation & TradeEvent (`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/`)
- **`TradeEvent` (`trade_event.py`)**:
  - Standardized atomic transaction event: `timestamp_ms`, `action`, `price`, `quantity`, `fee_usdt`, `funding_usdt`, `position_after`, `cash_after`, `realized_pnl_usdt`.
- **`Portfolio` (`portfolio.py`)**:
  - Real-time continuous cash, position, and mark-to-market equity accounting.
- **`EconomicSimulationEngine` (`simulator.py`)**:
  - Discrete event replay evaluating trade policies, managing exits, funding settlements, and equity curves.
  - Emits `SimulationSummary` containing gross PnL, net PnL, fees, funding, drawdown, Sharpe ratio, win rate, and profit factor.

### 2.5 Comparative Benchmark Suite (`src/btc_quant_agent/economic/benchmarks.py`)
- **Benchmark A**: Cash / No Trade (Capital Preservation, zero volatility).
- **Benchmark B**: Passive BTC Buy-and-Hold (captures underlying market beta).
- **Benchmark C**: Randomized Entry Baseline (Monte Carlo randomized entries matching trade policy holding durations).
- **Joint Qualification**: An alpha candidate qualifies economically only if net PnL exceeds both capital preservation and the randomized entry benchmark.

## 7. Remaining Risks & Next Recommended Steps

### Remaining Risks
1. **Queue Simulation Precision**: While the execution model rejects simple boundary touches, full Level-3 order queue simulation (matching order IDs against individual exchange book updates) is not modeled without full tick-by-tick market depth.
2. **Funding Rate Predictability**: In live trading, funding rates vary continuously over the 8-hour window; simulated funding assumes the settlement rate applies uniformly to open positions.

### Next Recommended Steps
1. **Integrate Microstructure Features into Research Contracts**: Formalize candidate features (e.g. M1–M8 once unblinded under authorized maturity) using `ExperimentMetadata`.
2. **Benchmarking Pipeline Harmonization**: Standardize automated qualification reports generating side-by-side tables comparing candidate Sharpe, Net PnL, and Max Drawdown against Cash, Passive BTC, and Random Entry benchmarks.
3. **Execution Latency Profiling**: Calibrate `decision_latency_ms` and `exchange_latency_ms` against empirical forward collector timing logs.
