## 1. Executive Verdict

Work Package **v0.4.1C Acceptance Repair** is **COMPLETE and ACCEPTED** (`P4_ACCEPTED`).

All 6 identified acceptance blockers (**AR1** through **AR6**) have been resolved with strict causal, risk, and execution contracts:
1. **AR1**: Spread evidence is explicitly required when binding; missing, NaN, infinite, or negative spread fails closed; no artificial `0.0` spread is fabricated; explicit `max_spread_bps = None` supports disabled spread filtering.
2. **AR2**: Current-bar completed volume is no longer accessed at bar open. Sizing and market state filtering source causal volume evidence from signal metadata or prior closed bars; future volume remainder has zero influence on open decisions.
3. **AR3**: Risk admission and pending exposure reservation operate exclusively ex-ante without peeking at future execution results. Orders derive worst-case price bounds from limit price or declared slippage bounds.
4. **AR4**: Accepted orders are never silently erased or dropped post-admission. Drawdown halts and risk changes block subsequent order admissions, not already committed pending fills. Unbounded market slippage models fail closed ex-ante if strict gross caps cannot be guaranteed.
5. **AR5**: `ambiguous_exit_handling` strictly enforces `CONSERVATIVE_STOP_FIRST` (stop loss executed first) and `REJECT_AMBIGUOUS` (raises deterministic `AmbiguousExitRejectionError`, failing closed without fabrication).
6. **AR6**: The inherited P3 execution gap is closed. All 6 economic exit paths (reversal, gap stop, stop loss, take profit, trailing stop, max holding) route uniformly through `ExecutionModel.simulate_order`. `Portfolio.apply_trade()` serves strictly as an atomic ledger commit.

Formal economic qualification remains intentionally marked `NOT_TESTABLE`. No P5 (protocol persistence), P6 (benchmark suite), or alpha/research code has been introduced.

## 3. Preserved P0–P4 Invariants

All baseline contracts established in P0 through P4 remain strictly preserved:
1. **Single Cash Source & Atomic Ledger**: Cash, equity, and margin accounting remain exclusively maintained in `Portfolio`; no sidecar ledgers or divergent state representations.
2. **Pending Fills Isolation**: Scheduled pending fills do not alter portfolio balances, equity, or positions until their exact causal fill timestamp.
3. **Asset Mismatch Rejection**: Signals targeting mismatched assets are skipped without portfolio modification.
4. **Explicit ATR & Stop Loss Validation**: Volatility sizing strictly requires finite positive ATR; risk fraction sizing strictly requires finite positive stop loss.
5. **Max Open Positions Bound**: Setting `max_open_positions = 0` or reaching the active + pending cap prevents any new order submissions.
6. **Pre-Order Gross Cap**: Orders exceeding `max_gross_exposure_usdt` are blocked ex-ante.
7. **Sticky Drawdown Halt**: Drawdown breaches permanently block all new order submissions.
8. **Allowed Regimes Enforcement**: Signals in unlisted or missing market regimes fail closed.
9. **Limit Offset Enforcement**: Passive offsets are computed and applied symmetrically.
10. **Monotonicity**: Cash flows, funding payments, and trade events strictly follow temporal monotonicity ($t_{\text{entry}} \le t_{\text{order}} \le t_{\text{fill}} \le t_{\text{settle}}$).
11. **Formal Economic Qualification Disabled**: Marked `NOT_TESTABLE` pending P5/P6.
