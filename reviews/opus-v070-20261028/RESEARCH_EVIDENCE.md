## What this evidence rules out — and does not rule out
- A trial that is negative under modeled charges does not become positive Alpha because another variant is less bad.
- An inadequate-support trial does not support positive or negative expected economics.
- Clean historical-source QC does not automatically prove receiver-known point-in-time observability, fill feasibility or exact charged funding.
- Fixed-horizon economic reports do not evaluate a fully specified dynamic strategy or user advisory lifecycle. Conversely, new dynamic exit flexibility is not a free statistical rescue for failed frozen entries.
- Research on one instrument, era and protocol cannot justify categorical rejection of all cross-sectional, carry, structural or risk-management strategies.
