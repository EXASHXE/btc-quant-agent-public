# Owner's Problem Definition — Product and Research Context
**Source-of-truth type:** Owner mandate and canonical docs, not an empirical performance result.

## Mission
Create an always-on **real-time cryptocurrency market-opportunity and position-management advisory system**. It should scan sufficiently liquid perpetual futures, identify candidate opportunities in a timely way, explain actionable LONG/SHORT entries with specific zones and confirmation conditions, provide structural invalidation and protective stops, and manage the user's **confirmed actual positions** after entry.

Advice should adapt to current price/trend structures, volume, volatility, liquidity, exchange derivatives state and eventually reliable news and event risk. The system should publish relevant, non-spammy risk alerts and propose to HOLD, tighten protective stop, partially reduce, exit, cancel an unfilled setup or request reconciliation. It must never claim a plan was filled merely because market price touched an entry/stop.

## Explicit Owner clarifications
- Targets must not be generated merely as fixed `2R` or by arbitrary ATR multiples described as verified resistance/support; they may be structural regions or explicitly specified open-ended trailing approaches.
- A position need not be liquidated at a universal 4, 8, 12 or 24-hour profit-taking deadline. Risk, thesis expiry, stale inputs and finite scientific observation still matter.
- Economic and technical indicators should help identify market opportunity, entry quality, trade feasibility and risk, not just create an opaque score.
- Old fixed-horizon strategy findings must remain intact, but their protocols should not define all future product behavior.
- No automatic trading, private account modification or live/testnet order execution is authorized.
- Future news monitoring is a goal; no trustworthy integrated live news-risk gate is established.

**These are product requirements, not hypotheses proved to generate net trading profit.**

## Fundamental success dimensions
1. **Operational / product:** source freshness; causal timestamps; real quote versus candle distinction; explicit data-quality UNKNOWN; usable opportunity yield and timing; plan lifecycle; user-reported fills versus verified fills; risk notification correctness and delivery; replay and recovery; human utility.
2. **Economic / scientific:** credible selection edge, costs and funding, realized and modeled economics (clearly distinguished), capital time occupancy, competing opportunities, regime/asset robustness, statistical uncertainty, exposure leakage, complete lifecycle accounting, prospective evidence.

A robust but unprofitable advisor is not demonstrated Alpha. A high-return simulation with hindsight or unfilled entries is not a usable trading product.
