# PART E — Residual limitations

## 18. Explicitly record non-claims

A GO does not mean the simulator is a perfect exchange model.

At minimum assess and document residuals such as:

- no liquidation/margin-engine realism claim unless actually implemented;
- OHLC-supported execution is not tick-accurate;
- market impact/slippage is a model, not exchange truth;
- local advisory-lock/filesystem guarantees are not distributed consensus;
- current publication locking is POSIX/Linux-oriented (`fcntl`), if still true;
- SQLite compatibility primarily verifies declared schema/column/version boundaries, not a universal SQL type/constraint proof;
- a quarantined OS/DNS worker may outlive the caller deadline even though caller behavior and retry amplification are bounded;
- M6 verifier performance/duplicate-work concern if still present.

Residual limitations are acceptable only if they cannot falsify current formal claims and their severity is clearly bounded.
