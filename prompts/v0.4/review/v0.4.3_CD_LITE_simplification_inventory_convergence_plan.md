## 2. Purpose

Design the smallest high-value simplification program that materially reduces active maintenance surface while preserving or strengthening research validity.

Do **not** mechanically reproduce the full historical C01-F02 DAG. Re-evaluate the repository at the repaired-kernel baseline and compress the useful convergence work into two implementation stages unless evidence requires a different split:

```text
C-lite: semantic/core convergence
D-lite: archival + application-surface convergence
```

Target outcome:

- one active semantic owner for each formal economic concept;
- substantially fewer active research runners and duplicated utilities;
- preserved historical evidence and reproducibility boundary;
- simpler diagnostics / CLI / API surface;
- no weakening of PIT, causal time, replay, provenance, qualification, anti-p-hacking, persistence, H39, or Final Holdout controls;
- no alpha search or strategy tuning during refactor.

A rough 20%-30% reduction in active maintenance surface is desirable only if justified by measured current inventory. Do not force a percentage target by deleting useful controls.
