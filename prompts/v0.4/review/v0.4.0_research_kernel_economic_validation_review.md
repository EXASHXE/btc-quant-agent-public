## Primary objective

Determine whether v0.4.0 actually provides a scientifically and economically coherent foundation for future BTC strategy qualification, rather than merely a set of plausible abstractions with passing synthetic tests.

The review must answer:

1. Are signal, policy, execution, cashflow and qualification semantics genuinely separated?
2. Is portfolio PnL accounting correct for LONG and SHORT positions?
3. Are fees, slippage and funding applied with correct sign, timing and notional semantics?
4. Can the simulator produce economically false-positive results because of unrealistic fill assumptions?
5. Are benchmarks comparable on capital, risk, exposure and timing?
6. Does the research contract remain generic enough for future experiments, or does it accidentally hard-code H39/FWL assumptions?
7. Does v0.4 preserve all v0.3.26 blindness / immutable-history guarantees?
8. Is the new foundation minimal and reusable, or has it already introduced unnecessary framework complexity?

## 4. Execution-model audit

Review:

`economic/execution_model.py`

The central question is whether the implementation avoids the old `price touched => filled` semantic error.

Audit:

- MARKET fill semantics;
- LIMIT fill semantics;
- trade-through requirement;
- queue / fill uncertainty;
- partial fills;
- limit expiry;
- candle-only ambiguity;
- latency;
- spread/slippage application;
- timestamp ordering;
- impossible fills using information unavailable at order time.

The current defaults reportedly include fixed 500 ms decision latency and 150 ms exchange latency. Treat these as assumptions, not verified facts. Determine whether they are safely parameterized and clearly labeled as scenario assumptions.

If candle OHLC is insufficient to establish ordering between entry, stop and target, the simulator must not silently choose a favorable sequence.

Create synthetic adversarial cases where both stop and take-profit are reachable in the same bar and inspect behavior.

## 10. Benchmark audit

`economic/benchmarks.py`

Evaluate whether each benchmark is apples-to-apples.

### Cash

Confirm initial capital and zero-risk semantics.

### Passive BTC

Check whether passive BTC comparison uses comparable capital/risk and transaction-cost treatment.

### Random entry

Check:

- number of trades;
- direction distribution;
- holding-time distribution;
- exposure duration;
- market-state availability;
- overlap constraints;
- cost treatment;
