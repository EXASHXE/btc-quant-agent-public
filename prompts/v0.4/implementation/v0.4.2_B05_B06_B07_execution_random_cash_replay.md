
## 16. CASH reconstruction source

CASH must be reconstructed from authoritative comparison inputs:

- initial capital
- interval boundaries
- canonical metric/equity timestamps required by the supported metrics contract
- terminal policy only insofar as it applies to a no-position vehicle
- product/benchmark identity where required

If candidate equity timestamps are currently used only as a convenience, replace that authority with an independently derivable canonical timestamp source or explicitly verify the timestamps against the canonical interval/metrics contract before use.

### B07-T7 wrong kind/vehicle/random fields
