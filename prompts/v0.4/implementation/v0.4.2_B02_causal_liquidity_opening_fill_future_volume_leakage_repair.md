## 2. Defect owned by B02

### P7 H3 — future completed-bar volume leakage

The opening execution path can price impact/slippage using a current bar's completed `volume` (or an equivalent quantity only known after the bar finishes), even when the MARKET or marketable LIMIT fill occurs at the bar open / before that volume has accrued.

That is a causal violation. The fill is allowed to depend only on information actually available at or before its execution timestamp.

The formal invariant is:

> **Holding the complete information set available at the fill timestamp fixed, modifying only the bar suffix after the fill timestamp MUST NOT change the opening fill price, impact/slippage, fee basis, execution cost, fill/rejection decision, or any downstream ledger/portfolio result attributable to that opening fill.**

A current bar's eventual completed volume cannot satisfy that invariant merely because it is stored on the same OHLCV row.

B02 owns the production closure of this defect class for all affected opening taker paths, including:

1. opening `MARKET` fills; and
2. the B01 marketable-at-open `LIMIT` path.

A repair that only fixes MARKET while leaving B01's new opening LIMIT path dependent on future volume is incomplete.

## 4. Required causal liquidity contract

Implement the smallest canonical repair that makes liquidity/impact inputs causal and auditable.

### 4.1 What may influence an opening fill

Liquidity used for impact/slippage may come from either:

1. a **timestamped observed liquidity input** whose observation/availability time is known and is `<= fill_timestamp`; or
2. when such a causal observation is unavailable, an **explicitly declared conservative/adverse scenario** that is part of the execution contract/provenance and does not derive from future bar suffix information.

The exact internal type/API may follow the current architecture, but the semantics must be explicit and testable. A valid observed-liquidity representation should carry enough information to prove, at minimum:

- the liquidity quantity/proxy used by the impact model;
- its observation/event timestamp;
- when it became available to the simulator, if distinct;
- source/provenance or scenario identity sufficient for replay/acceptance binding;
- validation that it is finite, positive where required, and causally available for this fill.

Do not create two competing liquidity semantics for MARKET and marketable LIMIT. Prefer one canonical execution-price/impact path around the existing frozen interfaces.

### 4.2 Forbidden fallbacks

For an opening fill, do **not** silently use:

- completed current-bar `volume`;
- later bar volume or later-bar-derived averages;
- current-bar high/low/close suffix information;
- a spread fabricated from future or unavailable data;
- zero impact merely because causal liquidity is missing;
- a favorable default;
- an invented timestamp that makes a future value appear available earlier.

If causal observed liquidity is missing, the declared conservative/adverse scenario must be deterministic, explicitly identified, and bound into the relevant execution identity/provenance. It must not be tuned against research outcomes in this task.

### 4.3 Time semantics

At the point impact/slippage is computed:

```text
liquidity_observation_timestamp <= liquidity_availability_timestamp <= fill_timestamp
```

or use the repository's equivalent authoritative time ordering if observation and availability are represented differently.

Any observation known only after the fill must be unusable for that fill. Time-travel input must fail closed; it must not merely generate a warning.

### 4.4 MARKET and marketable LIMIT parity

Both opening paths must consume the same causal liquidity contract:

- MARKET BUY / SELL
- B01 marketable-at-open LIMIT BUY / SELL

B01's LIMIT observability, executable quote, TIF/expiry, taker classification, fee classification, reserved notional cap, and ambiguity handling are not B02 redesign targets.

### 4.5 Max-slippage and no-fabricated-spread semantics

Preserve the already-repaired max-slippage contract and no-fabricated-spread behavior.

B02 must not weaken a max-slippage rejection by changing denominator/sign/threshold semantics, and it must not invent bid/ask spread information to compensate for missing causal liquidity.

### 4.6 Provenance / replay identity

If execution artifacts, qualification evidence, or acceptance verification presently bind the execution model, then the causal-liquidity mode used by an opening fill must be covered by the same authoritative identity/provenance surface.

At minimum, it must be impossible to:

- run with observed causal liquidity and later relabel it as fallback/adverse;
- alter a bound liquidity quantity/timestamp/source while preserving a supposedly equivalent verified execution artifact;
- use a future completed-bar quantity while claiming the causal input contract was satisfied.

Preserve backward compatibility only where the old artifact is semantically unambiguous. Do not silently treat a legacy missing field as proof of causal liquidity. If an old schema cannot establish causality, fail closed or explicitly route it through the declared conservative scenario according to the current compatibility policy.

Do not redesign B04/B05 replay bundles here; only bind what B02 requires in the existing execution/acceptance surfaces.
