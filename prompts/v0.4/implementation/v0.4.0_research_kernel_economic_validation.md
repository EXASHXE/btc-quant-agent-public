## Main objective

Establish:

Research Candidate
→ Information Evidence
→ Trade Policy
→ Economic Replay
→ Qualification Decision

Every future candidate must answer:

1. Does it provide incremental information?
2. What is the explicit trading policy?
3. How is execution modeled?
4. What is net performance after fees/slippage/funding?
5. What benchmark is it compared against?

## Step 3: Separate Signal and Policy

Do not assume signal equals trade.

Create separation:

Information Signal
        ↓
Trade Policy

Policy should describe:

- entry_rule
- exit_rule
- holding_period
- position_size
- risk_budget
- allowed_market_state

---

## Step 4: Economic Validation Layer

Create:

src/btc_quant_agent/economic/

Minimum components:

### fee_model
Support:
- maker fee
- taker fee
- slippage

### funding
Support:
- funding timestamp
- funding rate
- holding duration

### execution_model
Separate:

signal timestamp
order timestamp
fill timestamp
fill price

Do not use price-touch-equals-fill assumptions.
