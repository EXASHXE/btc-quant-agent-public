## 8. Information-rate feasibility

- usable asset-hours/days;
- source completeness;
- number of independent-ish clusters after common-factor/dependence adjustment;
- venue/instrument coverage;
- prospective event-opportunity upper/lower bounds derived from source
  availability or structural candidate definitions without reading protected
  performance outcomes;
- expected effective sample-rate ranges under conservative dependence factors.

Do not calculate strategy returns to rank architectures.

Do not claim N assets = N independent samples.

## 10. Feasibility scoring

Score each candidate campaign architecture on a fixed 1–5 scale for:

1. source-history depth;
2. PIT reconstructability;
3. provenance cleanliness / freeze-ability;
4. effective information-rate potential;
5. holdout independence quality;
6. engineering reuse;
7. implementation effort (reverse scored: less effort = higher score);
8. expected calendar latency to useful exploratory learning;
9. promotion path to rigorous Validation.

Weights:

```text
source/PIT/provenance: 30%
information rate/calendar learning: 30%
holdout quality: 20%
engineering reuse/effort: 20%
```

These scores are architecture/source feasibility scores, **not alpha scores**.

Provide evidence for every nontrivial score.
