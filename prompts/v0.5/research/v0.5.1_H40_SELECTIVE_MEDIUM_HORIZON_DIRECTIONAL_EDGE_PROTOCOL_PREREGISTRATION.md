## 3. H40 research question

Preregister H40 under the canonical research family name:

```text
H40_SELECTIVE_MEDIUM_HORIZON_DIRECTIONAL_EDGE
```

The central research question is:

> Does there exist a PIT-safe, preregistered conditional subset of BTCUSDT and/or ETHUSDT perpetual decision opportunities for which abstention-first directional decisions achieve stable out-of-sample directional precision and positive cost-adjusted expectancy, without forcing a prediction at every timestamp?

H40 is **not** a continuous LONG/SHORT classifier.

The admissible action space is exactly:

```text
LONG
SHORT
NO_TRADE
```

`NO_TRADE` is the default state.

The research funnel is fixed as:

```text
Regime
  -> Opportunity
  -> Direction evidence
  -> Expected excursion / MAE-MFE / cost-adjusted geometry
  -> Confidence / calibration gate
  -> LONG / SHORT / NO_TRADE
```

TP/BR or other magnitude/opportunity evidence may be used only as evidence that movement magnitude/opportunity exists. It must not be silently reinterpreted as directional evidence unless a separate preregistered directional hypothesis explicitly establishes that link.

## 5. Research architecture to preregister

The protocol must define each stage below as a separate semantic owner. Do not collapse all logic into a single opaque score.

### 5.1 Regime gate

Define the admissible regime families before any performance test. Candidate regime features may include PIT-safe, unprotected observations such as:

- realized volatility / range state;
- trend vs. range state;
- liquidity/activity state when independently available;
- BTC/ETH cross-asset state;
- funding/crowding state when backed by formal or otherwise PIT-safe evidence.

The protocol must distinguish regime classification from direction prediction.

### 5.2 Opportunity gate

Opportunity means "movement may be large enough to justify evaluation after costs", not "direction is known".

Allowed opportunity evidence may include preregistered PIT-safe families such as:

- range expansion / realized-volatility state;
- breakout-distance or compression/expansion state;
- TP/BR-style magnitude evidence that is available outside protected H39 outcomes;
- expected excursion / expected movement magnitude proxies.

Opportunity-gate output must be boolean or ordinal with a frozen semantic definition.

### 5.3 Direction evidence

Preregister a bounded list of directional hypothesis families. At minimum distinguish conceptually:

- trend / momentum continuation;
- breakout continuation;
- mean-reversion / failed-move reversal;
- BTC-ETH cross-asset confirmation/divergence;
- funding/crowding directional interaction, if PIT-safe data exists.

Do not allow unrestricted feature mining. Define a hard search budget before implementation, including:

- maximum number of hypothesis families;
- maximum variants per family;
- maximum hyperparameter combinations per variant;
- rules for dropping/replacing a failed family;
- whether combinations of families are allowed and the maximum combination depth.

The protocol must ensure the search budget is auditable and finite.

### 5.4 Excursion / geometry gate

Preregister how future movement is evaluated for research labels and economic geometry.

For every candidate horizon, define PIT-safe labels for at least:

- forward return;
- MFE;
- MAE;
- directional correctness;
- achievable reward/risk geometry under frozen execution/cost semantics where applicable.

Do not let the candidate choose its own favorable exit path after observing the future.

### 5.5 Confidence / calibration gate

The protocol must define how a candidate earns permission to emit LONG or SHORT instead of NO_TRADE.

Confidence may be a calibrated probability, bounded score, or monotonic evidence measure, but the calibration procedure must be fitted only on allowed training/calibration data.

Do not hard-code a target such as 99% NO_TRADE.

Coverage must emerge from preregistered confidence/economic thresholds and validation, not from manually forcing a desired abstention percentage after seeing outcomes.

## 10. Primary and secondary metrics

The protocol must explicitly reject overall classification accuracy as the primary metric because a selective system may abstain most of the time.

### Primary selection metrics

Preregister at least:

- coverage = non-NO_TRADE decisions / eligible decisions;
- directional precision conditional on taking a trade;
- LONG precision and SHORT precision separately;
- cost-adjusted net expectancy per trade;
- profit factor where sample size is adequate;
- trade count / effective trade count;
- MAE / MFE distribution;
- drawdown or adverse sequence statistic;
- calibration quality if probabilistic confidence is used;
- walk-forward stability across time and regimes.

### Secondary diagnostics

May include:

- raw forward-return distribution;
- opportunity hit rate;
- regime-conditional performance;
- asset/horizon contribution;
- turnover;
- exposure time;
- abstention reason distribution.

Do not let a secondary diagnostic override a failed primary gate after results are observed.

## 11. Low-frequency operating objective

The intended deployment profile is selective and low-frequency.

Record the operational target:

```text
approximately 2–5 high-quality accepted opportunities per week across the intended active scope
```

This is an operating objective, **not** a license to tune thresholds directly until the observed frequency lands in that band.

The protocol must define two distinct concepts:

1. **scientific/economic qualification** — edge and stability gates;
2. **operational suitability** — whether resulting frequency is usable.

A system with zero/near-zero coverage cannot qualify merely because its precision is undefined/high on a handful of observations. A high-frequency system cannot qualify merely by diluting a weak edge across many trades.

Choose explicit minimum effective-sample requirements before outcome evaluation.

## 13. GO / NO-GO criteria

The protocol must define concrete criteria **before** any candidate outcome evaluation.

Do not invent thresholds by looking at candidate results.

At minimum the final confirmation gate must require all of the following conceptually:

1. positive cost-adjusted expectancy on the untouched H40 confirmation partition;
2. no dependence on one single asset/horizon/regime unless the candidate was explicitly preregistered as that narrow scope;
3. directional precision materially above the preregistered no-edge/null reference at the selected coverage;
4. dependence-aware uncertainty that does not indicate the observed edge is purely noise;
5. adequate effective trade count;
6. no catastrophic MAE/drawdown/sequence behavior under the preregistered risk budget;
7. walk-forward consistency rather than one favorable window;
8. canonical-kernel replay remains economically positive after actual frozen cost/funding semantics;
9. no post-confirmation retuning.

The agent must choose numeric thresholds only from protocol logic, domain constraints, null behavior, and sample-size considerations **without reading H40 candidate outcomes**. Record the rationale.

If there is insufficient unprotected data to support defensible numeric thresholds, explicitly mark the affected gate `NOT_TESTABLE` rather than relaxing it post hoc.
