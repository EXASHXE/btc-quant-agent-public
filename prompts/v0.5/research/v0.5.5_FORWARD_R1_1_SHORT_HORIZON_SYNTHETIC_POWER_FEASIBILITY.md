1. global-null Discovery FWER one-sided 95% Monte-Carlo upper confidence bound
   <= 0.05;
2. single-candidate Validation type-I error one-sided 95% Monte-Carlo upper

   Monte-Carlo confidence bound on **full-pipeline success** >= 0.80 for every
   possible true coordinate;

   rather than conditioned away;

7. no real market outcome is used to tune the synthetic experiment.
