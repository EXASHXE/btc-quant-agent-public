# Severity rubric

## HIGH

Any reachable condition that can:

- authorize P3/Discovery without the intended independent authority chain;
- substitute economic source values while accepted hashes still verify;
- introduce look-ahead into production decision reconstruction;
- resurrect stale lifecycle authority;
- cross synthetic/test authority into production;
- access protected outcomes prematurely;
- change accepted scientific semantics without identity change;
- enable execution/write authority;
- invalidate the exact implementation-authority publication.

A HIGH blocks the next stage.

## MEDIUM

No immediate protected-authority bypass, but a real robustness/auditability defect that could make
future P3 publication unsafe or leave important semantics under-specified.

MEDIUM does not automatically authorize proceeding. Explain whether it must be fixed before P3
controller publication.

## LOW

Non-authoritative maintainability, diagnostics, duplication, test clarity, or defense-in-depth
improvement with no realistic authority/scientific integrity impact.

Do not inflate style issues.
