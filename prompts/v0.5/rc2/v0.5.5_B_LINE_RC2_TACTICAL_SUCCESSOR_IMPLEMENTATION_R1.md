## Objective

## Candidate budget

Maximum six executable candidates total, INCLUDING the frozen RC1 baseline.

Candidate families are limited to:

1. `SELECTIVE_CONTINUATION`
   - require trend persistence / slope agreement;
   - prefer controlled pullback in trend;
   - reject late/exhausted continuation.

2. `BREAKOUT_CONFIRMATION`
   - require structure transition / bounded bars-since-breakout;
   - require derivative confirmation where available;
   - avoid price/OI divergence inconsistent with direction.

3. `REGIME_VETO_OVERLAY`
   - preserve an otherwise valid directional signal but veto adverse range, exhaustion, volatility-shock or crowding states.

4. bounded deterministic combination of the above.

No black-box ML. No LLM execution policy. No per-symbol thresholds.
