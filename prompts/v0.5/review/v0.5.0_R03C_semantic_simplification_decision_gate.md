## Candidates to decide

Evaluate these independently. Do not bundle them merely because they all look redundant.

### C1 — `legacy_directional_role` in `research_registry.py`

Facts to verify:

- current registry entries still contain `legacy_directional_role = REJECTED` for opportunity components whose directional interpretation was rejected;
- this may encode historical research semantics distinct from current `role=OPPORTUNITY`;
- determine whether the field is genuinely redundant, can be represented without loss elsewhere, or should remain protected reproducibility metadata.

Attack/reproducibility question:

> Could removing it allow a future reader to incorrectly infer that retained opportunity evidence also retained the old directional conclusion?

### C2 — Two registry concepts

Compare:

- `research_contract/registry.py::ResearchContractRegistry` — formal P5 governance / append-only qualification-decision authority;
- `research_registry.py::ResearchRegistry` — operational/research component actionability registry.

Do NOT merge merely because both are called registry.

Determine whether complexity is primarily:

- naming/documentation confusion;
- API surface duplication;
- genuinely redundant persisted state;
- intentional separation of different authorities.

Evaluate options from least invasive to most invasive:

1. keep both, clarify names/docs only;
2. rename one public concept without changing persistence/authority;
3. extract a small common leaf type only if it removes duplicated semantics;
4. merge persistence/authority — presume forbidden unless overwhelming evidence proves equivalence.

Explicitly analyze whether a merge could let operational actionability state influence formal qualification/promotion authority.

### C3 — `SimulationSummary.round_trips`

Assess whether serialized `round_trips` is:

- an independent source of truth;
- a derived cache from atomic `TradeEvent`s;
- required for accepted evidence reproducibility;
- required by qualification gates, MAE/MFE, PF/expectancy, semantic goldens, or verifier cold replay;
- safe to recompute without changing identity hashes/artifact bytes/protocol compatibility.

Do not recommend removal if doing so requires broad artifact-version migration, changes accepted result hashes, or weakens independent accounting verification.

Consider three options:

1. KEEP persisted and verified;
2. retain persisted field but centralize derivation/validation code;
3. remove from current schema and reconstruct — only if migration/identity consequences are fully bounded.
