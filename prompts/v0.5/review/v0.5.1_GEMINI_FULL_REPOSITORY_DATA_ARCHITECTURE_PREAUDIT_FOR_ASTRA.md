# Gemini Long-Horizon Task — Full Repository + Local Data + Architecture Pre-Audit Package for Astra

## 0. Mission

Perform a **deep, repository-wide, evidence-grounded systems analysis** of the current `btc-quant-agent` project and its locally available data/runtime artifacts.

The goal is **not** to implement features, repair code, optimize a strategy, or make a trading recommendation.

The goal is to build the most complete and reliable **system fact base** possible for a later GPT-6/Astra full-project adversarial audit and, after that, a potential architectural refactor toward a validated-playbook / Trading Policy Engine design.

This task may be long. Do not optimize for token economy. Prefer completeness, precision, cross-checking, exact paths, exact symbols, exact hashes, diagrams, tables, and traceable evidence.
