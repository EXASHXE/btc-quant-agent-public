### D1 — signed log trend, both sides

For `W=4,h=4` or `W=12,h=24` on the ledger's asset:

```text
score_D1(t) = log(Close(B0(t)) / Close(BW(t)))
Q80 = numpy.quantile(abs(valid WF1_TRAIN scores), 0.80, method="linear")
```

Require at least 500 valid TRAIN score observations. Freeze Q80 before
calibration; do not recompute using calibration or validation. If
`abs(score) >= Q80` and score > 0, side = +1. If `abs(score) >= Q80` and
score < 0, side = -1. Otherwise side = 0 and `A[i,t]=0`. Equality with Q80
is eligible when the score is nonzero. Require complete, consecutive
`B0..BW`.

### D2 — Donchian breakout, both sides

For `(W,h)=(24,4)` or `(72,24)`, form
`U_W=max(High(B1)..High(BW))` and `L_W=min(Low(B1)..Low(BW))`;
**exclude B0** from the range. `Close(B0)>U_W` gives side `+1`;
`Close(B0)<L_W` gives side `-1`; otherwise abstain. Equality with either
boundary abstains. Require all reference bars complete and consecutive.

### D3 — prior-bar break, immediate close-back, both reversals

For `(W,h)=(24,4)` or `(72,8)`, form
`U_W=max(High(B2)..High(B(W+1)))` and
`L_W=min(Low(B2)..Low(B(W+1)))`. Define
`UpBreak=Close(B1)>U_W`, `DownBreak=Close(B1)<L_W`, and
`InsideBack=L_W<Close(B0)<U_W`. `UpBreak & InsideBack` gives side `-1`;
`DownBreak & InsideBack` gives side `+1`; otherwise abstain. Equality is
outside, not inside. No `High(B0)` breakout substitution is permitted.

### D4 — synchronized other-asset RET4 sign

```text
OTHER_RET4_SIGN(t) = sign(log(Close(B0_other(t)) / Close(B4_other(t))))
```

Every D4 child inherits its ledger parent event and side, whether parent is
RET4_Q80 or BO24. `CONFIRM` keeps the parent event only if the other RET4
sign matches the parent side; `DIVERGE` keeps it only if the sign opposes
the parent side. Zero other sign matches neither and both children abstain.
No one-hour other-asset candle direction substitution is permitted. All
D4 children have outcome horizon 4h.
