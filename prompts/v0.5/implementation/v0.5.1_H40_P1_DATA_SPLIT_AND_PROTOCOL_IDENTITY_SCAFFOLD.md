## 4. Allowed data access in P1

### 4.1 Allowed

P1 may access unprotected market artifacts only for:

- byte-level SHA-256 hashing;
- archive/checksum verification;
- file/schema inventory;
- product identity;
- interval identity;
- event/open/close/availability timestamps;
- row counts;
- duplicate timestamp detection;
- gap detection;
- coverage start/end;
- source provenance and retrieval metadata;
- construction of timestamp-only eligible/split arrays.

Reading raw bytes to hash an artifact is allowed even if the artifact contains price data. Parsing numerical market outcomes for research is not.

### 4.2 Forbidden in P1

Do not use OHLC/mark/funding/flow numerical values to derive:

- future direction;
- magnitude;
- profitability;
- features;
- thresholds;
- labels;
- candidate metrics.

Funding source artifacts may be inventoried and hashed, but no funding-direction or economic result may be calculated.

### 4.3 Source acquisition

Prefer already available repository/local unprotected artifacts and existing official checksum manifests.

If an exact required artifact is absent:

- do not substitute another source;
- do not loosen PIT semantics;
- do not infer its hash from an old report;
- record `MISSING` / `UNVERIFIED` / `NOT_TESTABLE` as appropriate.

Do not make large external downloads merely to make P1 pass unless the repository already has an explicit deterministic acquisition mechanism for that exact official source. If such a mechanism is used, record the exact official URI/archive identity/checksum and keep acquisition logically separate from outcome processing.

## 8. Exact timestamp and split manifest

Construct timestamp-only manifests from verified source timestamps, without computing market outcomes.

### 8.1 Base eligibility

Reserve:

```text
maximum feature lookback = 720h
maximum diagnostic endpoint = 24h
```

The timestamp universe must be derived deterministically from source coverage and gaps. No gap interpolation or boundary movement is allowed.

For pooled BTC/ETH scope, implement the BASE common trustworthy timestamp intersection. Preserve asset-specific missingness rather than forward-filling one asset from the other.

### 8.2 Frozen split schedule

Materialize exact timestamp arrays/counts and canonical hashes for the preregistered half-open partitions, including all purges:

- WF1 Train / Purge / Calibration / Purge / Validation;
- WF2 Train / Purge / Calibration / Purge / Validation;
- WF3 Train / Purge / Calibration / Purge / Validation;
- WF4 Train / Purge / Calibration / Purge / Validation;

- confirmation holdout `[2025-02-01T00:00:00Z, 2026-02-01T00:00:00Z)`, additionally restricted to timestamps whose complete 24h endpoint remains inside the source boundary.

Persist:

- exact timestamp list or deterministic compact representation sufficient for exact reconstruction;
- count;
- first/last timestamp;
- hash;
- exclusion counts by reason;
- source-manifest hash;
- protocol identity hash.

A coverage discrepancy must remove/mark timestamps; it must not shift boundaries to recover sample size.

### 8.3 No outcome exposure

- total budget never exceeds 168 complete configuration slots;
- dropped/invalid/NOT_TESTABLE slots are not recycled;

- exactly the BASE D1–D5 family authority and four allowed depth-two combinations;
- asset scope and one primary horizon are identity fields;

If the preregistration does not fully determine every slot without a later authorized enumerator, implement a capacity/budget ledger that can deterministically register the later predeclared rows and fail closed on unauthorized families. Do not outcome-tune the missing details.
