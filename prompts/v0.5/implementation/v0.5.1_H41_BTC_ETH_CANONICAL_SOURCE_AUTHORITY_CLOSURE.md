## Fail-closed decision

`PROTOCOL_SOURCE_SEMANTIC_CONFLICT`. In the checksum-matched January 2021 native
1h archive samples, `Open(B_k)` differs from `Close(B_{k-1})` in 559 of 743
consecutive BTC boundaries and 595 of 743 ETH boundaries. The frozen event/PIT
contract explicitly asserts these two marks are equivalent. The validator emits
counts and hashes only, never the compared prices.

Independently, the frozen authority states 2,184 canonical hours in the
`WF1_CALIBRATION` partition, while its stated half-open interval
`[2022-11-01T00:00:00Z, 2023-02-01T00:00:00Z)` contains 2,208 hours. Neither
the price-mark equivalence nor the partition size can be repaired by source
selection or an implicit protocol interpretation.

The local repository contains no complete paired official native 1h archive
set. A separate local research data tree contains the January 2021 BTC/ETH
native 1h samples used here, 61 ETH 1h monthly archives, 67 BTC 1m archives,
and only five BTC native 1h audit months. These are diagnostic inputs; their
historical manifest checksums match local bytes, but a current independent
download of the official `.CHECKSUM` files was unavailable. No complete
`H41CanonicalSourceReceiptV1` or joint source authority root is promoted.

Controller adjudication must resolve both frozen semantic conflicts. A future
source stage must then acquire and verify both complete official archive sets,
run the 16 invariants over the entire required interval, compare both paths
where available, and issue full-range receipts. This document grants no H41
implementation or real Discovery authority.
