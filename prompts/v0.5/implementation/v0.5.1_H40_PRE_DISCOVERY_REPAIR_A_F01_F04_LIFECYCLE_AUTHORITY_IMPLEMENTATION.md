# F04 implementation requirements

## Immutable service authority

Production service authority configuration is write-once after construction.

Do not add mutable setters that can swap accepted authority into an existing authoritative service.

A service/process created under superseded production authority must fail subsequent authoritative
operations after the in-process accepted anchor changes.

```text

```

```text

ACCEPTED_H40_P3_CONTROLLER_AUTHORITY_HASH
exact bound H40_DISCOVERY_RUN_GRANT_V1
```

A service that cached a formerly accepted value cannot keep using it after supersession.

## Hot republication

Hot authority republication within an already-authoritative service is unsupported.

The implementation/test semantics must make clear that a new accepted authority publication
requires new service/process construction for subsequent production authority.

## Synthetic isolation

Synthetic/test-only authority must remain a distinct domain and must not be promotable to
production by toggling a flag, passing a label, supplying a synthetic hash, or serializing and
restoring synthetic state.
