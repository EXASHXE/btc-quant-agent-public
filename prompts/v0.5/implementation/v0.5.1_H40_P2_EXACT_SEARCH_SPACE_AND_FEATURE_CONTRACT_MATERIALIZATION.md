## Feature-contract requirements

P2 may define feature semantics and parameter grids, but it may not evaluate feature values against future outcomes.

Every feature contract must specify:
- stable feature ID and version;
- semantic owner family;
- exact required raw/material inputs;
- lookback length;
- timestamp/availability rule;
- closed-bar rule (`close_time < decision t` where applicable);
- missing-data behavior;
- normalization/transform parameters;
- product/scope compatibility;
- whether cross-asset/funding authority is required;
- deterministic output schema;
- content hash.

No feature may depend on a future timestamp, label, forward-return endpoint, H39 result, Final Holdout, or unversioned historical research output.

Historical research artifacts may only motivate already-preregistered family semantics; they must not become dynamic feature-selection inputs.

## Candidate-lock boundary must remain untouched

P2 does not perform candidate selection.

Retain the preregistered first-lock boundary:
- search/fitting inputs: WF1 training only;
- calibration/discovery selection: WF1 calibration only;
- no WF1 validation, WF2-WF4, confirmation, H39, or Final Holdout information before candidate LOCK.

The P2 ledger should contain fields necessary to prove this later, but must not consume those outcomes now.
