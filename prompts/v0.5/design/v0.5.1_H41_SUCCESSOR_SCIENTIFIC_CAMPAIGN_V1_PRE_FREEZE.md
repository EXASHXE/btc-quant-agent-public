
# 9. Outcome and economic edge definition

For a candidate event at decision time `t` with action sign `s ∈ {-1,+1}` and horizon `h`:

```text
r_h = log(C[t+h] / C[t])
gross_directional_return = s * expm1(r_h)
```

Base friction:

```text
COST_PROXY_BASE = 12 bps round-trip
net_return_12 = gross_directional_return - 0.0012
```

Stress friction diagnostic:

```text
COST_PROXY_STRESS = 24 bps round-trip
net_return_24 = gross_directional_return - 0.0024
```

Only fully observable horizon outcomes inside the authorized Discovery outcome window are included.

No TP/SL/geometry assumption is part of H41 Discovery V1.

That is deferred until Playbook qualification after Candidate Lock.


after simultaneous multiplicity correction across the complete pre-outcome TESTABLE H41 candidate universe.


# 11. Dependence and simultaneous inference

H41 retains block-aware resampling because event outcomes overlap in time.

Proposed mechanism:

```text
source-hour aligned non-circular moving-block bootstrap
block length = 24 hours
replicates = 10,000
deterministic candidate-independent campaign seed family
```

For each TESTABLE candidate compute:

```text
point estimate = mean net_return_12
bootstrap centered distribution
```

Across the complete TESTABLE 20-candidate universe use a single-step or step-down **max-T / max-centered-statistic** simultaneous correction.

Primary pass condition:

```text
simultaneous one-sided 95% lower confidence bound > 0
```

Untestable candidates are frozen out of the testable universe before outcomes are read and recorded with their pre-outcome reason.

No outcome-dependent family deletion is allowed.

Astra must explicitly audit whether max-T, block length, and 10,000 replicates remain appropriate.

