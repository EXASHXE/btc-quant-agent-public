## 6A. Candidate-family relation classification

Every historical research/output surface that could matter to a proposed
protected interval must be classified as:

```text
candidate_family_relation:
EXACT
| SUBSTANTIALLY_EQUIVALENT
| RELATED_BUT_NON_ALIGNED
| UNRELATED
| UNKNOWN
```

Interpretation:

- `EXACT`: same or effectively identical candidate equation / endpoint /
  horizon / conditioning logic.
- `SUBSTANTIALLY_EQUIVALENT`: not byte-identical, but close enough that its
  observed historical performance could reasonably influence the current
  successor candidate family or tuning.
- `RELATED_BUT_NON_ALIGNED`: same broad market/feature domain, but outcome
  information is not directly informative enough to constitute protected-sample
  contamination.
- `UNRELATED`: materially different hypothesis family, feature domain,
  endpoint, horizon or research purpose.
- `UNKNOWN`: insufficient evidence to classify.

`UNKNOWN` fails closed only when the relationship is material to the proposed
protected interval **and** the information could have reached a principal with
successor scientific change rights. It must not be used as a generic substitute
for "some crypto research occurred."

For each alleged contamination route, the ledger must answer:

```text
1. What candidate or candidate family was evaluated?
2. What historical interval was evaluated?
3. What outcome/statistic was generated?
4. Was the outcome disclosed or accessible?
5. To whom?
6. Did that recipient have successor scientific change rights?
7. Could that information influence the current successor design?
```

## 7. Affirmative-cleanliness rule

A month is not certifiable solely because no result artifact is found.

The required standard is not proof of an impossible universal negative. It is:

> There is affirmative evidence that no **known material candidate-aligned
> outcome exposure route** capable of influencing successor scientific change
> rights contaminated the proposed protected interval, subject to an explicitly
> enumerated residual-risk model.

For `RETROSPECTIVE_HOLDOUT_CANDIDATE`, provide an explicit closure receipt
covering:

5. `known_exports_alerts_closed_or_non_material`;
6. `known_protected_campaign_overlap_resolved`;
7. `material_unknowns = []`;
8. explicit non-material residual risks.

must be recorded but must not be promoted automatically into contamination.
