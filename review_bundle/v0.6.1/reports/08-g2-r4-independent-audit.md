## Findings and limits

1. **The apparent economic contradiction is not a matched experiment.** W1/W2 are volatility-expansion entries over 35 consecutive score months with a continuous funded account; R3 A/B/C use different rules, 12 seasonal books, different clocks and funding proxies. The [comparability matrix](W1_W2_VS_R3_COMPARABILITY_MATRIX.md) classifies every cross-study entry/policy/horizon family. No numerical cross-study PnL delta was computed.
2. **R3's published NO-GO is supported.** All 27 STRESS account cells have N=5,34 or35, negative pooled and equal-year mean net bp, and weekly Bonferroni27 p=1. No cell meets the 50-trade gate. Unique common-eligible events are A43/B6/C51; exits/caps reuse these observations. The most favorable STRESS pooled mean is still -18.1497bp. This is an aggregate fact about the declared ex-funding proxy, not a universal statement about entry predictability. [R3_RESULTS](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/a69ccfdfd578bd0b7d3aa43d75959a659f969782/evidence/v0.6/b_line/g2_btc_exit_aware_r3/DEVELOPMENT_RESULTS_BASE_STRESS.json) (`rows`, `entry_counts`); [R3_MULTIPLICITY](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/a69ccfdfd578bd0b7d3aa43d75959a659f969782/evidence/v0.6/b_line/g2_btc_exit_aware_r3/MULTIPLICITY_SUPPORT_NEGATIVE_REPORT.json).
3. **No demonstrated cash/causal-clock defect explains the negative account result.** The seven reviewed R3 source files match the exact tested commit and receipt SHA256s. Five published synthetic Decimal ledgers independently conserve initial quantity and recomputed signed cash, including a short and genuine partial exits. The focused immutable engine suite was rerun: **61 passed, 0 skipped**, Python3.13.13/Linux, pytest8.4.2, numpy2.4.6. No source loader or historical scorer ran. [R3_MANUAL](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/a69ccfdfd578bd0b7d3aa43d75959a659f969782/evidence/v0.6/b_line/g2_btc_exit_aware_r3/MANUAL_DECIMAL_EXIT_LEG_AUDITS.json); [R3_TEST](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/3af438490da4497132a5c32c786752ca527e0d99/tests/test_v06_g2_btc_exit_aware_r3_engine.py).
4. **Execution realism remains limited.** Two invented deterministic probes reproduce disclosed minute-order rules. An open-crossed target can be discarded by stop-first; a time-cap open can close all residual quantity above earlier standing partial targets. The latter can be optimistic relative to a standing-target-first contract. These are frozen assumptions, not implementation drift. All54 published account rows record zero stop/target collisions, so that collision branch cannot explain the account negatives. Cap/standing-order incidence and paired-episode collision incidence are UNKNOWN from published aggregates. No historical replay was used to estimate their impact. [R3_ENGINE](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/3af438490da4497132a5c32c786752ca527e0d99/scripts/strategy_research/g2_btc_exit_aware_r3/engine.py#L351); [R3_PROTOCOL](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/a69ccfdfd578bd0b7d3aa43d75959a659f969782/docs/strategy_research/g2_r3/exit_aware_r3/EXPERIMENT_FROZEN_PROTOCOL.md#L87).
5. **Entry information has not been isolated.** Existing stop/target-dependent gross outcomes, post-flat MFE/MAE and policy contrasts do not estimate unconditional signed fixed-horizon returns versus matched non-events. Lag, weak prediction, sparse support and regime instability remain partially confounded. The [failure analysis](ENTRY_EDGE_FAILURE_MODE_ANALYSIS.md) separates observed facts from untested explanations.
6. **A distinct next trial is not presently admissible.** Generic reacceptance and OHLC sweep/reclaim would repeat failed definitions; a basis/flow mechanism needs new admissible source and availability evidence. A finite power screen fails at the observed sparse frequencies, and Consumer C still rejects late settled Funding. The [mechanism decision](BTC_STRATEGY_MECHANISM_PIVOT_DECISION.md), [design](NEXT_PROSPECTIVE_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md) and [integration review](FINANCIAL_PIT_INTEGRATION_OPEN_RISKS.md) state the conditions for reconsideration without authorizing a run.
