## 4. Causal E-RETRACE definition

At the final candidate confirmation only, the experiment creates a prospective limit at the already-known five-bar pullback extreme. Eligibility begins with the next 1m bar. A LONG fills only when a future 1m low reaches the limit; a SHORT fills only when a future 1m high reaches it. Fill price is exactly the limit and gap-through receives no improvement.

The event lifecycle is:

`PENDING_LIMIT -> FILLED -> OPEN -> STOP/TARGET/TIMEOUT`

The frozen 45-minute TTL, three-bar maximum age, original invalidation plus 0.25 ATR buffer, structural target, RR minimum 1.8, costs, funding treatment, position sizing, and 720-minute holding timeout remain unchanged. On same-1m ambiguity, stop has priority.
