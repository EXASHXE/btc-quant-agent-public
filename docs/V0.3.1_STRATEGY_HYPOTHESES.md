# v0.3.1 Strategy Hypotheses

These hypotheses are proposals for a separately pre-registered v0.3.2 experiment. None is implemented in v0.3.1, and development-set profit is not the ranking criterion.

## H1 — Trend Pullback target geometry is incompatible with the risk model

- Observed evidence: 404 TP candidates, 139 post-factor candidates, and 0 risk plans; 157 otherwise-surviving patterns lack a confirmed target.
- Mechanism: nearby confirmed targets and structure-based stops rarely leave net RR ≥ 1.8 after costs.
- Proposed minimal change: compare one pre-registered alternative target construction against the frozen target, without changing entry or stop.
- Expected trade-count effect: increase from zero to a measurable but still selective TP sample.
- Expected selectivity effect: unchanged pattern and factor selectivity; only priceability changes.
- Main overfit risk: selecting an ATR multiplier from development outcomes.
- Validation design: fixed multiplier chosen from market-mechanics rationale; report target reach, MAE/MFE, and outcome by year.
- Falsification: alternative targets still yield fewer than 20 development trades or destroy net RR realization.

Evidence categories: funnel + target/level + single-gate counterfactual.

## H2 — RR gate is conflating setup scarcity with execution economics

- Observed evidence: D8 count-only potential rises from 14 to 324 raw signals; RR removes 171/185 post-factor breakouts and all 139 post-factor TP candidates.
- Mechanism: entry-band reference, stop buffer, target distance, and costs combine at one terminal gate, hiding which component is binding.
- Proposed minimal change: decompose RR into gross geometry, fee/slippage, funding stress, and minimum-notional sub-gates before testing any threshold change.
- Expected trade-count effect: none in the diagnostic phase; a later single-component experiment could add a bounded sample.
- Expected selectivity effect: identifies whether rejected candidates are economically unpriceable or only cost-model sensitive.
- Main overfit risk: lowering RR to manufacture trades.
- Validation design: pre-register one component-level counterfactual and retain the 1.8 net-RR control.
- Falsification: no single component explains a stable majority of rejects across years.

Evidence categories: funnel + single-gate counterfactual + loss attribution.

## H3 — Correlated trend evidence creates score saturation without added discrimination

- Observed evidence: every confirmed decision is in the 100 score bin (14/14); volume is both a setup gate and participation credit, while trend is represented by regime, setup, 4h macro, and fixed 30/30 trend_structure.
- Mechanism: candidates surviving hard gates inherit full or near-full scores from evidence already required upstream.
- Proposed minimal change: test one pre-registered de-duplication—remove only the fixed trend_structure score while retaining every hard gate.
- Expected trade-count effect: depends on a predeclared rescaled threshold; should not be selected for profit.
- Expected selectivity effect: restore score dispersion and make marginal momentum/flow evidence visible.
- Main overfit risk: rescaling the threshold after observing the new distribution.
- Validation design: freeze normalization and threshold mapping before replay; compare calibration and rank ordering, not only P&L.
- Falsification: score dispersion remains saturated or ordering is unchanged.

Evidence categories: factor saturation + redundancy + funnel.

## H4 — Momentum removes disproportionately weak breakouts

- Observed evidence: A→B removes 11 trades: 8 losers and 3 winners, average −0.4441R; A expectancy rises from −0.0563R to B +0.1214R and losing streak falls 6→4.
- Mechanism: directionally aligned ROC and RSI admissibility filter late or exhausted breaks.
- Proposed minimal change: isolate ROC scoring from the RSI hard gate in a one-factor factorial test.
- Expected trade-count effect: roughly the observed 35→24 reduction under the current combined momentum layer.
- Expected selectivity effect: potentially higher win rate (observed 37.14%→41.67%).
- Main overfit risk: only 11 removed trades and regime clustering in 2021–2022.
- Validation design: pre-register ROC-only and RSI-only arms; no combinations beyond that declared factorial.
- Falsification: removal effect reverses across years or neither component explains it independently.

Evidence categories: ablation removal + loss attribution + redundancy.

## H5 — Participation/CVD may reject valid winners without sufficient compensating loss removal

- Observed evidence: B→C removes 3 trades—2 winners, 1 loser, average +0.6817R; expectancy falls +0.1214R→+0.0413R and win rate 41.67%→38.10%.
- Mechanism: candle CVD/volume confirmation may lag fast breakouts or measure noisy intrabar flow rather than durable participation.
- Proposed minimal change: separate Volume Z (already a setup gate) from CVD score contribution; retain the frozen setup volume gate.
- Expected trade-count effect: recover a small number of candidates.
- Expected selectivity effect: avoids double use of volume while testing CVD's independent value.
- Main overfit risk: conclusion rests on only three removed trades.
- Validation design: pre-register CVD-only incremental test and demand consistency by direction/year, not aggregate P&L.
- Falsification: CVD consistently removes more losers than winners in expanded forward data.

Evidence categories: ablation removal + redundancy + loss attribution.
