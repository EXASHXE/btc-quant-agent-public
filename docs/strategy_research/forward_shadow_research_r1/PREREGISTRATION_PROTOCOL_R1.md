### 2.2 Mechanism 1: P1_BASIS_REVERSION_V2_SHADOW
*Prospectively tests basis dislocation restoration between Last and Mark prices without relying on F3 historical thresholds.*

  $$\frac{|\text{Last}_{close} - \text{Mark}_{close}|}{\text{Mark}_{close}} > \max\left(5\,\text{bps},\, 2 \times \frac{\text{Ask}_{best} - \text{Bid}_{best}}{\text{Mark}_{close}}\right)$$
  Both $5\,\text{bps}$ ($0.0005$) and $2\times$ spread multiplier are ex-ante fixed constants.
- **Past Premium Shrink Requirement:**
  Normalised premium at decision time $t$ must have shrunk strictly towards zero compared to $t - 5\,\text{min}$:
  $$|P(t)| < |P(t - 5\,\text{min})|$$
  Where $P(t) = (\text{Last}_{close} - \text{Mark}_{close}) / \text{Mark}_{close}$.
- **Directional Assignment:**
  - **LONG:** Last is below Mark ($P(t) < 0$) and restoring upward ($P(t) > P(t - 5\,\text{min})$).
  - **SHORT:** Last is above Mark ($P(t) > 0$) and restoring downward ($P(t) < P(t - 5\,\text{min})$).

  30m high-to-low range $\frac{\text{High}_{30m} - \text{Low}_{30m}}{\text{Mark}_{close}} \le 300\,\text{bps}$. If exceeded, emits `NO_SIGNAL_ABNORMAL_VOLATILITY`.
- **Execution Rule:**
