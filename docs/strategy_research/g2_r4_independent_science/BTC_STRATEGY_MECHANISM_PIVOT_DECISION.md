## Why no trial is the primary decision

There is no demonstrated genuinely new, PIT-admissible mechanism with sufficient expected cluster support. R3 trend/reacceptance and OHLC reversal would consume further exposure without identifying a new state. Basis/flow is economically distinct but its source availability, Mark/carry, OI/quote identity and Consumer C integration are incomplete. Execution assumptions also need a separately authorized frozen ordering contract before a cost-complete trial.

The [prospective design](NEXT_PROSPECTIVE_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md) makes the information requirement concrete:21 endpoints with42 directional decision budgets, a20bp useful-effect null threshold and40bp planning alternative, with assumed150bp matched-residual SD, require about847 independent effective events per mechanism for80% idealized power on the20bp separation. Illustrative design effect2 raises required nominal count to1,694. A730-day window with24h event spacing and label drain caps enrollment at729 per mechanism: the proposed contract is structurally underpowered even before actual sparsity. These are planning assumptions, not measured variance or power guarantees. Historical raw W1/W2 signal rates would produce roughly52/69 per lead over24months **before** debursting/admission/matching if they persisted. New-state frequency is UNKNOWN. Do not initiate this underpowered design or loosen it after exposure.

An immediate trial would therefore lack a defensible source/power contract. The finite design is shelved and **DESIGN_ONLY_NOT_EXECUTED**, with no additional full-account backtest. Source procurement, integration repair, future observations and scoring need new Controller authority. We do not recommend a historical rescore, promotion of the least-negative cell, or a rollout based on CI.

## Conditions for later reconsideration

A separate Controller may reconsider only after receiving: (1) a demonstrably distinct feature-state definition and overlap/ablation rationale; (2) admissible future source receipts proving known-at, continuity and financing/execution semantics; (3) a fixed finite count/cluster/variance calculation that meets useful-effect power without loosening rules after outcomes; and (4) independent review of the preregistered entry-only protocol. These are necessary readiness conditions, not a second primary recommendation or authorization to collect data now.

The unresolved limitations do not support declaring R3 arithmetic corrupted or all entry information absent. The scientific audit can be complete while no next strategy trial is admissible. External review remains required; G2_ALPHA=NO_GO and TESTNET/LIVE/REAL_FUNDS=UNAUTHORIZED.
