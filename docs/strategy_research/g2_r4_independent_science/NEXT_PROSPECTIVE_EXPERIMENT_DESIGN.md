## Dependence, estimand and inference

Primary estimand per endpoint: equal-**active-month**-weighted mean of matched signed net event-minus-control increments, conditional on months with enrolled matched events. Let the outcomes-blind enrollment lock fix n_m>0 and M active UTC months; estimator=sum_m(sum_i(delta_i)/n_m)/M and event weight=1/(M*n_m). Month support/weights depend only on baseline enrollment, never returns. Empty months remain recorded with zero support and have no invented numerical mean. Require>=18 active months, both halves of the window and all originally enrolled labels/control labels cost-complete for any endpoint admission. Missing labels remain missing; no replacement, silent complete-case normalization or positive admission at that endpoint. Pooled event means are descriptive. The same fixed weights estimate absolute net economics for its required admission bound. This estimand is conditional on active months, not unconditional calendar/account return.

Keep all21 endpoint vectors, complete event/control tuples and cross-mechanism shared clusters together within frozen14-day calendar blocks, including empty blocks. A global block bootstrap retaining original month labels would frequently omit an active month and make adversarial bounds unbounded; it is not an accepted estimator here. Candidate fixed-design machinery instead retains n_m and weights: block score S_b=sum_i_in_b w_i(delta_i−observed_month_mean_m); shared independent standard-normal innovations Z produce xi_b=(Z_b+Z_(b−1))/sqrt(2), and theta*=theta_observed+sum_b xi_b*S_b. No further sample-size scaling or resampled month count is applied. Primary adjacent-block multiplier covariance is1/2;14-day sensitivity uses xi=Z and56-day sensitivity uses four consecutive Z divided by2. Absolute-net scores follow the same construction.

**Calibration unresolved:** centering month means with only2–3 active blocks can substantially understate variance. Require>=2 active14-day blocks per active month but do not treat that minimum, finite covariance or a bootstrap draw count as valid coverage. A separately authorized pre-exposure synthetic calibration must test nuisance-centering bias, fixed weights, sparse clusters, heavy tails, missing labels, persistent dependence and both-gate power. Until marginal coverage and joint power pass, this is candidate inference machinery, not a usable confidence estimator. [Bücher and Kojadinovic's dependent-multiplier research](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.3930v4) supplies methodological background under stationarity/mixing assumptions, not validation of this BTC weighted-month adaptation. This caveat is our inference from the design, not a result asserted by that paper.

The predeclared path-contained matching rule addresses direct label/control overlap across blocks, but not shared30-day features or persistent regimes. Dependence beyond56days or unresolved spillover prevents precision claims. Require104 calendar weeks coverage and>=60 active weekly clusters per mechanism. Debounce is not proof of independence. Undefined/degenerate/nonfinite inference is reported as method insufficiency, never silently dropped or zero-imputed.
