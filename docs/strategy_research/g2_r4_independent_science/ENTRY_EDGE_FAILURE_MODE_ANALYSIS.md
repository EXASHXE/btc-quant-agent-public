## What would discriminate the explanations

The [finite prospective design](NEXT_PROSPECTIVE_EXPERIMENT_DESIGN.md) fixes one event cohort and1/2/4/8/12/18/24h return labels before collection. Matched controls address measured differences in event timing/direction versus ambient trend and volatility; setup-to-confirmation motion describes lag without hypothetical early entry. Fixed positive **post-known-at** latency and prespecified same-event positive-delay diagnostics measure delay sensitivity, without a randomized causal claim. Executable Last/quote returns, Mark diagnostics and genuinely known funding remain separate. Cluster-adjusted power and a fixed comparison budget prevent selecting whichever horizon, side, year or cost looks best.

If a preregistered upper confidence bound is below the smallest economically useful net/control increment, the mechanism can be rejected at that scale. A wide interval crossing zero is inconclusive. A mean failing costs is an economic failure under the specified assumptions, not proof of absence of every possible entry signal. Currently source readiness and attainable sample power fail first, so no additional historical or prospective outcome collection is authorized by this audit.
