## Distinct clocks and financial meaning

A settled Funding event has an economic settlement time, a provider publication/event time, a receiver-known-at time, and a Mark witness with its own provider and receiver clocks. They cannot be collapsed into one timestamp. Late receipt can legitimately describe a past economic settlement; its rate must not become a feature or spendable accounting fact before it is known. Conversely, a fresh Mark at late receipt is not proof of the old settlement Mark. Signed cash for long/short exposure is −signed_position×rate×settlement_Mark. Exact same-clock entry/exit ordering and remaining quantity must be explicit.

R3.1's retrospective observed stream improves ex-post cost attribution but does not establish historical publication known-at; completed1m Mark is an approximation to exchange settlement Mark. R3 has no actual funding/Mark and reports NET_EX_FUNDING_PROXY. Neither BOOST hardening nor a later CI run retroactively repairs those scientific source limitations. ## Reproducible Consumer C witness

Let settlement T=1728720000000 and receipt T+3600000. A settled payload uses settlement_at_ms=T, rate_type=SETTLED_REALIZED and a correctly hashed envelope with provider_event=T, observed_wall=T+1h and available_at=T+1h. C's predicate rejects because avail_ms!=T. The published synthetic test explicitly expects FUNDING_PIT_INVALID. This is an invented test clock from source, not a2024 market-data read. [BOOST_TEST](https://github.com/EXASHXE/btc-quant-agent-public/blob/fdec02d5d7cf9d80ae45fc36c56af5008df003d6/tests/test_v06_architecture_lean_r1_financial_pit.py#L377).

Backdating available_at to T would bypass the equality but falsely claim receipt knowledge and violate PIT. Dropping funding or treating rejection as zero cost is also invalid. A separately authorized Consumer C contract change would need to distinguish economic settlement from observation, preserve the receipt clock, reconcile historical exposure at T exactly once, establish a verified settlement Mark or cost-incomplete state, and prevent decisions before known-at. Late revisions, epoch reconnects, duplicate receipt/valuation enrichment and same-clock partial exits need synthetic contracts. No such repair, new materialization, merge or operational integration test was performed in R4.

The published BOOST negative witness report claims its local hardening cases pass and explicitly leaves C unresolved. R4 inspected the immutable source and synthetic witness; it did not substitute that report for newly executed BOOST tests. The newly executed R4 tests cover only frozen R3 engine/synthetic bookkeeping.
