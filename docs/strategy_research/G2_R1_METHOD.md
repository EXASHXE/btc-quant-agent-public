unleveraged Binance spot candidates, three families and four fixed maximum horizons.
No grid or short strategy is registered. No parameters, time windows, cost scenarios,
or ranking rules may change after its first verified remote push.

DEVELOPMENT, including prior exposure, never an independent holdout. Source identity
and decision-time availability are separate requirements. Public archive existence
is not evidence that an executor historically received each signal input on time.

receipts. If absent, build and test the sidecar but do not compute archival trading
profits or replace receipt timestamps with a hypothetical latency.

were inspected read-only. Its derivative inputs and wider benchmark universe are
not available under this task's source manifest, so the frozen Tactical diagnostic
is explicitly not computable. No existing policy or schema is changed or approximated.

order-book execution. Spot identity makes funding zero. Trades have a fixed ATR risk,
next-minute execution, a bounded holding time and stop-first collision resolution.
