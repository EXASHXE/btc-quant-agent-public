> Common definitions: closed epoch-UTC 1m bars form 1h and 4h bars; missing minutes invalidate bars. ATR20 is the arithmetic mean of 20 hourly true ranges using prior closes. EMA20/EMA50 on 4h closes use SMA seeds then alpha 2/(N+1); require at least 60 completed 4h bars. ER12 is absolute 12-bar close displacement divided by sum of absolute consecutive displacements, with zero denominator ineligible. Direction sign d is +1 LONG, -1 SHORT. Thresholds below are design constants, not fitted to R2 returns; approval may amend them only before the later outcome campaign freeze.

**S+/S−:** LONG: last completed 4h close > EMA20 > EMA50; EMA20 now > EMA20 three 4h bars earlier; ER12 >=0.35. SHORT mirrors inequalities. Last 1h close breaks the highs/lows of the prior three complete 1h bars, its signed close-open body >=0.5 ATR20, and its range <=2 ATR20. Distance from 4h EMA20 <= one 4h ATR20 (same TR definition on 4h bars). Initial stop is decision close minus d×1.5 hourly ATR20. Hypothesis: persistent structure plus contained confirmation avoids first-spike false positives.

**R+/R−:** Same 4h EMA direction ordering, without ER or EMA-slope gate. A completed 1h close breaks the preceding 24 hourly high/low by >=0.25 ATR20, signed body >=0.5 ATR20. Freeze boundary and ATR at breakout. Only the next three closed hourly bars may confirm: LONG low touches [boundary-0.25ATR,boundary+0.25ATR], close >=boundary+0.10ATR and close>open; SHORT mirrored. First confirmation only. A preceding hourly close beyond boundary by 0.25ATR in the wrong direction cancels the event. Stop LONG is minimum low from breakout through confirmation minus 0.25 frozen ATR; SHORT is maximum high plus 0.25ATR. Hypothesis: rejection and recovery after breakout filters chasing.

> For both families: hourly decision at exclusive hour end+60s assumed availability; earliest fill at following minute open (hour end+120s). At decision ATR20/close must be 30–250bps. At fill, signed gap from decision close >0.25 ATR20 or an invalid stop vetoes entry; adverse and favorable gaps use the same absolute-gap veto. Risk distance at actual modeled entry must be 30–250bps. Fixed stop is not moved to pass economics. Target is entry+d×2×initial risk distance; maximum hold is exactly 4h or12h from fill. Stop/target/time exit, no trailing, partial TP, averaging down or new position within an open same-symbol position. If a1m bar touches both stop and target, SL resolves first. An open beyond the adverse stop executes at that worse open; an ordinary stop executes at stop, then adverse friction. Target executes at its fixed price even if the open gaps favorably beyond it, with taker friction and no gap overcredit. Time exit uses the first minute open at/after entry+maxhold; stop at that open takes precedence. All exits use adverse tick rounding and friction exactly once; no same-bar hindsight fill. After exit, four-hour cooldown; structural family additionally needs a newer completed 4h bar than its preceding entry. Retest event identity is symbol/direction/breakout-hour; at most one entry per event per variant, even after cooldown. Gap-veto/no-fill never retries the event. Every WAIT/veto/overlap is retained.

A signal's completed-hour boundary is t, assumed decision availability t+60s, earliest fill t+120s: 120s from closed signal, 60s from decision. A retest adds one to three full closed hours after breakout before this latency; slow recovery is a materially different entry time. Execution acknowledgment is another60s after the economic fill under accepted R1.1. These are causal modeled clocks, not observed receipt or actual fill guarantees. Completed bars can suggest persistent price acceptance, but do not reveal signed flow, genuine sweeps, liquidations or informed counterparties. ## Cost algebra, break-even and geometry

Nominal entry-notional basis: base transactions=2×(6 fee+2 half-spread+3 slippage)=**22bp**; stress=2×(12+4+6)=**44bp**, before funding/tick effects. All are scenario proxies, not authenticated historical Binance execution. Actual accepted accounting debits fees on each executed notional, applies spread/slippage adversely once and uses adverse tick rounding; exit notional variation means exact realized cost need not equal this entry-notional idealization. No private fee tier, maker discount, leverage uplift or favorable funding credit is inferred.

For idealized fixed outcomes, stop d bp, gross target2d bp and fixed total cost c bp:

`E[net bp] = p(2d−c) + (1−p)(−d−c) = 3dp−d−c`.

Set expectation to zero: **p_BE=(d+c)/(3d)**. At d=100bp, c=22bp gives **40.67%**, c=44bp gives **48.00%**. Costs are0.22R/0.44R only for that100bp stop. Generally `E[R]=3p−1−c/d` only under fixed binary payoff/risk; variable-risk meanR cannot be inverted into notional fees or account return. Account equity is a separate funded MTM USDT book, without leverage multiplication.

Gaps/stop slippage can exceed−d, time exits replace the two-point distribution, tick/minimums vary costs, partial/no fills alter eligibility, funding varies with ownership/notional, isolated loss/kill and fold censoring alter policy population. Adverse stop effects raise the required gross performance; time exits can change it in either direction. A hit-rate among resolved stops/targets omits other exits. Use mean netbp/expectedR and funded equity/control metrics; no profit projection is made here.
