## Canonical timeline required before a later freeze

These are proposed amendments, not implemented or certified behavior. Use separate economic-event ledger (for ex-post MTM) and decision ledger (only available observations); every cashflow/mark must reconcile, but ex-post rates/marks cannot feed earlier sizing or risk exits.

| Stage | Proposed decisive ordering |
|---|---|
| Bar identity | 1m[t-60s,t), last publisher close t-1ms, exclusive endt. Epoch1h/4h aggregates require every completed constituent; no current incomplete bar. |
| Available features | Trade bars end+60s; proposed mark bars also end+60s (archival assumption, not live receipt). Record source event, assumed available, present retrieval separately. Unknown mark publication lag is not zero. |
