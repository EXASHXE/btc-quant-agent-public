## Concrete hypotheses and finite budget

Universe is fixed BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, Binance USDT-M PERPETUAL with USDT quote and margin. Apply identical rules to all three; never select the best asset. Priority is structural continuation first, retest second, but execute and disclose all eligible eight in one later campaign. Two families × LONG/SHORT × 4h/12h = eight all-in trials. Four unused slots are relinquished, not reserved for replacements. No training, fitted classifier, threshold grid, adaptive regime label, cross-asset ranking feature or LLM label is proposed.

Common definitions: closed epoch-UTC 1m bars form 1h and 4h bars; missing minutes invalidate bars. ATR20 is the arithmetic mean of 20 hourly true ranges using prior closes. EMA20/EMA50 on 4h closes use SMA seeds then alpha 2/(N+1); require at least 60 completed 4h bars. ER12 is absolute 12-bar close displacement divided by sum of absolute consecutive displacements, with zero denominator ineligible. Direction sign d is +1 LONG, -1 SHORT. Thresholds below are design constants, not fitted to R2 returns; approval may amend them only before the later outcome campaign freeze.

| Family | Hypothesis and exact proposed entry |
|---|---|
| STRUCTURAL_CONTINUATION | LONG: last completed 4h close > EMA20 > EMA50; EMA20 now > EMA20 three 4h bars earlier; ER12 >=0.35. SHORT mirrors inequalities. Last 1h close breaks the highs/lows of the prior three complete 1h bars, its signed close-open body >=0.5 ATR20, and its range <=2 ATR20. Distance from 4h EMA20 <= one 4h ATR20 (same TR definition on 4h bars). Initial stop is decision close minus d×1.5 hourly ATR20. Hypothesis: persistent structure plus contained confirmation avoids first-spike false positives. |
| CLOSED_RETEST | Same 4h EMA direction ordering, without ER or EMA-slope gate. A completed 1h close breaks the preceding 24 hourly high/low by >=0.25 ATR20, signed body >=0.5 ATR20. Freeze boundary and ATR at breakout. Only the next three closed hourly bars may confirm: LONG low touches [boundary-0.25ATR,boundary+0.25ATR], close >=boundary+0.10ATR and close>open; SHORT mirrored. First confirmation only. A preceding hourly close beyond boundary by 0.25ATR in the wrong direction cancels the event. Stop LONG is minimum low from breakout through confirmation minus 0.25 frozen ATR; SHORT is maximum high plus 0.25ATR. Hypothesis: rejection and recovery after breakout filters chasing. |

For both families: hourly decision at exclusive hour end+60s assumed availability; earliest fill at following minute open (hour end+120s). At decision ATR20/close must be 30–250bps. At fill, signed gap from decision close >0.25 ATR20 or an invalid stop vetoes entry; adverse and favorable gaps use the same absolute-gap veto. Risk distance at actual modeled entry must be 30–250bps. Fixed stop is not moved to pass economics. Target is entry+d×2×initial risk distance; maximum hold is exactly 4h or12h from fill. Stop/target/time exit, no trailing, partial TP, averaging down or new position within an open same-symbol position. If a1m bar touches both stop and target, SL resolves first. An open beyond the adverse stop executes at that worse open; an ordinary stop executes at stop, then adverse friction. Target executes at its fixed price even if the open gaps favorably beyond it, with taker friction and no gap overcredit. Time exit uses the first minute open at/after entry+maxhold; stop at that open takes precedence. All exits use adverse tick rounding and friction exactly once; no same-bar hindsight fill. After exit, four-hour cooldown; structural family additionally needs a newer completed 4h bar than its preceding entry. Retest event identity is symbol/direction/breakout-hour; at most one entry per event per variant, even after cooldown. Gap-veto/no-fill never retries the event. Every WAIT/veto/overlap is retained.

The 4h variants represent 2–6h; 12h variants represent 6–12h. No 24h variant is proposed: no evidence that these transient confirmations remain predictive that long. At verified eight-hour cadence a 24h position can intersect three settlements, compared with up to one in 4h or two in12h; 4bp adverse proxy therefore adds approximately12 versus4/8bp, on top of22bp trading cost. Faster cadence can be much worse. Duration reduces turnover per exposure-day but increases drift, common-shock and funding risk; dilution of one-time fees is not an economic rationale.

Cross-asset relative-strength designs are deferred, not additional trials: they require synchronized receipts, matched beta/common-shock controls and a separately frozen feature budget. True grid and range-proxy are excluded. PAUSE is first-class: zero trades, zero modeled trading PnL, capital held as USDT with no assumed yield; net R undefined. It can remain the preferred research action.

## Stress before entry; accounting and controls

All fills are taker, even targets; no maker discount, queue benefit or leverage multiplication. Per-leg base assumptions remain fee6bp, half-spread2bp, slip3bp; stress doubles every component. Source gate must verify publicly applicable standard fee schedule <=6bp or block this proposal rather than silently lower fees. Costs are scenarios until independently supported, not measured execution. Let C_s be 44bp +8bp for every possible settlement during max hold at the source-verified cadence + adverse tick-rounding bound. Unknown cadence uses each possible hour and makes all economics proxy-only. Missing tick metadata is ineligible. Reject unless fixed stop distance >=2C_s and target distance >=3C_s; these geometry checks are necessary feasibility conditions, not estimates of expected profit or success probability. Missing plausible spread/impact evidence precludes a supported positive shortlist. Candidate geometry must survive stress at entry, not be selected after a low-cost rerun.

At each trade report gross/net bps on entry notional, USDT, initial-risk R and portfolio equity return separately. Charge entry/exit fees on actual adverse modeled execution notional; do not both subtract spread cashflow and embed the same spread in price. Funding is signed quantity×settlement mark×rate, with positive rates paid by LONG, negative by SHORT. Actual settlement benefits may appear only in measured reconstruction, while selection stress grants zero benefits and charges max(2×adverse actual rate,8bp) per settlement. Base actual costs and base proxy costs are distinct scenarios; neither retroactively edits R2. Settlement-window ambiguity charges adverse funding when overlap is possible. Missing settlement identity or settlement-associated mark mapping means PROXY_STRESS_ONLY and DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY, never a positive promotion, provided validated1m mark history remains available for account MTM. Proxy funding uses quantity×max(entry, settlement-minute mark high) and4bp base/8bp stress at every possible interval (hourly if cadence unknown). Missing1m trade-price or mark-price path hard-stops economic computation; no close-price substitute for MTM.

Each candidate and each unconditional control owns an independent virtual1,000-USDT ledger; they never compete for capital or change another variant's observations. This is eight alternative experiments, not a combined8,000-USDT portfolio. Per-asset allocated ceiling333.333333333333. At entry notional is min(allocated ceiling,0.95×current positive equity/3) rounded down to lot, preserving a5% equity reserve; no leverage above1x aggregate within its book. Reject minimum-notional/lot failures. At every minute close, mark-to-market exposure must remain <=positive equity. Simultaneous entries are sized from the same pre-entry equity in canonical symbol order, with costs reserved before fills; residual budget failures are WAIT. If adverse MTM breaches ceiling, deterministic pro-rata reduction at next minute incurs fees; insolvency terminates. These capital-limit reductions are mandatory exits, not signal retuning. Mark-price MTM, USDT collateral and funding are ledgered; gaps may exceed initial risk and must not be clipped. Whole-account MTM at every completed minute mark close, capital locks, underwater duration, exposure-day returns and drawdown replace realized-exit proxy as primary portfolio diagnostics. This does not claim observed intraminute equity extrema. Minute reductions and stop exits due at the same open process exits first; update equity before new entries, never reuse released capital retrospectively. The10% drawdown stop liquidates the whole virtual book at the next minute open and disables it for the remainder of the campaign; losses/costs and subsequent WAIT slots remain in evidence.

At identical candidate event times, LONG and SHORT episodes use the same stop-distance, horizon, costs and admissibility gates; compare candidate to balanced 50/50 mean, and to same-direction matched episodes to distinguish opportunity timing from direction. These paired counterfactuals can overlap and must never be added into funded portfolio ROI. Separately funded unconditional hourly LONG/SHORT4h/12h controls use decision-close stops of1.5ATR20, target2R, the same common volatility/risk/cost/gap gates, cooldown, no-overlap and equity limits, with isolated books. Matched episodes use the candidate's realized entry-notional and symmetric initial risk distance, same market/cost/availability and stop/target timing; no aggregate equity constraint is invented for overlapping nonfunded episodes. PAUSE uses the same calendar. RC1 Tactical is historical failed context only, NOT_COMPUTABLE under mismatched perpetual inputs; no rerun. Controls are predefined null comparisons, never selectable ninth strategies. All horizon outcomes have separate identities; no trade crosses a fold or becomes another horizon's observation.

## Experiment hierarchy, selection and invalidation

Proposed later development windows: January2026 warm-up [Jan1,Feb1); February, March, April2026 [Feb1,Mar1), [Mar1,Apr1), [Apr1,May1). April was exposed as R2 warm-up; others have unknown indirect exposure until ledger audit. All three are nonprotected development, not blind releases. This is a proposed constant calendar, not permission to download. No calibration/fitting stage is needed; January only initializes deterministic indicators. Purge/embargo twelve hours at both fold edges; prohibit exits beyond fold ends. Windows, source/version/costs, rules and all eight candidates must be independently registered and pushed before later selected price access. Source audit must disclose any prior BTC/ETH/SOL study exposure. Missing support never triggers a fourth month or extra variant without retiring the campaign and obtaining a new dispatch.

The future screening estimands are mean higher-cost net trade bps and incremental paired higher-cost bps; secondary R and minute-MTM account statistics cannot replace them. Nulls: each <=0. Eligibility requires complete admitted inputs/trade paths, >=100 unique trades in each of three partitions, >=12 joint weekly blocks, <=60% single-asset exposure, stress mean >=5bp, stress median >=-5bp, >=2 nonnegative partition stress means, minute-MTM drawdown <=10% of initial equity and mean worst5% trade loss no worse than-300bp. These are developmental heuristics, not release criteria. Simultaneous one-sided familywise95% bounds across8×2 estimands use common UTC-week clusters across assets and10,000 fixed-seed resamples, Bonferroni lower quantile0.003125; disclose two-week-block sensitivity and small-block instability. Both adjusted lower bounds must exceed0 and sensitivity must retain positive bounds, otherwise DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY for a potential positive. No resampling seed/search or test-model change after outcomes.

Eligible ranking is descending min(adjusted net-bps lower bound, adjusted incremental-bps lower bound); tie by lower MTM drawdown, then lexicographic ID. Shortlist <=2; may be zero. Report all configurations before ranking, including source-rejected and unsupported variants, all LONG/SHORT/WAIT counts, fill rates, cost split, gross/net bps/R, MFE/MAE, stop ordering, holds, tails, asset/fold/regime descriptive stability, latency+1m and half-participation counterfactuals. Neither counterfactual is a new selectable strategy; do not recompute candidate signals. No subgroup rescue.

When support and economics are demonstrable and every variant has nonpositive plausible stress mean, return DEVELOPMENT_NO_GO and retire both families. Also retire any adequately supported family whose every variant fails the frozen economic/tail screen; do not retune a losing family. A positive point estimate with unresolved uncertainty or cost proxies is DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY, not proof of edge. Missing source/causality, lookahead, hidden outcomes, checksum failure, method drift or protected access halts fail-closed. Early stops concern source/integrity and the frozen10% account drawdown limit only; no favorable efficacy peeking, result-dependent extensions or replacement candidates. See execution gate plan for independent boundaries and resource ceilings.
