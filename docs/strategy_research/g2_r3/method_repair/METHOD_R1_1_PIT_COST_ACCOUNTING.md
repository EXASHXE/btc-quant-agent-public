## Data and two ledgers

All event times are UTC integer milliseconds. A minute is[O,O+60000), exclusive endT=O+60000, publisher inclusive closeT-1ms. Preserve original clocks; never infer availability from present retrieval. Completed1h/4h bars are epoch-aligned unions of complete1m inputs. Frozen historical trade and mark availability isT+60000; this is ARCHIVAL_EVENT_TIME_RECONSTRUCTED, not original live receipt proof. Hourly signal at hour-end+60000; earliest signal entry at hour-end+120000. Prior formula lookbacks use only inputs with available_at<=decision_at; no feature from the entry minute.

Two ledgers share identical economic fills but serve different purposes. Economic ledger E_e records ex-post positions, fees/funding, realized PnL and minute-close mark equity at event time. Decision ledger E_d consumes only messages with available_at<=its clock; reports last available mark and acknowledged cash/positions, plus outstanding-order/funding reserves. E_e can show earlier insolvency or1x exposure breach, but cannot alter preceding decisions or erase fills. A virtual execution acknowledgment is explicitly modeled available_at=execution_event+60000; this assumption is not an exchange receipt. Intraminute barrier exits use minute-end-1ms as conservative reporting event/ack origin because finer time is unknown. Standing stops are economic protections, not a new decision using an unpublished candle. A pending symbol remains locked until its exit acknowledgment. Missing active trade/mark minutes halt empirical accounting as SOURCE_BLOCKED; no filler or invented exit price.

Per candidate and separately funded unconditional control, instantiate isolated1,000-USDT books. Base and higher-cost cases have separate causal books under identical formulas/calendar; supervisors may produce different quantities, kills and eligible-event sets. Record divergence, never present unpaired means as pure fee-only causal effects. They are two fixed cost cases, not16 new candidate trials; selection uses higher-cost case only. No pooled capital competition between policies. Matched LONG/SHORT/balanced episodes share each parent's cost-case entry identity/notional/risk/horizon, are unfunded and may overlap; never add them as portfolio ROI.

## State and total ordering

Book state: acknowledged collateral cash, positions(q signed base units,effective entry,stop,target,entry_at), pending acknowledgments, pending entry/exit/reduction orders, immutable source message IDs, outstanding commitment reserve, last available marks with source event/available times, pending funding ownership records/reserves, decision high-water equity, kill latch, insolvency flag and per-symbol cooldown/retest-event identity. States are FLAT,ENTRY_PENDING,OPEN,EXIT_PENDING,COOLDOWN or permanent DISABLED; acknowledgments release locks, never future OHLCV. Record duplicate IDs as errors; order source messages by(available_at,event_at,type_priority,symbol,source_id), where FILL_ACK=0,FUNDING_ACK=1,TRADE_BAR=2,MARK_BAR=3. Within FILL_ACK, exit/reduction before entry, then parent order ID; simultaneous acknowledgment means cash/quantity are committed as one batch before mark valuation. Distinct economic books never consume one another's messages. This fixes equality cases independent of input permutation.

For every minute openO, execute the following stages in order; equality atO is included only in stage1. Anything generated in stages1–3 has due_at>=O+60000. Preexisting due orders must have created_at<=O-60000. No retroactive executions.

## Equity reserve, orders and rounding

Units: q is base-asset quantity; notional N=abs(q)×price inUSDT (product multiplier must be verified); collateral/margin/equity areUSDT, not notionals. No leverage multiplication. At a decision, E_d is acknowledged cash plus acknowledged quantities valued at latest available marks; awaiting fill/cash deltas are represented by commitments, never applied early. Let G be acknowledged gross position notional, R_f adverse funding reserves for open/pending quantities, and C_o pending entry notional plus entry/exit-cost commitments (excluding R_f). Capital A=max(0,0.95×E_d-R_f-C_o); prospective asset budget B=max(0,min(333.333333333333,A/3,A-G)). Compute all simultaneous symbol B values from the same pre-entry snapshot, then commit sequentially in fixed symbol order, recomputing residual A-G after each commitment. No reuse of unacknowledged exit capital.

For a fully specified diagnostic scenario, reserve buffer is exactly1.10 (10% above last available price/mark), not fitted and not a guaranteed bound. Let P be last available signal close, ATR the unchanged signal ATR, M latest available mark, tau admitted tick, and lot admitted market step. Let X=ceil_to_tick((P+0.25×ATR)×(1+(spread_bp+slip_bp)/10000)), an adverse entry-notional bound used for both directions. Let exit reserve per quantity C_exit=1.10×X×(fee_bp+spread_bp+slip_bp)/10000+tau and entry fee reserve C_entry=X×fee_bp/10000. Let N_event count frozen scenario settlement windows intersecting[planned_entry,planned_entry+maxhold], inclusive. F_unit=1.10×M×scenario_adverse_rate×N_event. Compute q=floor_to_lot(B/(X+C_entry+C_exit+F_unit)); if denominator,price,lot,tick,mark or cap inputs are unavailable/nonpositive, reject with explicit SOURCE/COST_UNVERIFIED. Provisional admission notional q×X must satisfy residual capital; post-rounding risk/gap/minimum constraints can veto, never retune. These are reserves, not fees debited twice.

Unknown-schedule diagnostic grid is every UTC whole hour; ownership windows[S-15000,S+15000], rates4bp base/8bp higher. Verified-schedule scenarios substitute only separately admitted constant event identities/versions frozen before prices. At entry commitment, add q×(X+C_entry+C_exit) to C_o and q×F_unit to R_f. At acknowledged fill, release pending notional/entry fee reserve, convert quantity into G and retain exit-cost reserve for its remaining quantity; cash already includes the acknowledged actual fee/price basis once. At partial/full exit acknowledgment release proportionate exit/future funding reserves and quantity; past unresolved funding ownership retains its reserve until FUNDING_ACK. Each reserve has an immutable order/settlement ID and release-once flag. At each available mark update recalculate remaining-funding reserve from latest M×1.10 and pending scheduled events, subtract already resolved events, then risk-check; this can only schedule future action. Unexpected actual cost/gap above reserved scenario is recorded, never retroactive entry veto. Unverified real event count/cap is PROXY_ONLY, never insurance.

The5% reserve is an admission buffer, not a second fee debit. Calculate equity from collateral+realized+unrealized-fees-funding. Reserved capital affects spendability, not economic PnL. Decision1x supervisor requires committed gross notional<=A; if not, first cancel pending entries, then proportional known-position target notional=A, rounded down by lot. Execute reductions no earlier than next feasible open; fees/costs can require another delayed reduction. Transient lag/gap-induced true economic leverage>1 is explicitly flagged, not retrospectively erased or amplified; no supported positive claim from a violated capital path. Prevent intended>1x entries using available capital, retain adverse gaps. If liquidation open is unavailable, keep EXIT_PENDING and SOURCE_BLOCKED, never find a favorable substitute price.

Sizing and quote-execution checks use the preexisting original risk30–250bp and gap<=0.25ATR gates; no wider stop to pass fees. Round buy executions up and sell down to tick. Long stop rounds upward toward entry, short stop downward; targets toward entry (LONG down,SHORT up). Reject zero/negative rounded risk or invalid filters. Fixed stops are not tuned; target stays2×initial modeled risk as in design. Effective historical filter version must be admitted or explicitly named as proxy. Do not use pricePrecision as tickSize.

## Cost and return reconciliation

Effective execution embeds adverse spread/slippage/tick loss once. Fees separately debit executed notional. Do not debit embedded friction again in cash. Gross diagnostic uses raw quote entry/exit; execution drag is the difference between raw and effective quote PnL, fees/funding separately attributed. Economic PnL=quantity×direction×(effective exit-effective entry)-fees-funding. Unrealized MTM uses effective entry, not raw price plus another friction debit. Net bps=10000×netPnL/entry notional; R=netPnL/initial stop-risk dollars; equity return=delta account equity/initial1,000. Trade bps/R are not margin returns or portfolio sums. Gap/fees can exceed1R. PAUSE has zero exposure/cost, undefinedR and no assumed USDT yield.

Funding measured sign is cashflow=-d×abs(q)×settlement mark×rate; LONG pays positive and SHORT receives positive. Diagnostic proxy cashflow is always an adverse debit regardless direction. Cap/ownership mark reserve uses conservative known/admitted bound; where only future minute high is known, it can determine ex-post scenario notional but must not enter an earlier reserve or quantity decision. Reserve uses the latest available mark times the explicit1.10 diagnostic scenario buffer; if insufficient after an unforeseen gap, record the shortfall, do not backdate a veto. Same-minute funding/exit and SL/TP cases are therefore reproducible, not claims of real account payments.

Independently testable acceptance cases for a later authorized implementation: delayed mark cannot stop/reject an earlier entry; same-bar SL/TP is SL; entry/exit/reduction around settlement charge once; no funding benefit in selection; stale mark veto and delayed exit; kill at>=100USDT decision drawdown,never economic lookahead;5% buffer and commitments release once; tick/lot/minimum veto; zero/negative equity preserves gap loss; independent cost/policy books; unfunded overlapping matched episodes. These are contract scenarios, not test results or market outcomes.
