## Information milestones and stopping

Proposed future14/30/60-day reviews inspect receipt coverage, clock/gap counts, signal frequency, no-fill and dependence estimates using protocol-permitted metadata only; they are not repeated efficacy tests. Day14: require>=99.9% receipt coverage,100% active feature paths, zero unclassified sources. Day30: calculate signal rate and joint weekly cluster count; quantify time needed for the approved minimum support. Day60: decide whether a separately fixed collection cap can meet information target; without enough support report diagnostic. No automatic extension, candidate modification, efficacy peeking or collection is authorized here. Separate missing-minute behavior must be frozen;99.9% collection coverage does not permit filler or incomplete trades.

For planning let lambda be unique admissible trades/day, sigma_b estimated dispersion of weekly block mean net bps, and delta=5bp minimum economically meaningful effect. A rough independent-block planning count is B >= ((z_(1-0.05/16)+z_0.80)*sigma_b/delta)^2; compare to at least12 blocks and100 trades/fold. Also estimate paired-incremental dispersion and use the larger requirement. Account for unequal blocks, autocorrelation/design effect and cross-asset dependence; use conservative bounds, not aggregate997 R2 alternatives as independent n. Selective entries may make support unattainable cheaply; stop rather than validate known losers for a year. Neither14 days nor360 days promises power. Power planning assumptions and maximum collection cap must be frozen before confirmatory outcomes, not recomputed to extend a favorable trajectory.

Development evaluates exactly one dataset once scientifically, with deterministic reruns permitted only as hash checks. Source/integrity hard stops and account10% MTM drawdown stop are allowed; include stopped paths/losses. No positive early-stop promotion. If all supported variants fail plausible stress economics, retire family and return DEVELOPMENT_NO_GO; if data support/cost confidence is insufficient return DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY. Do not revise a live policy or cheaper-cost rescore mid-campaign. A new hypothesis after failure requires new authority, new exposure disclosure and another freeze, never pretending this cohort becomes fresh.
