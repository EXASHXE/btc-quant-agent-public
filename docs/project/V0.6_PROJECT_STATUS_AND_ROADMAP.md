## Why adding data may or may not solve the sample problem

Two distinct limitations must be measured, not conflated:

1. **Coverage-limited OI:** Only 3,667 OI-paired eligible origins and four signals were available in the frozen 2026 OI window. Investigate whether **native** OI/top-position data and exact 4h historical predecessors exist for additional public years. Do not use spot volume or another venue's OI as a Binance-native replacement. Binance OI history through a bounded REST endpoint is not equivalent to a complete multi-year native archive.
2. **Condition-limited Funding:** Funding/Premium history already yields many matched origins in several years. The fixed conjunction almost never triggered outside 2021, especially in 2022. Extra candles alone will not fix year/regime concentration. Compare true non-triggers with source-invalid periods; count non-overlapping episodes and effective weekly blocks before requesting real outcome testing.
