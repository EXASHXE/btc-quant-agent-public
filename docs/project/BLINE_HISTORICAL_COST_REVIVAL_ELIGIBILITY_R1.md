## Evidence gaps, admissibility prerequisites and hard stop

1. **Source/PIT [D]:** Historical SHA/checksum, minute-bar completeness and retrospective provider funding do not prove first receiver known-at, contemporaneous executable quotes, venue true publication, signed settlement membership, contract version/units, or legally permitted OI/depth. F2 in particular must not substitute later-known settled Funding for an earlier trading decision. H32 source gaps and cross-venue alignment remain explicit. No new source may be admitted by this report.
2. **Realistic cost [B]:** The 22/44bp proxies are immutable results, not measured NORMAL/ADVERSE distributions. Future *separately authorized* cost measurement would need versioned applicable maker/taker tier and promotions, per-fill role/notional, size-matched bid/ask/depth, decision→arrival→ACK clocks, partial fill/nonfill and passive queue censoring, stop-gap exits, signed funding paid/received and actual quote/Last/Mark basis; report joint p50/p75/p95/tails with dependent sample support and prevent double-counted spread, slippage or funding. The cost methodology's 2/5bp **public fee references** are neither a replacement for BASE22/STRESS44 nor proof that historic signals turn positive.
3. **Statistics and independence [A/C]:** Freeze the same economic event and matched non-event source-neutral population, signed entry-only horizon value separate from TP/stop-dependent gross, regime coverage and unique calendar clusters; carry forward Goal-A/B's **48 exposed trials**, W1/W2 exit's **8 additional contrasts** (joint 56-family not reconstructed), R3's **27** frozen cells, F's invalid sparse studentization and v0.3.12 H32/H33/H34 exposure. A sampled mean >0 or PF >1 is not an Alpha gate.
4. **Accounting/execution [D]:** R4 accepted R3's tested Decimal cash conservation but did **not** attest historical fill feasibility. Explicit R3 cap/standing partial-target ordering can be optimistic and cannot be numerically cost-corrected from published aggregates; 54 account rows reported zero stop/target collisions, so that particular collision is **not** an observed explanation for R3 losses. W1/W2 real-Funding overlays and R3 unfunded proxy account paths are **NOT_COMPARABLE** economic interventions. Never subtract their published PnLs or select whichever calendar/horizon looks best.
5. **Absolute STOP:** No historical re-backtest, alternative fee plug-in on exposed trade rows, exit retune, candidate proposal, raw trade/return/holdout read, Source Admission, TESTNET/LIVE, or release. A distinct independently reviewable mechanism/state plus eligible source, measured execution, statistical power and precommitted independent sample must all be evidenced **before** any future Controller can consider a new experimental dispatch.
