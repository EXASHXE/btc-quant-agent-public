## Staged authority

1. **Stage A: PRE-REGISTER and PUSH** candidate count ≤12, symbols, data/time boundaries, PIT availability rule, fill/cost model, action labels, hold horizon grid, historical overlap/exposure, baseline comparisons and hard stop triggers **before seeing newly selected outcomes**. Commit and verify remote SHA before empirical evaluation.
2. **Stage B: BUILD+EVALUATE** only frozen candidates using verified allowed read-only public/nonprotected sources; offline replay is deterministic, causal and fee/funding/slippage-aware; first valid trade at next tradable bar, ambiguous stop/TP conservative; no synthetic empirical profit or missing-data silent repair. Minimum 3 chronological partitions where data permits; lack of support => DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY.
3. **Stage C: REVIEW** all candidates, costs, uncertainty under dependence, bootstrap or appropriate confidence intervals, explicit multiple-comparison bias and regime/sample fragility. At most 2 exploratory survivors under pre-set ranking; NO_GO if nothing survives.
4. **Stage D: CONTROLLER L2** checks immutable pre-reg earlier than outcome access, source/partition/exposure integrity, code/test/result SHA and pushed receipts. L2 may accept method/insight or require bounded repair, **not** grant independent strategy PASS.
5. **Prospective R2** needs fresh, independently authorized, verifiably unexposed cohort (possibly new post-freeze forward time as it actually accrues). No claims about future samples collected now.

## Evidence quality and economics

- Compare same friction inputs across benchmark WAIT/no-trade, simple trend, historical Tactical baseline and preregistered families; account trade selection, repeated exposure and market regime shifts.
- Directional: gross/net R, mean/median, trade count, LONG/SHORT/WAIT, fill rate, MFE/MAE, stop-before-target, drawdown, time-held, fees/slippage/funding, partition variability and risk bounds; avoid using leverage to inflate apparent edge.
- Grid: matched market-to-market net PnL, realized paired profit plus underwater inventory, boundary breach, grid capital use and recovery under realistic maker fill and adverse movement. Do not compare paired profit to directional R without common risk/unit normalization.
- Adjust interpretation for up to 12 tried configurations, dependent observations/overlapping horizons, common asset shocks and thin samples. Never choose time horizon, symbol subgroup or fee model after outcomes to rescue a losing candidate.
- Prior RC1 operational heuristic (100 OOS actions; mean net >=+0.05R; median >=-0.05R; 2/3 partitions nonnegative; stop-before-target <=0.65) is **historical context only**; meeting it on previously exposed development data does not constitute a fresh strategy-quality PASS. This contract intentionally does not pre-authorize new release thresholds or positive alpha inference.
