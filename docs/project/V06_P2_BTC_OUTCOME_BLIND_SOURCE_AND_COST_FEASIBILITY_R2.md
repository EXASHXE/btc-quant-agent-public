## 3. P1A four execution cost regimes and cash invariants

| Regime | What may be asserted | Evidence prerequisite | Current verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FEE_ONLY_REFERENCE** | Abstract ordinary fee rates 2bp maker, 5bp taker per leg; equal-price notional M/M 4bp, M/T 7bp, T/T 10bp *fee-only*, neglecting spread/other costs | Public applicable USDⓈ-M reference, effective-date/tier assumptions and per-fill synthetic role | DESIGN ONLY; **never** an executable cost |

Old `BASE22=(6+2+3)*2` bp and `STRESS44=(12+4+6)*2` bp are frozen synthetic proxy decompositions. They are **not** actual ordinary USDT-M fees or normal/adverse measurements and may not be retroactively repriced, relabeled, replaced or used to claim positive expectancy. Ordinary current fee reference is dynamic and account-specific effective fees are UNKNOWN; the fee-table rendered evidence, venue/product, time, account tier and discounts must be version-checked under a new mandate.

**Per-fill accounting:** For side `s` (+1 long/-1 short) and matched flat quantity, `TradeCash=s*(sum(q_exit*P_exit)-sum(q_entry*P_entry))`; `FeeCash=-sum(|q_fill*P_fill| * verified_rate_fill)`; `NetCash=TradeCash+FeeCash+sum(F_T)-other_nonoverlapping_cash_costs`. Define `N_ref` before analysis and report `10000*NetCash/N_ref` bp; report collateral/margin return separately. Use Decimal and venue rounding with per-fill IDs and signed partial quantity reconciliation. Positions remaining after unfilled exit cannot be represented as completed trades.

**Avoid double charging:** An arrival-mid `m` versus taker execution ask/bid difference includes the half-spread; if `P_fill` is already executable bid/ask or realized VWAP, don't deduct that spread again. Decompose `decision_mid -> arrival_mid -> executable_quote -> actual_fill` only with non-overlapping timestamps and price witnesses. Maker benefit is **conditional on fill** and cannot be represented as an always-executed 2bp leg. Estimate maker markout/toxicity only with observed fill decisions; public book-touch is not own queue acceptance. Stop Mark/Last trigger is not stop fill; stop-limit may remain unfilled; ACK-dependent protection cannot act before ACK.

**Prospective measurement definitions, not values:** grouped by predeclared UTC block, vol/trend known before decisions, long/short, intended BTC size, order role, venue filters. Report full distribution of *intents* (including maker nonfills and censored cancels), filled fractions, bid/ask spread, quote age, visible-size VWAP, order time-to-fill, signed adverse selection and settled Funding, stratified p50/p75/p95 plus uncertainty. Do **not** sum independently estimated marginal p95 shocks and call the result an empirical total p95; joint tails require paired observations. No count, fee-effective distribution or market bp is measured here.

## 5. Outcome-blind frequency and independent support design

**One portfolio opportunity is not one alert and not one order.** Freeze an economic episode event key `(instrument, side, first_qualified_decision_at, causal_structure_id, source_identity_hash)`, then five stage counts: raw setup -> deterministic deduped episode -> closed-bar/source-PIT confirmed -> quote/risk/order-eligible hypothetical candidate -> one-position portfolio-admitted *candidate*. Repeated 5m/15m alerts and same source impulse produce **one** episode. Entry blocking by existing hypothetical occupancy, contradictory sides, quote freshness, cooldown and one common portfolio capital budget must be deterministic, documented and outcome-blind. No overlapping new BTC positions in this design, even if signals arrive concurrently. Exposure starts only at predefined hypothetical acceptance; stop/TP/4–24h cap assumptions are frozen **for occupancy only**, with no future-price-triggered early exit computed in this permit. For unbiased preliminary frequency, use a predeclared conservative 24h occupation cap (or a complete outcome-independent planned occupancy rule), label actual early-exit variability UNKNOWN; avoid looking at future prices to decide eligibility.

**Denominator:** every UTC calendar hour in which all required sources passed predefined admission, plus separate outage/censored hours; *also* publish total scheduled hours so favorable missingness cannot inflate rate. Raw `rate=N_portfolio_candidates/(eligible_hours/24)` and a second total-wall-clock rate; dedupe across clock partitions, avoid cherry-picked scoring months or removing quiet regimes. Count side, mechanism and suppression causes; publish 30/60/90d rolling rates, weekly/monthly counts, inter-event gap percentiles and fractions >48h/>72h/>7d, occupancy conflicts, 4/8/12/18/24h intended cap mix and source missingness.

**Design frequency:** 0.5 eligible portfolio event/day implies 15 in a 30-day all-covered month, ~45 in 90d, ~90 in 180d, ~182.5 in a 365d year, **not expected observations or trading instructions**. Under a highly idealized independent Poisson process, an invented 90-day N=45 yields rate 0.5/day and approximate standard error sqrt(45)/90=0.0745/day (a heuristic 95% band ~0.35–0.65/day); actual events cluster, independent-block variance may be larger. A 24h source-permit window has expectation 0.5 event at the design target and **cannot measure the target frequency**. Preregister a longer, separately authorized sampling season, at least rolling 90d for preliminary opportunity-rate precision and preferably annual multi-regime coverage for stability; no extension is authorized by this document. Freeze Controller-defined acceptance tolerance and minimum active-block count before counting; do NOT choose thresholds or which mechanism to retain using observed counts.

**Power/selection discipline:** event count != independent samples. Define independent calendar-block unit in advance and prevent double-counted overlapping 4–24h event horizons; calendar-week/month active support and overdispersion/cluster confidence methods require independent statistician review. Missing-zero blocks and support censoring must be explicit, rather than sending degenerate studentized resamples through epsilon `SE`. Freeze all mechanisms × horizons × ablations × size buckets as one exposed selection family; test entry incremental information in a *different later Controller authorization*, with precommitted minimum effect, stress-after-cost net endpoint, sufficient effective calendar blocks, trial-wise family adjustment and no OOS leakage. There is **zero present outcome-based power estimate**, because that would require return variance labels prohibited here. Supporting a 0.5/day *rate* never supports a profitable *edge*.
