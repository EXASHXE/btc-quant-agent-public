## Durability and error policy

Single Linux writer lock; canonical JSONL WAL record seq, previoushash, eventhash, afterstatehash and recordhash. Pure validation precedes append. WAL writes are unbuffered, complete write-loop, then fsync; immutable hash-named checkpoint files use exclusive link and directory fsync. Recovery replays WAL from bound genesis/fixture and checks every immutable checkpoint against its actual prefix. Complete pre-fsync records surviving process death may be recovered; no power-loss durability claim. Torn/malformed/duplicated-key/hash-corrupt WAL and checkpoints ahead of WAL stop and quarantine; no automatic repair or tail truncation.

Crash hooks beforeappend / afterappend-beforefsync / afterfsync / aftersnapshot and physical outbox delivery are test-only local process exits. Outbox JSONL physical delivery is at-least-once; dedupe key guarantees logical identity, not physical exactly-once. Synthetic trust tokens are not production human identity authentication. Local hashes detect corruption, not a malicious writer able to rewrite all history; disk-loss and distributed/concurrent brokers are outside this preview.

Recoverable retransmission: identical durable eventID/body is an idempotent no-op. Fatal/conflicting: reusedID/bodydrift, future clocks, stale approval, integrity or unsafe portfolio. Denied events leave original state and WAL unchanged. Errors use static codes; credential-like model text is redacted before WAL/log/outbox.
