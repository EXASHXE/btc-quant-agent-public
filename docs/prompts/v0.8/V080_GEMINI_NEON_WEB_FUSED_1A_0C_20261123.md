QUANT is a personal, noncommercial, AI-enhanced trading intelligence platform.

It is not an autonomous trading robot, an institutional compliance platform, or merely a Feishu notification bot.

The initial product targets Binance USD-M perpetual markets:

- BTCUSDT
- ETHUSDT
- SOLUSDT

Primary holding period: **2–12 hours**, including possible overnight holds.

Timeframe hierarchy:

`1D background → 4H regime → 1H primary setup → 15m confirmed trigger → 5m/quote actionability`

Market analysis must respect:

- Confirmed closed candles.
- Independent trigger confirmation.
- No lookahead.
- Point-in-time market data.
- Data freshness and causal ordering.
- Open interest and market structure.
- Order-book depth, spread, and slippage.
- Funding costs and trading fees.
- ATR-based stop placement.
- Important 4H/1D obstacles.
- TP1/TP2/TP3 and after-cost net R.
- Strict separation of WATCH, EXECUTABLE, INVALID, NO_SETUP, and NOT_EVALUATED.

**QUANT Core is the only authority for numerical market decisions.**

The AI Agent may explain, challenge, research, or annotate signals.

It must NEVER independently promote WATCH into EXECUTABLE or silently modify accepted Core numerical decisions.
