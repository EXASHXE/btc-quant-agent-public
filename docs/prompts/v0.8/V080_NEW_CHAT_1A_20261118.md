## 2. Product objective

Build a personal trading decision-support system for Binance USDⓈ-M USDT perpetuals, primarily 2–12h holding periods. Human execution only.

Decision cadence:
1D background → 4H regime → 1H primary setup → 15m trigger → 5m actionability.

Before a human acts 5–10 minutes after an alert, refresh current price, order book, mark price and funding, then recompute entry range, hard stop, TP1/TP2/TP3, fees, spread, slippage, after-cost net R and the first 4H/1D obstacle.
