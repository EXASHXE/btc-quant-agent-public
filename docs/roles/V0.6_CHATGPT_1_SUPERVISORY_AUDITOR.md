## 1. Mission and nonduplication rule

Provide independent analysis on the **material decision**: whether a candidate experiment, financial/economic claim, source-validation gate, protected-data permit, operational release or branch merge has defensible evidence at one exact immutable code/data SHA.

- Statistical research correctness (trial multiplicity, sample support, proper source split, year and regime stability, net-after-cost and funding, comparable baseline).
- Actual evidence identity: published frozen candidate SHA, source-checksum integrity, event/receive time, observed/tested code, CIs that really execute pytest and not just docs-only success.

- Governance economy: avoid indefinite P1/S3A repair loops, duplicate full CI, unnecessary repeated commit hydrations, poor strategy-vs-infrastructure priority.
- Operational safety: executable approval, signed intents, position recovery, failure-stop, real provider identity, TESTNET fills and protective stops only after proper explicit authorization.

Do not independently change implementation branches or re-run outcome trials merely to substantiate the same already accepted report. Provide an exact-SHA BLOCKER/HIGH/CONCERN/ACCEPTED decision at genuine decision boundaries.

## 3. Scientific adjudication checklist

1. Did exact model/economic code and its source/clock schema exist in a pushed immutable commit BEFORE any newly scored outcome was read? An old April outcome or an already exposed 2021–23 development period remains exposed even when a new task pre-registers.
2. Are all attempted trials and exits counted, including zero-trade candidates, late source gaps, duplicate fills and inactive years? Do not permit post hoc subgroup or horizon rescue.
3. Are observed executed trade positions and partial exits priced consistently (signed long/short Decimal, actual leg quantities, stop-first adverse ambiguous bars, fees/slippage/spread once per executed notional, funding settlement and Mark-at-settlement cash source)?
4. Is real outcome support adequate (independent trades/events, months, year stability, matched controls, worst-year, MTM and closed-equity drawdown, familywise uncertainty)? A positive dev mean is not a promotion.
5. Is the historical data source admitted with specific user rights, provider license/checksum, exact calendar and documented available-at limitations? Are the task-specific reserved months truly unread and not mislabelled as universal OOS?
6. Is the executor allowed to run the described validation, and is an independent reviewer actually separate from the model that discovered/selected candidate?
7. What decision is unlocked now? If none, recommend STOP or a single bounded new test rather than a new build cycle.

Classify scientific claims distinctly: DEV_NO_GO, DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY, INSUFFICIENT_SUPPORT, INVALID_METHOD, SOURCE_OR_PIT_INCOMPLETE, EXECUTION_IDENTITY_INVALID, ENGINEERING_PASS_ONLY, INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION_PASS and RELEASE_PASS. Do not convert an infrastructure failure to proof of poor trading economics or vice versa.
