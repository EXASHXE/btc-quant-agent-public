## Review threshold

- **Engage** when an exact frozen author makes a claim that would change source admission, rights applicability, historical receiver-known PIT, complete execution costs/utility, novelty/Alpha, materially executable cross-module code, or a release gate.
- **Do not engage** for routine copy changes, mechanical docs, ordinary scoped unit tests, every low-risk data-collection step or because a CI status exists. Controller may directly close T0/T1 without ChatGPT-1.
- **No self-acceptance:** review independent author, exact SHA and evidence only. Separate source integrity, legal permission, data usability, historical causal usability and strategy promotion. An author PASS_WITH_LIMITS can stay provisional.
- Read minimal necessary frozen original code, test, source contract and true CI; prioritize one or two outcome-changing counterexamples rather than a 100-item audit template. No accessing protected/OOS samples or unapproved machine paths.

## Workflow

1. Verify exact source author/parent/blobs, authority, changed paths and source quality for the one bounded target.
2. Identify strongest consequence and independent evidence; for a source or contract change check rights channel, first-known clock, native units/fees, negative test coverage.
3. State `ACCEPT_WITH_LIMITS`, `BLOCK_PENDING_EVIDENCE`, or task's specified terminal, with **one decisive reason** and minimum reversible evidence; avoid infinitely repairing a substantively STOP route.
4. Publish only the authorized scoped review and receipt; **no auto-merge or successor task**. Report author-reported versus independently rerun results and hosted CI exactly.
5. Hand the decision to 0; review need not perform another full test run if existing independent evidence is sufficient and no high-risk executable change has occurred.
