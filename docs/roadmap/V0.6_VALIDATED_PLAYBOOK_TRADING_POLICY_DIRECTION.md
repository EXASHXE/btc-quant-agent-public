This document records the intended strategic direction after the current H40 lifecycle/Discovery program reaches an accepted research terminal state. It is an input to the future Astra pre-Discovery/full-architecture audit and any later v0.6 redesign. It does not supersede current H40 protocol authority.

---

## 1. Strategic conclusion

The project should not choose between:

1. endlessly searching for one universal alpha formula; or
2. immediately building an LLM-driven “expert trader” that subjectively reads the market.

The preferred direction is:

```text
Validated Alpha / Edge Research
        ↓
Condition-specific validated playbooks
        ↓
Validated Playbook Library
        ↓
Deterministic Trading Policy / Router
        ↓
Risk + Execution
        ↓
Later: Agent/LLM orchestration and explanation
```

The central principle is:

> **Validated edge produces expectancy. The Policy layer decides when and where each validated edge is allowed to operate.**

A Policy Agent cannot create positive expectancy from unvalidated heuristics. Conversely, a single global alpha formula is unlikely to be robust across all market regimes.

## 2. Intended project evolution

Current v0.5 research infrastructure remains valuable and should be preserved:

- PIT-safe data semantics;
- causal replay;
- source authority;
- deterministic feature/scientific computation;
- frozen/preregistered research contracts;
- bounded search;
- multiple-testing controls;
- candidate lock;
- walk-forward validation;
- holdout/confirmation boundaries;
- evidence receipts;
- replay/auditability;
- execution default-deny.

These become the **validation substrate** for future playbooks.

The likely v0.6 architectural objective is:

```text
Market State Engine
        ↓
Playbook Eligibility
        ↓
Playbook Evaluation
        ↓
Policy Selector
        ↓
LONG / SHORT / NO_TRADE
        ↓
Risk Engine
        ↓
Execution Engine
```

## 3. Market State Engine

The system should explicitly represent market context rather than assume one strategy works everywhere.

Candidate state families include, subject to future preregistration and validation:

```text
TREND_UP
TREND_DOWN
RANGE
COMPRESSION
VOLATILITY_EXPANSION
BREAKOUT
FAILED_BREAKOUT
HIGH_VOLATILITY
LOW_LIQUIDITY
CROWDED_LONG
CROWDED_SHORT
CROSS_ASSET_CONFIRMATION
CROSS_ASSET_DIVERGENCE
```

State classification must remain deterministic and PIT-safe before any LLM interpretation is allowed to influence trading authority.

---

## 4. Validated Playbook Library

Future research should validate playbook families conditionally rather than search indefinitely for a universal direction formula.

Potential examples:

```text
TREND_CONTINUATION
PULLBACK_CONTINUATION
BREAKOUT_CONTINUATION
FAILED_BREAKOUT_REVERSAL
RANGE_MEAN_REVERSION
VOLATILITY_EXPANSION
CROWDING_REVERSAL
CROWDING_CONTINUATION
NO_TRADE
```

Each playbook should independently pass the same research discipline:

```text
preregister
→ deterministic implementation
→ bounded search
→ multiple-testing control
→ candidate lock
→ walk-forward
→ confirmation / holdout where authorized
→ future-only shadow
```

A failed playbook family should close rather than be repeatedly tuned until profitable.

---

## 5. Role of H40

H40 should be treated as a current directional-playbook research campaign, not as the entire final trading system.

Its purpose is to answer whether the currently frozen selective medium-horizon directional information set contains a sufficiently robust edge.

Valid H40 terminal outcomes include:

```text
H40_NO_GO
```

or a candidate that survives the full authorized validation chain.

Either result is useful:

- PASS may seed the first validated directional playbook;
- NO_GO should close this family and inform the next playbook/regime design.

H40 should not be post-hoc expanded until it becomes profitable.

---

## 6. Policy Engine

The future policy layer should initially be deterministic and auditable.

Example:

```text
if regime == TREND_UP:
    evaluate eligible trend/pullback playbooks

elif regime == RANGE:
    evaluate eligible mean-reversion playbooks

elif regime == COMPRESSION:
    evaluate eligible breakout playbooks

else:
    NO_TRADE
```

The policy layer should select only among **already validated** playbooks and should retain NO_TRADE as the default when:

- no playbook is eligible;
- evidence conflicts;
- expected excursion does not cover cost/risk;
- confidence is insufficient;
- source authority is incomplete;
- risk or execution constraints fail.

---

## 7. Role of LLM / Agent

The LLM should not become numerical truth authority by default.

Preferred near-term responsibility:

- research orchestration;
- architecture analysis;
- evidence synthesis;
- market-state explanation;
- playbook explanation;
- conflict explanation;
- operational diagnostics;
- report generation.

Initial trading authority should remain deterministic.

Only after a separate experiment proves incremental out-of-sample value should an LLM/Agent be allowed to influence playbook selection or decision authority.

---

## 8. Profitability objective

The target is not guaranteed profitability or 100% directional accuracy.

The system seeks repeatable positive net expectancy:

```text
Net Expectancy
=
P(win) × AvgWin
- P(loss) × AvgLoss
- fees
- spread
- slippage
- funding
- execution effects
```

A commercially useful system may:

- trade infrequently;
- abstain most of the time;
- use different playbooks in different regimes;
- have moderate win rate;
- rely on asymmetric payoff and disciplined risk.

## 10. Astra audit questions

The future Astra audit should challenge at least:

1. whether the current research kernel is sufficient for multi-playbook research without multiplying research degrees of freedom;
2. what current H40/H39 infrastructure should be generalized versus retired;
3. whether Market State should be a feature layer, routing layer, or separately validated model;
4. how to isolate playbook-specific research from policy-selection research;
5. how to prevent regime classification from becoming hidden post-hoc tuning;
6. whether the Policy Engine should initially be deterministic, probabilistic, contextual-bandit-like, or another bounded design;
7. where an LLM can add value without becoming an unverifiable numerical authority;
8. how to validate portfolio interaction between multiple individually valid playbooks;
9. what execution/risk surfaces are missing before future shadow/paper operation;
10. what repository refactor is justified only after H40 reaches a research terminal state.

## 12. One-line direction

```text
Do not search forever for one universal alpha and do not trust an unvalidated “AI trader”.
Build a library of condition-specific validated edges, then route among them with an auditable Trading Policy Engine.
```
